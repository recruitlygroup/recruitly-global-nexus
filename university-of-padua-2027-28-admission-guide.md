# University of Padua 2027/28 Admission Guide: Programs, Deadlines & How to Apply

The **University of Padua (UniPD)** is one of the oldest and most respected universities in Europe, and it is a top choice for international students who want an **English-taught degree in Italy** at a fixed, predictable tuition fee. Applications for **academic year 2027/28** are already open, and **Call One closes on 15 November 2026**.

This guide covers the English-taught programs, every application call, entry requirements, and how to check your chances before you apply.

> **Quick answer:** Call One runs **15 September to 15 November 2026**. Call Two runs **1 December 2026 to 1 March 2027**. Some limited-access programs have a separate window for non-EU applicants abroad: **7 January to 7 March 2027**.

---

## Why Study at the University of Padua?

- **Heritage:** founded in **1222**, it is the second-oldest university in Italy. Galileo taught here, and Elena Lucrezia Cornaro Piscopia became the world's first woman to graduate from a university here in 1678.
- **Rankings:** **#204 in the QS World University Rankings 2027**, up 29 places and its best result to date. It also sits in the **151-200 band of the Shanghai (ARWU) ranking** and the **201-250 band of Times Higher Education**.
- **Scale:** more than **75,000 students** across multiple schools and departments.
- **Cost:** non-EU students living abroad pay a **fixed annual fee** (reported at about **EUR 2,790 to 2,990 in 2026/27**, plus a regional tax), far below many other European universities.
- **Location:** Padua is a historic student city close to Venice, with fast rail links to Milan, Bologna and Verona.

## 2027/28 Application Deadlines

International students apply through the **apply.unipd.it** portal. The university lists the following calls for academic year 2027/28:

| Call | Dates | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Call One** | **15 Sep to 15 Nov 2026** | Most programs, including many English-taught Master's |
| **Call Two** | **1 Dec 2026 to 1 Mar 2027** | Programs that remain open |
| **Limited-access programs (non-EU abroad)** | **7 Jan to 7 Mar 2027** | Non-EU applicants living outside Italy |
| **EU and equated, limited-access English programs** | 15 Mar to 7 Apr 2027 | EU and equated applicants only |
| **Call Three** | 1 Apr to 1 Jun 2027 | EU students and non-EU students residing in Italy only |
| **English certificate** | By **31 Jul 2027** | Can follow your application, per third-party guides |

Deadlines close at **23:59 Rome time**. Not every program uses every call, so **always check the deadline on your program's page**.

### Key points
- **Non-EU students apply in Call One or Call Two**, or the limited-access window where it applies. Call Three is not for you.
- You can apply to a program **only once per academic year**.
- The application fee is reported at **EUR 60** and covers up to two programs. Verify this on the official portal.
- Classes start in **late September or early October 2027**.

## Entry Requirements

- **English at B2:** for example **IELTS 6.0** or an equivalent TOEFL score. Requirements vary by program.
- **No Italian certificate:** not required for English-taught programs.
- **Qualification:** a secondary school diploma for Bachelor's, or a recognised Bachelor's degree for Master's. Third-party sources note at least 12 years of schooling for Bachelor's entry.
- **Documents at application:** CV in English, motivation letter in English, passport, and an English or Italian **translation of your transcript and diploma**. **No legalised documents** are needed at the application stage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the deadline for University of Padua 2027/28?
For most programs, **15 November 2026** (Call One). Call Two closes **1 March 2027**, and limited-access non-EU windows close **7 March 2027**.

### Do I need Italian to study at Padua?
No. **No Italian certificate** is required for English-taught programs.

### Can I submit my English certificate after applying?
Third-party guides say it can follow your application, up to **31 July 2027**, but check your program's rules.

### How much does it cost?
Non-EU students living abroad pay a fixed annual fee, reported at about **EUR 2,790 to 2,990** in 2026/27. The 2027/28 amount may differ.

### Can I apply to more than one program?
The portal lets you choose one or two programs, and you can apply to a given program only once per academic year.
