# Study in Austria: University of Klagenfurt English-Taught Programmes Guide

Looking for an affordable, high-quality degree in Europe taught entirely in English? The **University of Klagenfurt** (Universität Klagenfurt, also known as AAU) in southern Austria is a strong option. Founded in 1970, it is one of the world's top 50 young universities, and it offers **16 degree programmes taught in English** across technology, business, media and the humanities.

This guide covers the English-taught Bachelor's, Master's and PhD options, what they cost, when to apply, and how **Recruitly Group** can support your application.

---

## Why Study at the University of Klagenfurt?

- **Young, internationally recognised university**: ranked among the 50 best young universities in the world.
- **Strong student satisfaction**: the university has received two Global Student Satisfaction Awards from Studyportals, and 97% of students on StudyCheck.de recommend it.
- **Accredited business education**: the business school holds AACSB accreditation, a distinction earned by fewer than 6% of business schools worldwide.
- **Lakeside location**: the campus is in Klagenfurt am Wörthersee, in the Carinthia region, with estimated living costs of roughly EUR 720 to 980 per month.
- **Exchange opportunities**: students can spend a semester abroad at one of around 250 partner universities.

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programmes at Klagenfurt

If you are searching for an **English-taught Bachelor's degree in Austria**, Klagenfurt offers programmes such as:

- **Robotics and Artificial Intelligence**
- **International Business and Economics**
- **Digital Media, Culture, and Communication**
- **Worlds of English** (Bachelor of Arts, 180 ECTS, six semesters)

### Bachelor's admission basics

Applicants need a general university entrance qualification (a recognised school-leaving certificate) and proof of English proficiency. For Worlds of English, the requirement is English at **B2 level**. The general Bachelor's admission period for the winter semester 2026/27 ran from 6 July to 5 September 2026, and the summer semester 2027 window runs from 8 January to 5 February 2027.

> **Tip:** Requirements vary by programme, so always check the specific programme page before applying.

## English-Taught Master's Programmes at Klagenfurt

Klagenfurt offers **11 Master's programmes taught in English**. Examples include:

- **Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity** (also offered as a double degree with the University of Udine)
- **Information and Communications Engineering**
- **Game Studies and Engineering**
- **International Management**
- **Media and Convergence Management**
- **Cross-Border Studies**
- **Worlds of English**

### Master's admission basics

Some programmes, such as Worlds of English, only require a relevant Bachelor's degree and English at C1 level. Others use a **special admission procedure**. International Management, Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity, Game Studies and Engineering, and Media and Convergence Management all fall into this group, with limited places in some cases. English requirements differ by programme (for example, B2 for Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity and C1 for International Management).

The general Master's admission period for the winter semester 2026/27 ran from 6 July to 31 October 2026. Special-procedure programmes run on their own, earlier timelines.

## English-Taught PhD Programmes at Klagenfurt

Doctoral studies at Klagenfurt are research-led and typically take **three years full-time**. Doctoral areas listed on international study portals include:

- **Technical Sciences**
- **Natural Sciences**
- **Social Sciences and Economics**
- **Law**

Doctoral candidates can also access internal grants and scholarships. Because PhD admission usually depends on your research proposal and finding a supervisor, start by identifying researchers whose work matches your interests, and confirm the language of supervision and coursework directly with the faculty.

## Tuition Fees and Living Costs

Austria's public universities are affordable compared with many study destinations.

- **EU/EEA students**: no tuition during the standard programme duration plus tolerance semesters; after that, EUR 363.36 per semester.
- **Non-EU/EEA students**: EUR 726.72 per semester from the first semester, with the university offering full or partial tuition reimbursement based on certain performance rules.
- **Student union (ÖH) fee**: payable every semester in addition to tuition.
- **Living costs**: estimated at EUR 720 to 980 per month.

> **Note:** Fees are adjusted periodically. Confirm the current amounts on the university's official website.

## How to Apply: A Simple Checklist

1. **Choose your programme** and check its language and admission requirements.
2. **Prove your English level** with an accepted certificate.
3. **Prepare your documents**: academic transcripts, degree certificates, CV and any programme-specific materials (for example a motivation video for Media and Convergence Management).
4. **Check the admission window** and whether the programme uses a special admission procedure.
5. **Apply on time**, and as a non-EU applicant, apply in the earliest available window where one exists.
6. **Plan your visa and accommodation** once you have an admission offer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the University of Klagenfurt teach in English?
Yes. It offers 16 degree programmes taught in English, including several Bachelor's programmes, 11 Master's programmes and doctoral options.

### Is it free to study at the University of Klagenfurt?
EU/EEA students pay no tuition within the standard study period. Non-EU students pay EUR 726.72 per semester, plus the student union fee.

### When should I apply?
Bachelor's applications for the winter semester generally open in July and close in early September; Master's applications for the winter semester generally run until the end of October, with special-procedure programmes on earlier timelines. Dates change every year, so verify them on the official site.
