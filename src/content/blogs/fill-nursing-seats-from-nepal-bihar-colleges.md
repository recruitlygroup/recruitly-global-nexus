# How Bihar Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Bihar borders Nepal, and many families in Nepal's southern plains already have **personal and community ties** to the state. That closeness gives Bihar colleges a real advantage, if they present themselves clearly and earn trust.

---

## Why Nepali Families Consider Bihar

- **Very short travel distance** from many parts of Nepal
- **Language and cultural familiarity**
- **Lower travel and living costs** for families
- **Easy family visits**, which supports retention
- **Existing community connections** that influence decisions

---

## What Bihar Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** with hostel and clinical charges
- ✓ **Clinical training facilities** and hospital exposure
- ✓ **Hostel safety and supervision**
- ✓ **Admission process** and documents needed from Nepali students
- ✓ **A named contact** who replies quickly

> State rules and affiliations differ by institution and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

---

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Families comparing Bihar with other states** for facilities and reputation
- **Trust and quality concerns.** Colleges must prove recognition and clinical exposure
- **Competition from nearby border-state colleges**
- **Expectations about hostel and living standards**

---

## How to Stand Out

- Lead with **proof**: approvals, hospital names, student stories
- Offer **virtual or in-person counseling** for border-region families. See [education fairs and virtual counseling](/blog/education-fairs-and-virtual-counseling-for-nepali-nursing-students/)
- Keep **fees transparent** and consistent across all materials
- **Prevent drop-outs** with strong orientation. See our [retention guide](/blog/why-nepali-students-drop-out-of-nursing-in-india-and-how-to-prevent-it/)
