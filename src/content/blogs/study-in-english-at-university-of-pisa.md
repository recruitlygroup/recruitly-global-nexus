# Study in English at the University of Pisa: Bachelor, Master & PhD Guide

Want to **study in Italy in English** at a historic research university? The **University of Pisa (Unipi)**, founded in 1343, offers **17 master's programs and an MBA taught exclusively in English**, plus two English-taught bachelor's programs and PhD programs where you can work in English.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It covers programs, costs and deadlines.

> **Heads up:** Dates, fees and programs change each cycle. The details below come from Unipi's official pages and 2026/27 calls. Confirm everything on the official Unipi site and the application portal before you apply.

---

## Why Study at the University of Pisa?

Unipi is a public university with twenty departments and a strong reputation in engineering, computer science, sciences and economics. For international students, that means:

- **A large English-taught master's offer**, especially in engineering and computer science
- **A dedicated application portal** for English-taught master's programs (ApplyMscEnglish)
- **Scholarship options** such as DSU grants, MAECI scholarships and, for some programs, Invest Your Talent in Italy

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Degrees at the University of Pisa

Unipi's English-programs page lists **two bachelor's degrees**:

- **International Programme in Humanities (IPH)**, Department of Civilisations and Forms of Knowledge
- **Management for Business and Economics (MBE-L)**, Department of Economics and Management

Some courses within the single-cycle degree in Pharmaceutical Chemistry and Technology are also taught in English. Most other bachelor's degrees are taught in Italian. Check each program's admission rules and language requirements.

### English language proof

Programs generally require English at **B2 (CEFR)** or higher. If your bachelor's degree was taught in English, some calls accept a university certificate of the teaching language instead of a language test.

## PhD Programs at the University of Pisa

For the **42nd cycle (2026/27)**, Unipi ran **two calls**: **Call A** (deadline **8 June 2026**) and **Call B** (program sheets list **10 August 2026**). All programs last **three years** and start on **1 November 2026**.

- **Tuition:** PhD candidates are exempt from tuition and pay only the annual regional tax of **€140**.
- **Funding:** Each program sheet lists its available positions with grants. Check the sheet for the exact number and amount.
- **Language:** Some programs allow interviews and proposals in English. Others assess Italian too. Read the sheet for your program.

---

## University of Pisa Application Fee and Tuition

- **Application fee:** **€30** per application for English-taught master's programs, paid in the portal. It applies to applicants holding a foreign qualification, and the portal allows **no more than two applications**.
- **Tuition:** Annual fees depend on your country of origin. For 2025/26 they ranged from **€446 to €2,900**. Fees are usually announced in June, so confirm the 2026/27 figures.
- **Scholarships:** DSU Toscana grants (free housing, meals and tuition exemption), MAECI scholarships, Invest Your Talent in Italy for some programs, and academic excellence awards for some master's programs.

## University of Pisa Application Deadlines

Deadlines are program-specific. Examples from the **2026/27** calls:

| Applicant type | Window |
|---|---|
| **Visa applicants (Round 1, e.g. Nuclear Engineering)** | 5 Nov 2025 – 5 Feb 2026 |
| **Applicants without a visa requirement (Round 2)** | 6 Feb – 31 May 2026 |
| **Other programs** | Window opened between late November and January, with some closing 30 April |
| **PhD Call A / Call B** | 8 Jun 2026 / 10 Aug 2026 |

For **2027/28**, last year's calls opened between November and January, so check the portal now and plan for a **February** deadline if you need a visa.

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Choose your master's program** from Unipi's English-taught list.
2. **Read the call for applications**, including requirements and interview steps.
3. **Register on the ApplyMscEnglish portal** and complete your application (maximum two).
4. **Pay the €30 fee** in the portal before submission.
5. **Pre-enrol on Universitaly** if you need a student visa.
6. **Enrol after arrival in Pisa**, following the instructions for international students.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the University of Pisa offer programs in English?
Yes. It offers 17 master's programs and an MBA taught exclusively in English, plus two English-taught bachelor's degrees.

### How much is the Pisa application fee?
For English-taught master's programs it is €30 per application for applicants with a foreign qualification.

### When is the deadline for non-EU master's applicants?
For 2026/27, visa applicants had until 5 February 2026 in the programs I reviewed. Check your program's call.

### Is a PhD at Pisa free?
PhD candidates are exempt from tuition and pay only a €140 regional tax. Many positions come with grants, which vary by program.
