# Best Nepali Hospitality Staff for Maldives Resorts

Whether you run a luxury island resort or a guesthouse, your guest reviews depend on your frontline team. Nepal offers a deep pool of hospitality talent. Here is what you can hire and what to look for.

---

## Roles You Can Fill From Nepal

### Housekeeping
- Room attendants
- Housekeeping supervisors
- Laundry and linen staff
- Public area cleaners

### Food & Beverage Service
- Waiters and waitresses
- Bartenders and barbacks
- Stewards
- Restaurant hosts

### Kitchen
- Commis and demi chefs
- Indian, Asian, and continental cuisine cooks
- Bakery and pastry assistants
- Kitchen stewards

### Front Office and Guest Services
- Front desk assistants
- Bellboys and porters
- Guest service attendants

### Spa and Recreation
- Spa therapists
- Spa attendants
- Gym and pool attendants

### Engineering and Maintenance
- Electricians, plumbers, AC technicians
- General maintenance technicians

---

## What to Check When Screening Hospitality Candidates

- ✓ **Relevant experience** in hotels, resorts, or restaurants
- ✓ **English communication** suitable for guest contact
- ✓ **Skills demonstration** for chefs, therapists, and technicians
- ✓ **Grooming and presentation** standards
- ✓ **Attitude and adaptability** for island living

---

## Tips for Better Hiring Results

- **Write a clear job description** with duties, hours, and accommodation details
- **Hire supervisors and staff together** so teams settle faster
- **Include a skills test** for specialized roles
- **Be upfront about island conditions** to reduce early resignations
