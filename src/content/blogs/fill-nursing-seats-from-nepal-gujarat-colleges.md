# How Gujarat Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Gujarat is a large western state with a strong industrial and education base. It is far from Nepal, so colleges here win students by being **clear about value, safety, and total cost.**

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Gujarat

- **Well-developed urban centers** such as Ahmedabad and Surat
- **Strong healthcare and hospital infrastructure** for clinical exposure
- **Hindi widely understood** in daily life
- **A range of nursing program options**

## What Gujarat Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** with hostel, clinical, and travel costs
- ✓ **Clinical training hospitals** and exposure
- ✓ **Orientation and a named contact** for students from Nepal

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Long distance and higher travel costs**
- **Low visibility** among Nepali families compared with border states
