# How Sikkim Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Sikkim has a large Nepali-speaking population and sits close to eastern Nepal. For Nepali families, that means a **familiar language, culture, and landscape**, which is a strong advantage for any college here.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Sikkim

- **Nepali widely spoken** in daily life
- **Cultural familiarity** and shared traditions
- **Close to eastern Nepal**, with easier family visits
- **Himalayan setting** and cool climate

## What Sikkim Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and living costs
- ✓ **Clinical training exposure** and partner hospitals
- ✓ **A named contact** and a warm orientation for students from Nepal

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **A small number of institutions and limited seats**
- **Hill roads and travel conditions** that families may ask about
