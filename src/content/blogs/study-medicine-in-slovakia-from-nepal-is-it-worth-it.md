# Study Medicine in Slovakia From Nepal: Is It Worth It If You Missed a Nepal Seat?

If your CEE rank did not reach a government scholarship seat and the Nepal paying fee is too high, Europe is one of the options families ask about. **Slovakia** teaches General Medicine in English at several faculties. This guide lays out the facts, and the trade-offs, so you can decide with clear numbers.

> **Verify before you pay.** Fees and exam dates below are the published figures for the 2026/27 intake (Jessenius for 2027/28), as reported by university guides. Faculties change them every year. Confirm each on the faculty's own admissions page.

## What it costs

| Faculty | City | Published tuition per year |
|---|---|---|
| Comenius University, Faculty of Medicine | Bratislava | €13,000 |
| Comenius University, Jessenius Faculty of Medicine | Martin | €11,500 (2027/28) |
| UPJŠ, Faculty of Medicine | Košice | €13,000 |

General Medicine takes **six years**, and the faculties state the same tuition applies each year of the standard period. At €11,500–13,000 a year that is roughly **€69,000–78,000 in tuition over six years**, before food, housing, insurance and travel. Compare that with the Nepal paying-seat ceiling of about NPR 40–46 lakh for the whole MBBS, and remember the euro exchange rate moves.

## How admission works

- You sit an **entrance exam in biology and chemistry**, multiple choice. For example UPJŠ Košice uses a written exam of 200 questions in 2.5 hours; Comenius Bratislava uses an online exam with 100 biology and 100 chemistry questions in 180 minutes.
- Faculties offer **several exam dates a year**. Register and upload your documents before the deadline for the date you choose.
- The exam is competitive and prepared for in advance. Do not register without studying the faculty's syllabus.

## What Nepali students must do first

1. **MEC rules.** The Medical Education Commission requires Nepali students going abroad for a medical degree to pass MECEE-BL and obtain an eligibility letter / NOC through the official process. Check the current notice before you apply.
2. **Recognition in Nepal.** To practise in Nepal the degree must be recognised and registered by the Nepal Medical Council, and you must pass the NMCLE. Ask the Council in writing about the specific faculty.
3. **Visa.** Nepal has no Slovak embassy. Applications go through the Slovak Embassy in New Delhi. Read the [step-by-step visa guide](/blog/slovak-student-visa-from-nepal-step-by-step).

## Is it worth it?

It can be, if:
- you passed MECEE-BL and have the MEC eligibility letter,
- your family can fund roughly €70,000+ in tuition plus living costs,
- you are ready for a six-year programme and an entrance exam in biology and chemistry.

It may not be, if the total cost is beyond your budget, or if you cannot get the Nepal recognition steps confirmed in writing. A cheaper route might be a Nepal paying seat, India, or another destination.
