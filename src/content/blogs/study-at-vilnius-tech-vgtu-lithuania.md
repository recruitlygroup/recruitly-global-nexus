# Study at VILNIUS TECH (VGTU) in Lithuania

Looking for an **engineering, IT, or business degree in Europe taught in English**? **Vilnius Gediminas Technical University (VILNIUS TECH, formerly VGTU)** offers Bachelor's and Master's programmes for international students in Lithuania's capital. This guide explains what to expect and how **Recruitly Group** supports you from first enquiry to arrival.

> **Verify before applying:** Fees, deadlines, scholarship rules, and programme lists change every intake. Confirm everything on the official VILNIUS TECH admissions pages.

---

## Why Choose VILNIUS TECH?

VILNIUS TECH is a technical university based in **Vilnius**, with its main campus in the Saulėtekis student district. It is known for **engineering, information technology, architecture, and business** studies, and it publishes a list of programmes taught in English each academic year. Third-party sources report that students come from **more than 90 countries**.

**Why students choose it:**

- A technology-focused education in **Vilnius**
- English-taught Bachelor's, Master's, and doctoral options
- **Tuition-free and partial scholarships** for eligible international applicants
- Online application through the university's admissions platform

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programmes at VILNIUS TECH

### What to expect

- **Format:** Full-time study, with university Bachelor's programmes commonly lasting around 4 years (some professional programmes are shorter)
- **Fields:** Engineering, IT and artificial intelligence, architecture, business management, creative industries, and more
- **Typical requirements:** Secondary school certificate, with legalised and translated documents, plus proof of English
- **Tuition:** For 2024-2025, non-EU fees were published at roughly **€3,120 to €5,320 per year** depending on the field, with Engineering/IT/Technology programmes around €4,050. Fees are updated each year, so check the current table.
- **Scholarships:** The university has offered **tuition-free and partial (50% and 25%) scholarships** for first-year Bachelor's applicants, with a separate earlier deadline (April 20 in recent years)

### Who it suits

Students who want a **practical, technology-driven degree** in a capital-city setting.

---

## English-Taught Master's Programmes at VILNIUS TECH

### What to expect

- **Format:** Full-time study, commonly around 2 years
- **Fields:** Engineering, IT, business, aviation, biomedical and nanobiotechnology, and more
- **Typical requirements:** A Bachelor's degree in a relevant field (some programmes list a minimum 60% CGPA) and English at around B2 level, sometimes with an interview
- **Tuition:** 2024-2025 non-EU Master's fees started around **€4,510 per year** for some business programmes, and some engineering programmes are listed higher (roughly €5,950 on third-party sites). Check current fees.
- **Scholarships:** Tuition-free and partial scholarships have been offered for first-year Master's applicants

### Who it suits

Graduates who want to **specialise, change fields, or earn an international qualification**.

## Scholarships and Tuition Fee Support

VILNIUS TECH has announced **tuition-free scholarships and partial scholarships (50% and 25%)** for both Bachelor's and Master's applicants. The scholarship deadline has been **earlier than the general application deadline**, which is why starting early matters. Ukrainian applicants have also been offered a tuition discount, according to the university.

**Tip:** Prepare your documents and English proof well before the scholarship deadline.
