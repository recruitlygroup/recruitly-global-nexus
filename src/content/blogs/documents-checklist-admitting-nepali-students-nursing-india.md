# Documents Checklist for Admitting Nepali Students Into Nursing Programs

Incomplete files slow admissions and frustrate families. Use this checklist to prepare early.

> **Important:** Requirements vary by program, state, and council rules. Always confirm the current list with the relevant Indian authorities and your university.

---

## Student Documents

- ✓ Valid passport or citizenship certificate, as required
- ✓ Academic transcripts and certificates, including grade 12 results
- ✓ Proof of subjects studied (for science-based eligibility)
- ✓ Equivalence or recognition documents, where required
- ✓ Passport-size photographs
- ✓ Medical fitness certificate, where required
- ✓ Character or migration certificate, where required
- ✓ Entrance or eligibility exam results, where applicable

---

## College Documents

- ✓ Program recognition and approval details
- ✓ Offer or admission letter
- ✓ Fee structure and payment terms
- ✓ Hostel and accommodation details
- ✓ Academic calendar and orientation schedule

---

## Verification Tips

- **Match name spellings** across every document
- **Check certificate authenticity** where required
- **Keep clear, complete scans**
- **Collect everything before submission**
- **Track each file** with a single owner
