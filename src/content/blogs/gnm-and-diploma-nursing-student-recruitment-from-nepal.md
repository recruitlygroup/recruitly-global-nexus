# GNM and Diploma in Nursing Student Recruitment From Nepal

Not every student wants a four-year degree. Many Nepali families prefer a **shorter, practical route** into nursing. That makes GNM and diploma programs a strong source of steady admissions for Indian colleges.

---

## Who Applies for These Programs

- Students who want a faster route to clinical practice
- Families with budget constraints
- Candidates looking for hands-on, hospital-based training
- Students who may later bridge into higher qualifications

---

## What to Screen For

- ✓ **Academic eligibility** per current Indian Nursing Council and state council requirements
- ✓ **Age and qualification** rules for each program
- ✓ **Basic English or Hindi communication**
- ✓ **Health and fitness** for clinical placements
- ✓ **Realistic expectations** about workload and career path

> **Always verify** current eligibility rules and any required exams with the relevant councils.

---

## What Colleges Should Communicate Clearly

- **Program duration and structure**
- **Total cost** including hostel, uniforms, and clinical fees
- **Hospital training exposure**
- **Recognition status** of the program
- **Pathways** for further study

---

## Tips for Better Results

- **Separate information** for GNM and diploma applicants so families are not confused
- **Offer counseling in Nepali or Hindi** where possible
- **Arrange campus or virtual tours**
- **Provide a clear orientation** for new arrivals
