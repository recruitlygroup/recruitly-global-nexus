# Study at the University of Camerino in English (2027/28)

**Quick answer:** The University of Camerino (UNICAM) offers **one Bachelor's program in English** (Biosciences and Biotechnology), **several English-taught Master's programs** in sciences and computing, and **PhD programs** in six research areas. Every international student, EU or non-EU, must start with an **online pre-admission**. The 2027/28 call has **not been published yet**; last year's non-EU deadline was **31 March 2026**.

This guide is for students in **Nepal, Finland and other EU countries** who want to study in Italy in English.

> **Note:** Official UNICAM pages I checked still show the **2026/27** pre-admission. Treat 2027/28 dates as unconfirmed until UNICAM publishes them at [international.unicam.it](https://international.unicam.it).

---

## Why choose the University of Camerino?

UNICAM is a **public university in the Marche region**, in the small hill town of Camerino, with additional campuses in Ascoli Piceno, Matelica and San Benedetto del Tronto. It is a good fit for students who want a **smaller, science-focused university** rather than a big-city campus. Education databases report roughly **6,000 students**, about **950 of them international**.

---

## Bachelor's program taught in English

UNICAM's Bachelor's degrees are mainly taught in Italian, with one exception:

- **Biosciences and Biotechnology** (3 years, 180 credits), with two curricula: **Biology** and **Biotechnology**, held in English.

All other Bachelor's programs are in Italian. For those, international students must prove **Italian at B2** by certificate or exam.

---

## Master's programs taught in English

UNICAM's official Master's list marks the following as taught in English (two years, 120 credits):

### Sciences

- **Chemistry** (partner: Instituto Superior Técnico, Lisbon)
- **Physics**
- **Mathematics and Applications** (two curricula: Advanced Theoretical Mathematics; Applied Engineering Oriented Mathematics)

### Computing and earth sciences

- **Computer Science** (four curricula; partners include Reykjavik University and the University of Applied Sciences Northwestern Switzerland)
- **Geoenvironmental Resources and Risks**

### Life sciences

- **Biological Sciences** (English-taught curricula: **Molecular Diagnostics and Biotechnology** and **Nutrition: Functional and Sustainable Food**; the conservation curriculum is mainly in Italian)

**Note:** *Food Science and Innovation* is taught in Italian with only a few exams in English, so it does not count as an English program.

## PhD programs

- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Research areas for 2026/27 (cycle XLII):** Architecture, Design, Planning; Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences and Biotechnology; Computer Science and Mathematics; Legal and Social Sciences; Life and Health Sciences; Physics, Earth and Materials Sciences
- **Calls:** usually announced **between May and July** each year on unicam.it, section "Bandi"
- **Funding:** positions and scholarships co-funded through special programs or agreements are available from time to time
- **Contact:** dottorato@unicam.it

UNICAM's International School of Advanced Studies was created in 2005 to increase foreign participation in its PhD programs. Check each program's call for its language and funding details, as these vary.

## Pre-admission: how it works

All international students must apply for pre-admission, because it is the only way to have your curriculum evaluated.

1. **Register** on the UNICAM application database using your details exactly as on your passport.
2. **Complete the preliminary evaluation application** and upload your documents. If even one mandatory document is missing, the application is rejected.
3. **Pay the €20 fee.** It is non-refundable, and applications without payment are not considered.
4. **Wait for the result.** Evaluation took an estimated **3 to 4 weeks** in the last cycle, and holidays can cause delays.
5. **If positive,** the Welcome Office sends instructions for **pre-enrollment on Universitaly**.

**Last cycle (2026/27):** the non-EU deadline was **31 March 2026**, and the call is now closed for non-EU students. For 2027/28, expect a similar autumn-to-spring window, but this is **not confirmed**.

> A positive pre-admission does **not** guarantee a visa or final enrollment, and it does **not** include a scholarship.

## Requirements and documents

- **Bachelor's applicants:** secondary school documents and transcripts as listed for your country
- **Master's applicants:** a Bachelor's degree that satisfies the access requirements, a **transcript of records** or Diploma Supplement, and course syllabi (recommended)
- **English:** a **B2 certificate** (Cambridge, IELTS or TOEFL) is recommended at pre-admission but not mandatory, and you should not upload medium-of-instruction certificates at that stage. A B2 certificate is expected for English-taught courses, so confirm the requirement for enrollment.
- **Translations:** not required for documents in English, Spanish or French
- **Country-based list:** UNICAM publishes country-specific requirements, so check the entry for **Nepal** or your country

Provisional documents can be uploaded if you are still studying, but final versions are required at enrollment.

## Scholarships and funding

### ERDIS regional scholarship

ERDIS is the regional student-aid body. Its scholarships are awarded on **merit and certified family income** and offer:

- **Tuition fee waiver**
- **Free housing and meals** in student residences and canteens from October to July
- **Possible additional cash assistance**

The UNICAM page I could check showed the 2024/25 call, so confirm the current call and international-student rules at erdis.it.

### UNICAM scholarships

- **Welcome in UNICAM:** for new students, with the notice usually announced in **July**
- **Talenti in UNICAM:** from the second year, usually announced in March
- **Part-time student jobs:** the notice is usually announced in November

> **Reality check:** "Fully funded" is possible through ERDIS but not guaranteed. Prepare a budget and a backup plan, and check the proof of funds required for your visa.

## From admission to visa

1. **Pre-admission** at UNICAM and a positive result
2. **Pre-enrollment on Universitaly**, validated by the university, with a pre-admission letter sent to your chosen embassy
3. **Apply for the national study visa (type D)** at the Italian embassy or consulate. UNICAM states that booking an appointment is your own responsibility.
4. **After arrival:** residence permit, tax code and final enrollment

**Nepal:** confirm which Italian mission and visa center cover Nepal and book early.
**Finland (non-EU nationals):** apply through the Italian mission covering your country of residence, and verify the current process.
**EU citizens:** no student visa is needed, but you still go through UNICAM pre-admission as an EU or EU-equivalent applicant. Contact welcome@unicam.it.

## FAQ

### When does UNICAM pre-admission open for 2027/28?

It has not been published yet. Last year's non-EU deadline was 31 March 2026.

### Does Camerino offer a Bachelor's in English?

One: Biosciences and Biotechnology, with Biology and Biotechnology curricula.

### How much is the application fee?

The pre-admission fee is **€20**, non-refundable.

### Do I need IELTS?

A B2 certificate is recommended at pre-admission, not mandatory, but English-taught courses expect B2, so prepare one.

### Is there a scholarship that covers everything?

ERDIS can cover tuition, housing and meals, based on merit and family income, but it is competitive.

### Can I apply to a PhD now?

PhD calls are usually announced between May and July. Prepare your proposal and documents beforehand.
