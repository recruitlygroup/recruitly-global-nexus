# How Telangana Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Telangana, centered on Hyderabad, is a major education and healthcare hub in southern India. Hyderabad's size and connectivity are strengths, but distance from Nepal means colleges must **reassure families on cost and safety.**

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Telangana

- **Hyderabad as a major education and healthcare center**
- **Strong air and rail connectivity**
- **Hindi and English widely understood in the city**
- **Many hospitals** for clinical training

## What Telangana Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** with hostel, clinical, and city living costs
- ✓ **Clinical training exposure** and hospital partners
- ✓ **Hostel safety** and a named contact for Nepali families

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Big-city living costs**
- **Long distance** from Nepal
- **Competition** among many Hyderabad institutions
