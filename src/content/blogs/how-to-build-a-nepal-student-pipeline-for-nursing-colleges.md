# How to Build a Nepal Student Pipeline for Your Nursing College: A 12-Month Blueprint

A pipeline is not one good intake. It is a **repeatable flow** of qualified students, year after year. Here is a practical 12-month plan for colleges starting from zero.

---

## Months 1 to 2: Prepare Your Foundation

- ✓ Create a clear **brochure and all-in fee sheet**. See our [brochure checklist](/blog/how-to-prepare-a-nursing-college-brochure-for-nepali-students/)
- ✓ Confirm **eligibility rules** for Nepali applicants
- ✓ Decide your **commission model** in writing
- ✓ Name one **admissions contact** with a fast response time

## Months 3 to 4: Choose Your Partner

- ✓ Shortlist **one or two** recruitment partners, not ten
- ✓ Ask the [10 key questions](/blog/how-to-choose-a-student-recruitment-partner-in-nepal-for-nursing/)
- ✓ Sign a written agreement

## Months 5 to 6: Launch Counseling

- ✓ Partner begins **family counseling** in Nepal
- ✓ Offer **virtual sessions** for parents. See [education fairs and virtual counseling](/blog/education-fairs-and-virtual-counseling-for-nepali-nursing-students/)
- ✓ Share student stories and hostel photos

## Months 7 to 8: Applications and Verification

- ✓ Receive **verified applications**
- ✓ Reply fast, because slow replies lose students
- ✓ Issue offers with clear fee terms

## Months 9 to 10: Confirmations and Arrival Prep

- ✓ Confirm seats and fee payments
- ✓ Prepare **orientation** and hostel allocation
- ✓ Brief families on travel and arrival

## Months 11 to 12: Retention and Review

- ✓ Run first-semester **check-ins**. See [how to prevent drop-outs](/blog/why-nepali-students-drop-out-of-nursing-in-india-and-how-to-prevent-it/)
- ✓ Review what worked: sources, programs, response times
- ✓ Pay commission **on time** and adjust terms for the next cycle

---

## What Makes a Pipeline Last

- **Consistency.** Same partner, same standards, every year
- **Fairness.** Clear terms and on-time payment keep good partners motivated
- **Honesty.** Realistic counseling lowers drop-out
- **Feedback.** Share outcomes so your partner improves matching
