## Why Engineering & Computing Degrees in the Philippines Carry Global Weight

For international students from **Nepal, India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Nigeria, Kenya, and the GCC**, choosing an engineering or computer science university comes down to **international license recognition and employer trust**. Philippine engineering and computing education stands out for three concrete reasons:

1. **US ABET & Washington Accord Alignment:**
   * **Mapúa University** became the **first institution in Southeast Asia** to earn accreditation from the US-based **Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology (ABET)** across both engineering and computing disciplines (including Computer Science, Information Technology, and Information Systems).
   * Furthermore, the Philippines (via the **Philippine Technological Council — PTC**) is a signatory to the **Washington Accord**, meaning graduates of PTC/ABET-accredited engineering programs hold substantial academic equivalence in the **United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, and GCC engineering councils** (such as the Society of Engineers UAE and Saudi Council of Engineers).
2. **100% English-Medium Technical Training & Industry Internships (OJT):**
   * All lectures, coding labs, CAD/BIM workshops, capstone theses, and mandatory **On-the-Job Training (OJT)** placements are conducted in English—preparing graduates immediately for multinational engineering firms and global tech teams.
3. **High-Growth BPO, Fintech & Semiconductor Ecosystem:**
   * Metro Manila and Cebu serve as major regional hubs for global IT consulting, cloud engineering, semiconductor manufacturing, and large-scale infrastructure development, giving students direct exposure to industry-sponsored labs and hackathons.

## Admission Requirements & Document Checklist (2026/27)

To qualify for an Engineering, IT, or Computer Science program, international applicants need:

1. **Grade 12 / 10+2 / A-Level Transcripts:** Strong passes in **Mathematics (Algebra, Trigonometry, Pre-Calculus)** and **Physics / Chemistry** (typically minimum 60% aggregate; competitive schools like UP Diliman, DLSU, and Mapúa evaluate math/science grades closely).
2. **University Entrance Assessment:**
   * **Mapúa University:** Mapúa Program Placement Assessment (MPASS) — available online for international applicants.
   * **DLSU & UP Diliman:** University admission test (DCAT / UPCAT) or SAT/IB/A-Level international credential evaluation track.
3. **Authenticated Documents (Apostille or Embassy Legalization):**
   * **Hague Apostille Member Countries** *(India, Indonesia, Nigeria, Kenya, GCC states)*: Apostille your Grade 12 mark sheets, graduation certificate, birth certificate, and national police clearance.
   * **Non-Apostille Countries** *(Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, UAE, Qatar)*: Complete **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation followed by Philippine Embassy/Consulate legalization**.
4. **Valid Passport & Financial Proof:** Passport valid for at least 12 months plus bank statement/certificate showing **$6,000–$8,500 USD** equivalent.
5. **English Proficiency:** Waived if your secondary school curriculum was taught in English; otherwise, IELTS (5.5–6.0), TOEFL iBT, or university English placement test applies.

## Global Recognition After Graduation: Licensure & Tech Careers

* **Practicing Engineering in the GCC, North America, or Home Country:**
  * Because the Philippines restricts local PRC engineering board licensure exams to Filipino citizens (unless formal reciprocity exists), international engineering graduates rely on **ABET accreditation and Washington Accord equivalence** to register directly with engineering bodies abroad (such as **PEO in Canada, NCEES/FE exam in the USA, Engineers Australia, or GCC engineering councils**).
  * *Nepali & Indian Applicants:* Verify equivalence rules with the **Nepal Engineering Council (NEC)** or **Association of Indian Universities (AIU)** prior to enrollment—selecting a **CHED Center of Excellence** or **ABET-accredited university** ensures smooth credential recognition.
* **Global Software, AI & Data Science Careers:**
  * Computer Science and IT roles do not require country-specific board licenses. Graduates build GitHub portfolios, complete industry capstone projects, and launch careers across global tech hubs in Singapore, the Middle East, Europe, North America, or remote engineering teams.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are engineering degrees from the Philippines recognized in the USA, Canada, and the GCC?
Yes—especially if you graduate from an **ABET-accredited program** at institutions like **Mapúa University** or **T.I.P.** ABET accreditation and the Philippines' **Washington Accord** membership provide direct academic equivalence for engineering licensure and employment across North America, Europe, Australia, and the Middle East.

### How long does an Engineering or Computer Science degree take in the Philippines?
Following the Philippines' K-12 education reform, **Bachelor of Science degrees in Engineering, Computer Science, and Information Technology take 4 years** to complete (with BS Architecture taking 5 years). Universities on a trimester calendar like De La Salle University can sometimes be completed slightly faster depending on credit load.

### Do I need to pass a local Philippine board exam to graduate with an Engineering degree?
No. You earn your full **Bachelor of Science (BS) degree** upon completing your university coursework, capstone thesis, and mandatory On-the-Job Training (OJT). Local Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) board exams are separate post-graduation licensing exams for Filipino citizens practicing domestically.

### Which university in the Philippines is best for Computer Science and AI?
**UP Diliman**, **De La Salle University (DLSU)**, and **Mapúa University** lead the country in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Data Science, offering dedicated AI research centers, industry partnerships, and ABET Computing Accreditation.

### Can international students do paid internships in the Philippines?
Required academic **On-the-Job Training (OJT)** for university credit is permitted under your **9(f) Student Visa** when endorsed by your university's practicum office, though standard off-campus commercial employment remains prohibited under Philippine immigration rules.
