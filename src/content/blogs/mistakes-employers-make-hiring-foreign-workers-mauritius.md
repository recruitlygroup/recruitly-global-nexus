# 7 Costly Mistakes Mauritius Employers Make When Hiring Foreign Workers

Overseas hiring can transform your business, or drain your budget. Most problems come from the same avoidable mistakes.

---

## 1. Starting Too Late
Waiting until you are short-staffed forces rushed decisions. **Start early** and build in time for permits and documentation. See the [recruitment timeline](/blog/nepal-to-mauritius-recruitment-timeline/).

## 2. Writing Vague Job Descriptions
"Need 10 workers" is not a requirement. Specify duties, skills, experience, salary, and conditions.

## 3. Using Multiple Unconnected Agents
Several middlemen means unclear responsibility, duplicate fees, and inconsistent quality. **Choose one accountable partner.**

## 4. Skipping Skill Verification
A CV is not proof of skill. Use interviews and trade tests, especially for chefs, technicians, machine operators, and tradespeople.

## 5. Ignoring the Full Cost
Focusing only on the agency fee hides permit, medical, travel, and replacement costs. Read the [cost breakdown](/blog/cost-of-hiring-nepali-workers-in-mauritius/).

## 6. Weak Contracts and Unclear Terms
Disputes about salary, hours, accommodation, or duties damage trust and retention. **Put everything in writing** before departure.

## 7. No Onboarding or Support Plan
Workers who arrive without orientation are more likely to leave. Plan arrival, housing, introductions, and a point of contact.
