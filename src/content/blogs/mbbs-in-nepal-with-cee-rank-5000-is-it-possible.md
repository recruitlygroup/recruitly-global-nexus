# MBBS in Nepal With a CEE Rank of 5,000: Is It Possible?

Short answer: **a scholarship is very unlikely, and a paying seat is unlikely unless seats stay vacant.** Here is the reasoning, using real MEC numbers, so you can judge your own rank.

## Where rank 5,000 sits

In the MECEE-BL 2027 MBBS list, rank 5,000 has a score of 86.25 and is ahead of about 51.6% of the 10,321 qualified candidates. You qualified (the cut-off is 56.25), but 2,745 MBBS seats exist in total for 2083/84.

## Scholarship seats: closing ranks in BS 2082

| Quota | Last rank admitted (2082) |
|---|---|
| Open scholarship | 453 |
| Female | 2,723 |
| Adivasi Janajati | 3,633 |
| Tharu | 3,998 |
| Madhesi Dalit | 5,214 |
| Dalit | 5,271 |
| Dalit female | 6,261 |

Only the **Dalit-related quotas** (Madhesi Dalit, Dalit and Dalit female) closed beyond rank 5,000 in 2082, and quota ranks move a lot between years. See every quota for 2080–2082 on our [scholarship cut-off page](/cee/mbbs-scholarship-cutoff-rank). If you belong to a quota, compare against that quota, not only Open.

## Paying seats

MEC allocated 2,745 MBBS seats across 28 institutions. Paying seats are filled in merit order by candidates who choose to pay. A rank of 5,000 is well beyond the total seat count, so a seat would depend on vacancies and later rounds. We cannot promise that, and no honest consultancy can. The reported MEC fee ceiling for Nepali paying seats is about NPR 40.2–46.0 lakh in total, so also check whether the budget works.

## What to do with a rank like this

1. **Check your exact position** with the [CEE result checker](/cee/result-2083).
2. **Check your quota** (Female, Janajati, Dalit, Madhesi, Tharu, Muslim, Backward Area and others).
3. **Keep a second plan.** Compare [MBBS abroad](/cee/mbbs-abroad-for-nepali-students): Georgia, the Philippines and others have different costs, entry rules and Nepal recognition rules. Nepali students must follow MEC's eligibility-letter process before enrolling abroad.
4. **Consider India.** MBBS/BDS there need NEET-UG; BSc Nursing is admitted on merit. See [study in India](/student-recruitment/india).
