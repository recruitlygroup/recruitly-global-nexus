# Education Fairs and Virtual Counseling for Nepali Nursing Students

Families want to **meet the college**, or at least someone who speaks for it, before sending a student away. Counseling sessions and fairs let your admissions team answer questions face to face, which builds trust faster than any brochure.

---

## Option 1: Virtual Counseling Sessions

### What They Are
Scheduled online sessions where your admissions representative meets students and parents.

### Why They Work
- ✓ **Low cost.** No travel required
- ✓ **Direct answers** about fees, hostel, and clinical training
- ✓ **Parents can join**, which matters for decisions
- ✓ **Repeatable** each intake cycle

### How to Run One
- Share a **short presentation** with approvals, fees, and student life
- Show **hostel and campus photos or video**
- Allow time for **parent questions**
- Follow up within 24 hours with the fee sheet and next steps

---

## Option 2: Education Fairs and Counseling Days in Nepal

### What They Are
In-person events where several colleges meet students and families in one place, or a counseling day run by a local partner.

### Why They Work
- ✓ **Face-to-face trust**
- ✓ **Visibility** in the student's own city
- ✓ **Direct feedback** on what families care about

### Things to Plan
- Clear **printed fee sheets**
- A representative who can speak **Hindi or English** comfortably
- A simple **sign-up and follow-up process**
- Realistic **travel and cost planning**

---

## Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Attending with no clear fee sheet
- ❌ Promising admission on the spot without checking eligibility
- ❌ Collecting contacts and never following up
- ❌ Overstating job or placement outcomes
