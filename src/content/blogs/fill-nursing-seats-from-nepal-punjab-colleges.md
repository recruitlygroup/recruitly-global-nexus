# How Punjab Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Punjab is a northern state with many private colleges and universities. It is well connected by rail to the rest of north India, and its institutions are often **active in student outreach**, so competition for Nepali families can be strong.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Punjab

- **Many institutions** and a wide choice of programs
- **Hindi widely understood**
- **Good road and rail links** across north India
- **Established culture of private higher education**

## What Punjab Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel, clinical, and travel costs
- ✓ **Clinical training exposure** and hospital partnerships
- ✓ **A named contact** and orientation for Nepali students

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Crowded market.** Families compare many Punjab options
- **Winter weather** and distance from Nepal
