## What is "MBBS in Italy"?

In Italy the medical degree is called **Medicine and Surgery (Laurea Magistrale a ciclo unico, LM-41)**. It is the equivalent of an MBBS/MD: a single-cycle program with no separate bachelor stage. Several public universities teach it fully in English for international students, with tuition set according to family income, which makes it far cheaper than private medical colleges in many other countries.

## Program duration and structure

- **Duration:** 6 years (360 ECTS/CFU)
- **Years 1-3:** basic sciences (anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, histology) plus early clinical exposure
- **Years 4-6:** clinical rotations in university hospitals, clinical electives and the final thesis
- **Language:** English-taught lectures and exams. You will still need to learn basic Italian, because patient contact during clinical years happens in Italian.
- **After graduation:** a state qualifying exam and registration are needed to practise in Italy. Recognition elsewhere in the EU follows EU rules.

## English-taught universities (general quota)

Universities offering English-taught Medicine through the national IMAT include, among others:

- University of Pavia
- University of Milan (Statale) and Milano-Bicocca
- Sapienza and Tor Vergata (Rome)
- University of Naples Federico II and Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli"
- University of Bari and University of Messina
- University of Padua, Turin, Perugia, Brescia and Insubria (Varese)

The list, seat numbers and non-EU quotas change every year. Private universities such as Humanitas or San Raffaele run their own admission tests and are outside the general public quota. In the 2026 decree, the Ministry increased both total seats and the seats reserved for non-EU candidates, so check the current decree for exact numbers.

## Admission requirements

- A **secondary school diploma** with at least 12 years of schooling (Nepal's +2 / Grade 12 with science subjects), valid for university entry in Nepal
- **Pre-enrolment on Universitaly** (mandatory for non-EU students living abroad)
- A **Declaration of Value / diploma recognition** from the Italian diplomatic mission, with your documents attested by the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), Kathmandu**
- Registration and fee payment for the **IMAT**
- A valid passport. Check each year's decree for whether an English certificate is needed; the IMAT itself has not required one in recent years.

## About the IMAT exam

The **International Medical Admissions Test (IMAT)** is the national entrance exam for English-taught Medicine in Italy.

- Paper-based, **100 minutes**, multiple choice
- Covers logical reasoning and general knowledge, biology, chemistry, and physics and mathematics
- Scoring is positive for correct answers, negative for wrong answers and zero for blanks, so accuracy matters
- Held once a year, in September, at test centres in Italy and in selected countries. Centres change annually, so check the decree and choose early.
- Scores appear in your Universitaly account a few weeks later, and you are ranked against everyone who listed the same universities

**Preparation:** start at least 6-8 months before the exam. **Recruitly Group offers an online IMAT preparation course** covering all sections, past-paper practice and timed mocks. Email info@recruitlygroup.com for the current batch.

## Fully funded regional scholarship

Each Italian region has a student-welfare body (for example DiSCo in Lazio, ER.GO in Emilia-Romagna, EDISU in Piedmont, Regione Lombardia/university DSU offices). Their **regional (DSU) scholarship** can cover a tuition waiver, a yearly cash grant, subsidised accommodation and meal vouchers. It is awarded **after you are enrolled**, based on your economic situation and academic merit, and you must renew it by meeting credit requirements each year.

**Documents usually required (the exact list depends on the region and university):**

1. Income documents from Nepal proving your family's financial situation, including a **Low Income Certificate** in the required format
2. A **Relationship Certificate** in the required format, to show who the family members are
3. All of the above **attested by MoFA, Kathmandu**, then translated and legalised as the university requires
4. Passport copies and the university's own forms

Not sure about the format? **Request the template letter by writing to info@recruitlygroup.com.**

## Italian student visa from Nepal

Nepal is served by the **Embassy of Italy in Kolkata**, and you can submit your application at **VFS Global, Kathmandu**.

1. Get your **assignment/acceptance** from the university after the IMAT ranking
2. Book your appointment and submit the Type D (study) visa file at VFS Kathmandu
3. Provide passport, admission letter, accommodation proof, health insurance, flight booking and proof of financial means
4. Wait for processing, collect your passport, then travel and apply for your permit of stay (*permesso di soggiorno*) within 8 days of arrival

### ⚠️ Important: the scholarship is not proof of funds

The regional scholarship is paid **after** you enrol, so the consulate **cannot count it as your financial resources**. The visa officer assesses whether you can support yourself from your own means. You must therefore show your genuine funds in the amount the consulate requires for your stay (confirm the current figure with the embassy or Recruitly before you prepare your bank documents). Always answer every question truthfully and make sure every document is accurate. Misstatements can lead to refusal and long-term problems with future applications. Scholarship is a benefit you apply for once you are in Italy, not something to build your visa case on.

- **Oct-Dec 2026:** choose universities, check IMAT syllabus, start the Recruitly online IMAT course, begin collecting academic documents

- **Mar-Apr 2027:** profile review, finalise university choices, prepare your English certificate if required
- **May-Jun 2027:** Universitaly pre-enrolment. In recent years the deadline for students needing a visa has been around the end of June, so do not wait.

- **Aug 2027:** full mock tests, plan travel to your test centre, prepare scholarship papers
- **Sep 2027:** sit the IMAT
- **Oct 2027:** scores and ranking released, accept your seat
- **Oct-Dec 2027:** enrolment, visa appointment at VFS Kathmandu, accommodation, insurance, travel
- **After arrival:** residence permit, DSU scholarship application, ISEE parificato for tuition bands

## Already inside the EU?

If you are already in Italy or another EU country, the route is **different**. Your residence status, your permit type and the quota you fall under determine whether you need pre-enrolment, a new visa or only a permit conversion. Rules differ for legal residents in Italy compared with those elsewhere in the EU, and they can change each year. Send us your permit details and we will confirm your exact steps.
