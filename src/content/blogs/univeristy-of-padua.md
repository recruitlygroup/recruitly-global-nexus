# University of Padua Admission 2026 for Non-EU Students: Complete Application Guide

Admissions for the **First Call** at the University of Padua (Università degli Studi di Padova) are open for non-EU applicants. The application window runs from **15 September 2026 to 15 November 2026**. If you plan to study in Italy, now is the time to prepare your documents and submit a complete application.

This guide is part of our University Series. It explains what you need to apply, what to watch out for, and where to check your eligibility.

## Key Dates at a Glance

- **Applications open:** 15 September 2026
- **First Call closes:** 15 November 2026
- **Who can apply:** Non-EU applicants (EU applicants follow their own entry rules)

Always confirm dates and programme details on the official University of Padua website before you submit.

## Documents Required to Apply

You will upload the following documents in your online application:

1. **Valid passport or ID card** (ID card only for EU or equivalent applicants)
2. **Italian residence permit** (only for non-EU applicants already living in Italy)
3. **Academic transcript or marksheet**
4. **Upper secondary school certificate or academic qualification**, if already obtained. Check the country-based entry title requirements here: https://www.unipd.it/en/entry-title-requirements
5. **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**
6. **Motivation letter**
7. **Language proficiency certificate.** The full list of accepted English certificates, minimum scores, and exemptions is here: https://www.unipd.it/en/studying-padua/admission/language-requirement
8. **Other documents required for your chosen degree programme**

This is a general list. Once you add a degree programme to your application, the system adds tasks to your **Checklist** section. Follow those tasks for the exact documents your programme needs.

## Document Rules You Must Follow

- **All documents must be uploaded in PDF format.**
- Diplomas and transcripts must be **official**, meaning they carry the institution's stamp and the signature of the person responsible for issuing them.
- Documents must be written or translated in **Italian or English**.
- If your documents are issued in another language, upload them together with an **official translation** into Italian or English.
- **No legalised documents are required to submit your application.**

If you are admitted, you will need to submit further documents to complete your enrolment. Details are available here: https://www.unipd.it/en/studiare-inglese-immatricolarsi

## Application Tips

- Read the guidelines and instructions carefully while completing your application.
- Prepare scanned, clear PDFs before you begin.
- Have your language certificate ready early, since test dates can take time to arrange.
- Keep your motivation letter specific to the programme you choose.
- Do not wait for the last week. Submit well before 15 November 2026.
