# CEE Result 2083: What Your Rank, Score and Percentile Actually Mean

The Medical Education Commission (MEC) published the MECEE-BL 2027 results (CEE 2083) on **18 November 2026 for MBBS**, **19 November for BDS** and **21 November for BSc Nursing/Midwifery**. If you are staring at a roll number, a score and a rank and wondering what they mean for admission, this guide explains it with the real numbers.

## The three numbers you were given

- **Score:** your marks in the exam. Higher is better.
- **Rank:** your position on your program's list. Rank 1 is the highest score. Candidates with equal scores sit next to each other.
- **Qualifying cut-off:** MEC's 50th-percentile score. Below it you do not qualify for a seat.

## What qualified this year

| Program | Qualifying cut-off | Candidates who qualified | Top score |
|---|---|---|---|
| MBBS | 56.25 | 10,321 | 187.5 |
| BDS | 62.25 | 6,256 | 183.75 |
| BSc Nursing/Midwifery | 48 | 10,805 | 181 |

## Turning your rank into a percentile

Percentile here means the share of qualified candidates ranked below you. For MBBS, with 10,321 qualified:

| MBBS rank | Score at that rank | You are ahead of |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | 159 | 99.0% |
| 500 | 143.75 | 95.2% |
| 1,000 | 132.5 | 90.3% |
| 5,000 | 86.25 | 51.6% |

A rank in the top 10% sounds strong, and it is. But admission is decided by **seats**, not percentile. MEC allocated 2,745 MBBS seats for 2083/84, so even rank 2,745 (percentile 73.4) is at the edge of total capacity.

## Qualifying is not the same as getting a seat

- **Seats:** 2,745 MBBS, 625 BDS and 2,640 BSc Nursing for 2083/84, against 10,321, 6,256 and 10,805 qualified candidates.
- **Quota:** reserved quotas (Female, Janajati, Madhesi, Dalit, Tharu, Muslim, Backward Area and others) close at different ranks. See [MBBS scholarship cut-off ranks by quota](/cee/mbbs-scholarship-cutoff-rank).
- **Scholarship vs paying:** government colleges give about 75% of seats as scholarships. Private colleges charge MEC-capped tuition.
