# Partner With Recruitly Group: Send Us Your Nursing College Brochure and Commission Model

If your nursing college wants more qualified students from Nepal, the fastest way to start is simple: **send us your brochure and your commission terms.** We review them, tell you honestly how well your program fits the Nepali market, and begin counseling students.

---

## Who This Is For

- Colleges offering **BSc Nursing**
- Colleges offering **GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery)**
- Colleges offering **Diploma in Nursing** programs
- Institutions with open seats for the upcoming intake
- Colleges that want a reliable, long-term Nepal pipeline

---

- ✓ **Students and families never pay us.** We do not charge for college search or admission support
- ✓ **You pay by commission**, flexible and agreed in writing, so your cost follows results
- ✓ **Documents verified** before applications reach your admissions team
- ✓ **Honest counseling** that sets realistic expectations and lowers drop-out
- ✓ **One accountable contact** from first inquiry to student arrival

## How the Partnership Works

### Step 1: Send Us Your Details
Email your brochure, fee structure, and commission model. See [what to include](/blog/how-to-prepare-a-nursing-college-brochure-for-nepali-students/).

### Step 2: We Review and Reply
We assess your program against what Nepali families look for and tell you where you are strong and where to improve.

### Step 3: Agree Terms in Writing
Commission trigger, amount, payment timeline, and drop-out terms. Learn more in our [commission guide](/blog/flexible-commission-student-recruitment-nursing-colleges-india/).

### Step 4: We Start Counseling Students
We match your program with suitable students and families.

### Step 5: Verified Applications Reach You
Complete files, ready for your admissions decision.

### Step 6: Arrival and Follow-Up
We support orientation and stay in touch with families after arrival.
