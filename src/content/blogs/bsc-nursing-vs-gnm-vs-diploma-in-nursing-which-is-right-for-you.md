## The Three Programs

| | BSc Nursing | GNM | Diploma in Nursing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level** | Degree | Diploma | Diploma or certificate |
| **Duration** | Longer (about 4 years) | Shorter | Varies |
| **Best for** | Students aiming for wider career and further study | Students wanting a faster route to clinical work | Students wanting a short, practical start |
| **Further study** | Opens the way to higher nursing degrees | Limited unless you bridge later | Depends on the program |

> Duration, rules, and recognition can change. Always confirm with the council and the college.

---

## How to Choose

- ✓ **Think about your career goal.** Hospital work, teaching, or further study?
- ✓ **Think about time and budget.** A longer program costs more in total.
- ✓ **Check eligibility.** Each program has its own requirements.
- ✓ **Ask about recognition** of that exact program, not just the college.

---

## A Warning About Names

Some institutions use similar names ("nursing assistant", "paramedical", "nursing care") for courses that are **not** the same as recognised nursing programs. Always ask for the exact program name and its approval.
