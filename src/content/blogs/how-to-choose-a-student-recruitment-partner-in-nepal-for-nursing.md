# How to Choose a Student Recruitment Partner in Nepal: 10 Questions to Ask

Your partner shapes your admissions, your drop-out rate, and your reputation in Nepal. Ask these questions before you sign anything.

---

## The 10 Questions

### 1. Are you registered, and can you show proof?
Ask for registration details and verify them independently.

### 2. Do you charge students or families?
The answer should be a clear **no**. Fees charged to families lead to misleading promises and complaints.

### 3. How do you counsel students?
Look for honest guidance on cost, eligibility, and career outcomes, not pressure selling.

### 4. How do you verify documents?
Files should be checked **before** they reach you.

### 5. What are your commission terms?
Insist on a written structure with trigger, amount, timeline, and refund terms. See the [commission guide](/blog/flexible-commission-student-recruitment-nursing-colleges-india/).

### 6. Which nursing programs do you have experience with?
BSc Nursing, GNM, and Diploma in Nursing each need different counseling.

### 7. What is your realistic admission timeline?
Be wary of guarantees. See the [admission timeline](/blog/nepal-to-india-nursing-admission-timeline/).

### 8. How do you reduce drop-outs?
Ask about orientation, family communication, and follow-up after arrival.

### 9. Do you represent other colleges in the same program and city?
Understand how conflicts of interest are handled.

### 10. Can you provide references?
Other colleges can tell you what a brochure cannot.

---

## Red Flags

- ❌ Charging families for "admission guarantee" or "seat booking"
- ❌ Promising guaranteed admission or results
- ❌ No written agreement
- ❌ Unclear commission claims
- ❌ Refusing to share company details
