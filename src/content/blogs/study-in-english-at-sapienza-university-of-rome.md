# Study in English at Sapienza University of Rome: Bachelor, Master & PhD Guide

Want to **study in Italy in English**, in the capital? **Sapienza University of Rome**, founded in 1303, is the largest public university in Europe and offers a very wide range of degrees taught entirely in English.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It covers English-taught programs, costs and deadlines.

> **Heads up:** Dates, fees and programs change each cycle. The details below come from Sapienza's official international admissions page and 2026/27 calls. Confirm everything on the official Sapienza site before you apply.

---

## Why Study at Sapienza?

Sapienza describes itself as an international, public, comprehensive and multidisciplinary university with over 700 years of history. For 2026/27 it lists:

- **59 Bachelor's and Master's programs taught entirely in English**
- **240+ programs in Italian**
- **A Foundation Year** designed for international students
- **Low public-university tuition** of **€300 to €1,500 per year**, depending on your country of residence

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Degrees at Sapienza

Sapienza's 2026 English-programs brochure lists **nine three-year bachelor's degrees**:

- **Applied Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence**
- **Bioinformatics**
- **Business Sciences**
- **Classics**
- **Economics and Finance**
- **Global Humanities**
- **Molecular Biology, Medicinal Chemistry and Computer Science for Pharmaceutical Applications**
- **Nursing**
- **Sustainable Building Engineering** (Rieti campus, with an entrance exam)

Bachelor's applicants need a high school diploma after at least **12 years of schooling**. Some programs require an in-person admission test.

---

## English-Taught Master's Degrees at Sapienza

The brochure lists around **50 master's-level programs in English** across these areas:

- **IT and computer science:** Artificial Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Electronics Engineering, Telecommunication Engineering
- **Engineering and technology:** Space and Astronautical Engineering, Energy Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Nanotechnology Engineering, Astrophysics and Cosmology, Physics
- **Business, law and social sciences:** Business Management, Economics, Finance and Insurance, European Studies, Health Economics
- **Medicine, psychology and life sciences:** Biochemistry, Cognitive Neuroscience, Genetics and Molecular Biology, plus **Medicine and Surgery** and **Dentistry** (6 years)
- **Arts and humanities:** Classical Archeology, English and Anglo-American Studies, Fashion Theory and Practices
- **Architecture and design:** Landscape Architecture, Product and Service Design, Architecture (Conservation)

Master's applicants need at least a **3-year bachelor's degree** in a relevant discipline. Some programs are restricted access with an entrance exam and a comparison of qualifications.

### English language proof

All applicants need at least **B2 (upper-intermediate)** English or Italian, depending on the program. Check Sapienza's language requirements document for accepted certificates.

## PhD Programs at Sapienza

For the **42nd cycle (2026/27)**, Sapienza's call announced **94 PhD programs**, published on 15 May 2026 with a deadline of **17 June 2026**. PhD programs last **three years**.

- **Scholarships:** Funding varies by program. Third-party guides report a 50% scholarship increase for research abroad of up to 12 months. Confirm details in the call.
- **Language:** Some programs teach in English, and many allow interviews in English or online. Check each program's page in the call.
- **Calls:** PhD calls are usually published between May and July.

---

## Sapienza Application Fees and Tuition

- **Pre-selection application fee:** **€30** per application. You can submit a **maximum of two applications** per intake, and **no fee waiver** is available.
- **Restricted-access programs:** Some calls add a separate fee, for example **€35** for certain restricted-access English programs. Check the specific call.
- **Foundation Year:** The 2026/27 call listed a **€200** non-refundable application fee.
- **Tuition:** **€300 to €1,500 per year** for Bachelor's and Master's programs, depending on your country of residence and income documentation.

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Read the admission requirements** and check that your qualification is accepted.
2. **Apply for pre-selection on MoveIn**, if available for your program (maximum 2 applications).
3. **Create an Infostud account**, Sapienza's student portal.
4. **Follow the program's call for applications** in the course catalogue.
5. **Register on Universitaly** for a student visa (non-EU).
6. **Enrol and pay your first tuition instalment** by the deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Sapienza offer programs in English?
Yes. Sapienza lists 59 Bachelor's and Master's programs taught entirely in English.

### How much is the Sapienza application fee?
The pre-selection fee is **€30 per application**, with up to two applications per intake and no waivers.

### How much is tuition at Sapienza?
Between **€300 and €1,500 per year** for Bachelor's and Master's programs, depending on your country of residence.

### When is the Sapienza deadline for non-EU students?
For 2026/27, pre-selection for visa applicants ran from 22 December 2025 to 15 May 2026. Dates for 2027/28 will be on Sapienza's site.

### Are Sapienza PhD programs funded?
Many positions include scholarships, but funding varies by program. Check the call.
