## The Hiring Process at a Glance

### Step 1: Define Your Requirements
Specify job title, number of positions, skills, experience, salary, accommodation, and contract length. In Slovenia, pay and conditions must meet legal and sector standards.

### Step 2: Choose a Reliable Recruitment Partner
Work with an agency that understands **Nepal's foreign employment procedures** and Slovenian requirements. See [How to Choose a Nepal Recruitment Partner for Slovenia](/blog/how-to-choose-a-nepal-recruitment-partner-for-slovenia/).

### Step 3: Sourcing and Screening
Your partner shortlists candidates, then verifies experience, documents, and skills.

### Step 4: Interviews and Selection
Interview online or in person. Trade tests can be arranged for technical roles.

### Step 5: Permits and Documentation
Non-EU workers generally need a **single permit** (combined residence and work permit), plus employment contracts, medicals, and clearances. Use our [documents checklist](/blog/documents-checklist-hiring-nepali-workers-slovenia/).

### Step 6: Visa, Travel and Arrival
Visa processing, travel arrangements, arrival coordination, registration, and onboarding.

> **Pro tip:** Most delays happen at the permit and documentation stage. A partner that prepares paperwork on both sides saves weeks. See the full [recruitment timeline](/blog/nepal-to-slovenia-recruitment-timeline/).

## Common Challenges (and How to Avoid Them)

- **Unverified agents** who promise speed but deliver poor candidates
- **Incomplete or inconsistent documents** that stall permit processing
- **Underestimating Slovenian compliance rules** on pay, housing, and contracts
- **Mismatched expectations** about duties, language, or working conditions
- **No after-arrival support** when issues arise
