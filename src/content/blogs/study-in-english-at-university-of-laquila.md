# Study in English at the University of L'Aquila: Master's & PhD Guide

Looking for an affordable way to **study in Italy in English**? The **University of L'Aquila (UnivAQ)** is a public university in the Abruzzo region with a focused set of **international master's programs** in science, engineering and mathematics.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It covers programs, costs and deadlines.

> **Heads up:** Dates, fees and programs change each cycle. The details below come from UnivAQ's official pages for **2026/27**, plus a few third-party figures that are flagged. Confirm everything on univaq.it before applying.

---

## Why Study at the University of L'Aquila?

UnivAQ lists **14 international degree courses for 2026/27**, all at master's level. They include joint degrees and double or multiple degrees offered with foreign universities, as well as degree courses delivered in English.

For international students, that means:

- **A focused English-taught master's offer** in engineering, computer science, mathematics, physics and neuroscience
- **Dedicated calls for students with a foreign bachelor's degree**
- **Support grants:** UnivAQ ran a call for **50 contributions to travel and installation costs** for foreign students enrolling in English-taught master's programs for 2026/27

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Degrees at the University of L'Aquila

UnivAQ's 2026/27 list of international degree courses contains **no bachelor's programs**. Bachelor's degrees at UnivAQ are taught in Italian, so English-taught study starts at master's level.

If you want a bachelor's degree here, browse the full degree catalogue on univaq.it and check the language and admission rules for each program.

## English-Taught Master's Degrees at the University of L'Aquila

The 2026/27 international list, all two-year (LM) programs, includes:

- **Applied Data Science**
- **Computer Science**
- **Computer and Systems Engineering**
- **Control Systems and Automation Engineering**
- **Telecommunications Engineering**
- **Mathematical Engineering**
- **Mathematical Modelling**
- **Mathematics**
- **Physics**
- **Atmospheric Science and Technology for Meteorology and Climate**
- **Neurosciences**
- **Structural and Construction Engineering**
- **Mechanical Engineering**
- **Business Administration, Economics and Finance**

### InterMaths network programs

Three programs belong to the **InterMaths network**: **InterMaths** (an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master), **MathMods** and **RealMaths**. They use a separate call, and some come with scholarships.

## PhD Programs at the University of L'Aquila

UnivAQ opened its **42nd-cycle (2026/27)** PhD call with a deadline of **24 August 2026 at 13:00 (Italian time)**. For example, the **PhD in Information and Communication Technology (ICT)** offered **11 fully funded scholarships**, and its call is available in English.

Language and funding differ by program. Check the call section for your PhD program.

---

## University of L'Aquila Fees and Support

- **Application fee:** The call summaries I reviewed did not state a general application fee. Check the specific call.
- **Yearly fees:** Third-party listings show about **€156 per year** for several master's programs, while joint programs such as MathMods (about €1,000), InterMaths (about €1,500) and RealMaths (about €4,500) cost more. Treat these as indicative.
- **Student housing:** UnivAQ ran a dedicated residence call for the InterMaths network for 2026/27 (deadline 27 July 2026).

| Call | 2026/27 cycle |
|---|---|
| **English-taught master's (Cohort 2026)** | 25 Feb 2026, 1:00 pm (Italian time) |
| **InterMaths network (Cohort 2026)** | 25 Feb 2026, 1:00 pm |
| **Travel and installation contributions** | 9 Jul 2026 |
| **PhD, 42nd cycle** | 24 Aug 2026, 13:00 |

Earlier master's calls closed on **2 March 2022**, **2 March 2023**, **29 February 2024** and **5 March 2025**. Expect the next master's call to close around **late February to early March 2027**, but confirm the exact date on the "Call for international students" page.

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Pick your master's program** from the international degree list.
2. **Check admission requirements** in the call, including the course description attachment for your bachelor's degree.
3. **Apply through the online application** linked from the call page before the deadline.
4. **Prepare English proof** (or the self-certification, if your earlier studies were in English).
5. **Apply for your visa and enrol** after admission, following UnivAQ's enrolment instructions for international students.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the University of L'Aquila offer programs in English?
Yes, at master's level. UnivAQ lists 14 international degree courses for 2026/27.

### Are there English-taught bachelor's degrees at UnivAQ?
Not on the 2026/27 international list. Bachelor's degrees are taught in Italian.

### When is the master's application deadline?
For 2026/27 the call closed on 25 February 2026. Previous years closed between 29 February and 5 March.

### Does UnivAQ offer scholarships?
It offered 50 contributions to travel and installation costs for English-taught master's students in 2026/27, plus InterMaths network scholarships. Check the current calls.
