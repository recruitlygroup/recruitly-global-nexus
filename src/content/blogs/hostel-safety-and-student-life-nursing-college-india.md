# Hostel, Safety and Student Life at Nursing Colleges in India

> **Parents and students: questions about safety? WhatsApp us: +977 974 3208282. Free guidance.**

Many nursing students are young and living away from home for the first time. A safe, supportive hostel makes a big difference to your studies and your family's peace of mind.

---

## Hostel Checklist

- ✓ **Supervision.** Is there a warden living on site?
- ✓ **Security.** Gates, guards, entry and exit rules, visitor policy
- ✓ **Rooms.** How many students per room? Cleanliness? Ventilation?
- ✓ **Food.** What is served? Is it suitable for you?
- ✓ **Medical help.** What happens if you fall sick?
- ✓ **Distance** from the college and hospital, and how you travel
- ✓ **Rules** about timings and leave

---

## Student Life Checklist

- ✓ **Orientation** for new students from Nepal
- ✓ **A named person** you and your family can call
- ✓ **Other Nepali students** on campus
- ✓ **Complaint process.** Who do you tell if something is wrong?
- ✓ **Language support** in the first months
- ✓ **Festivals and holidays.** Can you go home when needed?

---

## What Parents Can Do

- **Visit or video-call** the hostel before paying
- **Talk to current students** and their parents
- **Keep regular contact** after arrival
- **Know the nearest help** and emergency numbers
