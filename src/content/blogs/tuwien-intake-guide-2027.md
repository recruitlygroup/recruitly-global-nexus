# TU Wien Intake Guide 2027: Deadlines, Requirements and How to Apply

**TU Wien (Vienna University of Technology) has two intakes a year, winter and summer, and each has its own application window.** If you apply outside it, you lose that semester. This guide covers the dates, the language rules and the steps that matter most for international applicants.

> **Verify before you apply.** Dates below follow TU Wien's published cycle for non-EU/EEA applicants. TU Wien can update them, so confirm on tuwien.at before you submit.

---

## TU Wien Intakes at a Glance

| | Winter intake | Summer intake |
|---|---|---|
| **Semester starts** | 1 October | 1 March |
| **Non-EU application window** | 16 January – 15 July | 16 July – 15 January |
| **Personal enrolment with admission letter** | Until 31 October | Until 31 March |
| **Bachelor pre-registration (international certificate)** | By 5 September | By 5 February |

**Right now (November 2026):** the summer 2027 window for non-EU applicants is open and closes on **15 January 2027**. The winter 2027/28 window should open on **16 January 2027**.

---

## Summer Intake (Starts 1 March)

The summer semester suits you if you finish your degree or language exam in the autumn. Apply between 16 July and 15 January, receive your admission letter, then enrol in person by 31 March.

### Who should choose summer?

- Applicants who missed the winter window
- Master's applicants whose bachelor's degree finishes in the autumn
- Students who need extra time for a language exam

---

## Winter Intake (Starts 1 October)

Winter is the main intake for international students. Apply between 16 January and 15 July, then enrol with your admission letter by 31 October.

**Tip:** apply early in the window. Document certification and visa processing take longer than most applicants expect.

---

## Language Requirements: Bachelor vs Master's

- **Bachelor's programs:** usually taught in German, so plan for **German at C1** level.
- **Master's programs:** TU Wien has a strong English-taught master's offer. Each program sets its own English proof.

If you want to study in English, a master's degree is the most direct route. Always check the specific program page for the exact certificate and score.

---

## Eligibility, Documents and Costs

### What you need

- **Master's:** a relevant bachelor's degree, usually 180 ECTS or more
- **Language proof:** German or English, depending on the program
- **Documents:** transcripts, diploma and ID, certified and translated where required
- **Complete file:** a missing document can cost you the semester

### Tuition

Non-EU/EEA degree students pay **€726.72 per semester** from the first semester, plus the student union fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the TU Wien application deadline for non-EU students?
Winter intake: 16 January to 15 July. Summer intake: 16 July to 15 January.

### Can I study at TU Wien in English?
Mostly at master's level. Bachelor's programs are generally taught in German.

### How much is TU Wien tuition for non-EU students?
€726.72 per semester, plus the student union fee.

### Which intake should I choose?
Winter is the main intake. Choose summer if your documents or language exam will be ready later.
