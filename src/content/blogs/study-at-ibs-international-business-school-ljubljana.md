# Study at IBS International Business School Ljubljana

Looking for a **business degree in Slovenia** with an international focus? **IBS International Business School Ljubljana** offers a **Bachelor's in International Business** and a **Master's in International Business and Sustainable Development** in Slovenia's capital. This guide covers programme details, entry requirements, fees, and how **Recruitly Group** supports your application.

> **Read this first:** IBS lists both programmes as **part-time** study, with a language of instruction of **"Slovenian and English"**. Before applying from abroad, confirm with IBS which teaching language and study format apply to your intake, and check how part-time or online study affects any **visa or residence permit** you need. IBS's FAQ covers visas and proof of funds.

---

## Why Choose IBS Ljubljana?

IBS is a **private higher education institution (higher professional school)** based in **Ljubljana**. Its programmes focus on **international business, management, and sustainable development**, with lectures held on-site or in a **hybrid format** that combines classroom and live online sessions.

**Key points:**

- **Two programmes:** a first-cycle Bachelor's and a second-cycle Master's
- **International business focus** with sustainability built into the Master's
- **Flexible formats:** on-site and hybrid (the Bachelor's also lists distance study)
- **Online application** through the national eVŠ portal

---

## Bachelor's: International Business

- **Type:** Professional higher education programme (first cycle)
- **Duration:** 3 years, **180 ECTS**
- **Practical training:** 400 hours (20 ECTS)
- **Final work:** Diploma thesis (10 ECTS)
- **Title:** Graduate in International Business, Bachelor of Science in Business (VS)
- **Topics:** Entrepreneurship, economics and law, marketing and finance, business English, international business, and sustainable development
- **Entry requirements:** Four- or five-year vocational secondary education with a final exam, or a general or vocational baccalaureate. If places are limited, selection is based on baccalaureate or final exam results (60%) and grades from the last two years of secondary school (40%).

---

## Master's: International Business and Sustainable Development

- **Type:** Second-cycle Master's programme
- **Duration:** 2 years, **120 ECTS**, including a **30 ECTS master's thesis**
- **Title:** Master in International Business and Sustainable Development (M.Sc.)
- **Topics:** International economics and business strategy, global marketing and market research, human resource management, financial management, sustainable development, and business ethics
- **Careers:** Professional and managerial roles in companies, public administration, and institutions involved in international cooperation
- **Entry requirements:** A first-cycle degree (at least 180 ECTS) in a relevant field (social sciences), a higher professional degree in a relevant field, or an equivalent degree in another field plus additional requirements set by the IBS Senate. If places are limited, selection is based on first-cycle average grade (60%) and thesis grade (40%).

## Tuition Fees (2026/2027)

According to IBS's published pricelist:

- **Bachelor's:** €4,200 per year, plus a **€300 enrolment fee** each academic year
- **Master's:** €4,200 per year, plus a **€300 enrolment fee** each academic year

Other costs, such as exam retakes, thesis fees, and re-enrolment, are listed separately. **The pricelist states that the school has no refund policy for tuition or enrolment fees**, so read the payment and refund terms carefully before paying, especially if you need a visa.
