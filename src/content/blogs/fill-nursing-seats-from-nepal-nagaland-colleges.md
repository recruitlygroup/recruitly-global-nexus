# How Nagaland Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Nagaland is an English-speaking hill state in the Northeast. For Nepali students looking for an English-medium environment in a hill setting, it can be an appealing, **under-the-radar option.**

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Nagaland

- **English widely used** in education and public life
- **Hill climate and setting** familiar to hill-region students
- **A close-knit, community-oriented environment**
- **Little competition** for visibility among Nepali families

## What Nagaland Institutions Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **Travel guidance and estimated costs** from Nepal
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and living expenses
- ✓ **Student safety and a named contact** for families

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by institution and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Long, costly travel** for most families
- **Low awareness** of the state in Nepal
