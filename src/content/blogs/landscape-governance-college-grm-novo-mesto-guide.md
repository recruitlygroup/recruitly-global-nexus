# Study Landscape Management at Grm Novo mesto, Slovenia

The **Landscape Governance College Grm Novo mesto** (Slovene name: *Visoka šola za upravljanje podeželja Grm Novo mesto*, short name LGC Grm) is a public higher education institution in Novo mesto, in southeastern Slovenia. It now appears on its website as UNM VUP, part of the new University of Novo mesto. It runs one study program: a **Bachelor's in Landscape Management**.

> **Read this first:** the college's website states that the **language of instruction is Slovenian**. The official 2026/27 call says the **part-time** program will also run in English, but **only if at least 10 candidates enrol** for English delivery. Check English availability with the college before you pay for anything.

All dates and fees below come from the college's own 2026/27 pages and the Slovenian national call. The 2027/28 call has not been published yet.

---

## Program snapshot

- **Program:** Landscape Management (*Upravljanje podeželja*), higher professional, first cycle
- **Length:** 3 years, 180 ECTS
- **Title on graduation:** Graduate Engineer of Landscape Management (Bachelor of Applied Science)
- **Places in 2026/27:** 150 full-time and 20 part-time
- **Location:** Ljubljanska cesta 28, Novo mesto
- **Master's:** none offered at the college in 2026/27

The degree is **interdisciplinary**. It combines business (management, marketing, economics) with natural science and environmental protection: agricultural and forest land, nature protection, regional development and rural tourism. Thesis topics can come from landscape, tourism, logistics, economy and energy. The college holds an **Erasmus+ charter**, so students can apply for internships abroad.

## Who can apply?

You can enrol if you hold any of these:

1. A general matura
2. A vocational matura
3. A final exam from any **four-year** secondary school program

If applications greatly exceed places, selection uses **60%** your overall result in the final exam or matura and **40%** your results in years 3 and 4 of secondary school.

Each candidate may submit **one application with one program choice** per period. Applications go through Slovenia's **eVŠ portal**. Your application is complete only when all documents are attached, and incomplete applications are rejected.

## What it costs

For foreign citizens, per the college:

- **Tuition (year 1):** €3,300
- **Study contribution:** €140
- **Administrative fee:** €69.97
- **Application fee:** none listed

If the college interviews you abroad or remotely, **you cover those costs**. The college describes full-time study as free and part-time as paid. Whether that exemption applies to your passport is a question to ask the college.

---

## Documents

Submit scans through eVŠ with your application. Your application also starts the **recognition of your foreign education**.

1. Certificate(s) of education, **without legalisation at first**. The college checks authenticity with the issuer.
2. If the college cannot confirm authenticity, the **original certificate, legalised** for your country:
   - **No legalisation:** Austria, Bulgaria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Czech Republic, France, Greece, Croatia, Hungary, Romania, North Macedonia, Serbia
   - **Apostille:** Hague Apostille Convention countries
   - **Full legalisation:** all other countries
3. A court-certified translation into Slovene or English, **if requested**
4. Proof of contents, duration and completed requirements of your education
5. A **short chronological description** of your education (a form is available)
6. A copy of your **ID card or passport** (EU citizens) or **passport** (non-EU citizens)
7. Anything extra the recognition authority requests

The recognition decision is issued within two months of a complete application.

## The mandatory interview

Slovenian higher education institutions must interview applicants from countries whose citizens need a visa to cross the EU's external border. The interview checks that you meet the program's admission requirements, takes place before further review, and a failed interview ends your application with **no appeal**. Check whether your passport is on the EU visa-required list.

## Guide by region

### Students already in the EU
- You apply through eVŠ with an ID card or passport.
- Certificates from the countries listed above need no legalisation.
- Ask the college how tuition applies to you, because the website describes full-time study as free.

### Nepal and Sri Lanka
- Expect the **€3,300** tuition and the interview if your passport is on the EU visa-required list.
- Check your country's status under the Hague Apostille Convention. If it is not a member, plan for full legalisation through your foreign ministry and the Slovenian embassy.
- Confirm English delivery before you apply.

### Indonesia and the Maldives
- The same non-EU rules apply: eVŠ application, recognition, interview if required, €3,300 tuition.
- Check your apostille or legalisation route early, because it can take weeks.

### GCC countries
- GCC passports are treated differently for the interview depending on the EU visa list, so check your own passport.
- Verify your certificate's apostille or legalisation route with your ministry of education and foreign ministry.
