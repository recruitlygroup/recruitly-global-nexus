# Study at Jožef Stefan International Postgraduate School (IPS) in Slovenia

If you want a **research-focused Master's or PhD in science and technology in Europe**, the **Jožef Stefan International Postgraduate School (IPS)** in **Ljubljana, Slovenia** is worth a close look. This guide covers its Master's and doctoral programmes, with a spotlight on **Ecotechnologies**, plus entry requirements and how **Recruitly Group** supports your application.

> **Important:** IPS is a **postgraduate-only** school. Its published study offer is **Master's (second cycle)** and **doctoral (third cycle)** programmes. We did not find a Bachelor's programme. This guide is for Bachelor's graduates applying to a Master's, and Master's graduates applying to a PhD. Confirm current details on the official IPS website before applying.

---

## Why Choose IPS in Ljubljana?

IPS describes an **international research environment**, **access to state-of-the-art research equipment**, **leading researchers as supervisors**, and **collaboration with industry**. Studies are linked to research and development projects in industry, services, and the public sector.

**Key reasons students consider IPS:**

- **Research-led** Master's and PhD study
- Programmes in **nanotechnology, ICT, ecotechnologies, and sensor technologies**
- Located in **Ljubljana**, Slovenia's capital
- Option of **reduced tuition** for Master's programmes (see the school's current call)

---

## Master's Programmes at IPS

IPS lists **three Master's programmes**, each lasting **2 years (4 semesters, 120 ECTS)**:

- **Nanosciences and Nanotechnologies**
- **Information and Communication Technologies**
- **Ecotechnologies**

### Master's entry requirements

- A first-cycle degree of at least **180 ECTS** in **natural sciences, technical disciplines, or computer science**
- **Proficiency in English**, shown by a language certificate
- Graduates from **other disciplines** may need to complete **10 to 60 ECTS** of additional obligations before enrolment
- Graduates with a **240 ECTS** degree in a relevant field may enter the **second year**, with 60 ECTS recognised and some additional exams
- Admitted candidates are approved by the IPS Senate under the annual call for enrolment

### Completing the Master's

Students usually need to complete all required and elective courses, typically have **at least one paper published or accepted** (or a patent or successful transfer to production), and **defend a master's thesis**.

## Doctoral Programmes at IPS (Spotlight: Ecotechnologies)

IPS offers **four doctoral programmes**: Ecotechnologies, Information and Communication Technologies, Nanosciences and Nanotechnologies, and Sensor Technologies.

### Ecotechnologies PhD at a glance

- **Credits:** 180 ECTS (3 years)
- **Structure:** Required courses (65 ECTS), elective courses (15 ECTS), individual research work (70 ECTS), and a doctoral dissertation (30 ECTS)
- **Focus:** Sustainable development, efficient use of raw materials and energy, waste minimisation and recycling, and transfer of ecotechnologies into industry and the public sector
- **Who it is intended for:** In principle, candidates with **at least two years of work experience** and the support of their employers
- **Electives include:** Environmental biotechnology, critical raw materials, environmental physics, biomonitoring, fuel cells, and artificial intelligence for science

## Tuition Fees

IPS's published pricelist (valid from March 2023) showed a **tuition fee of €4,500 per academic year**, with tuition exempt from VAT. This figure may be outdated, so **confirm the current fee** with IPS. The school also offers a **call for reduced tuition fees** for Master's students.
