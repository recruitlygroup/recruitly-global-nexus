# University of Maribor 2027/28 Admission Guide: Programs, Deadlines & How to Apply

The **University of Maribor (UM)** is Slovenia's second-largest university, with **17 faculties**, about **14,000 students**, and a growing set of **English-taught programs**. It is a practical choice for international students who want a European degree in a smaller, more affordable city than the capital.

> **Important:** The official **2027/28 call for enrolment has not been published yet**. Slovenian universities typically publish it in early February. This guide uses the **2026/27 cycle** as the best guide to what to expect, and clearly marks what is confirmed and what is a pattern.

---

## Why Study at the University of Maribor?

- **Scale and range:** founded in **1975**, with faculties covering engineering, economics, law, tourism, logistics, health, education, arts and natural sciences.
- **European network:** UM is part of the **ATHENA** European University alliance.
- **Affordable location:** Maribor is Slovenia's second city, and student costs are generally lower than in Ljubljana or Western Europe.
- **Honest context:** UM is listed in the **QS World University Rankings 2027 in the 901-950 band** and sits in the 1201+ band of Times Higher Education. Its appeal is **practical, affordable, career-focused study**, not top-tier global ranking.

## English-Taught Programs at the University of Maribor

Most UM programs are taught in **Slovene**, and English options are more common at Master's level. Based on published information for 2026/27:

### Bachelor's level (limited)
- **Logistics of Systems** (Faculty of Logistics, Celje), full-time in English. A **B2 English** certificate, English at the matura, or completed education in English is required.
- **Contemporary Tourism Practices** (Faculty of Tourism), an English-taught Bachelor's.
- **Part-time English options** in economics and business and in crisis management, according to third-party summaries.

### Master's level
- **Economic and Business Sciences** (Faculty of Economics and Business)
- **European Legal Studies** (Faculty of Law)
- **Tourism Destinations and Experiences** (Faculty of Tourism)

UM announced **54 Master's and 36 doctoral programs** for 2026/27, and the call states the **language of each program**. Always check the language of your chosen program in the official call.

## Application Windows: What We Know

All non-EU applicants apply electronically through Slovenia's **eVŠ portal**. You need a way to verify your identity electronically (such as an SI-PASS account). Here is the **2026/27 cycle**, which is the best pattern for 2027/28:

### Bachelor's (first year)
| Period | Dates (2026/27) |
| --- | --- |
| First application period | **18 Feb to 18 Mar 2026** |
| Second application period | **3 to 4 Sep 2026** |

There were **two deadlines in the first period**: one for non-EU candidates and one for non-EU candidates applying to **programs in English**. You may apply under one **or** the other, not both. You can list up to **three programs** in order of preference.

### Master's
| Period | Dates (2026/27), non-EU applicants |
| --- | --- |
| First application period | **31 Mar to 30 Apr 2026** |
| Second application period | **24 Aug to 4 Sep 2026** |

Some programs hold **online interviews** after the window closes, according to third-party summaries.

> **What to do now:** Expect the 2027/28 call in **early February 2027**. Use November to February to gather and prepare documents. Windows are short, and recognition and translation take time.

## Entry Requirements

### Bachelor's
- A **recognised secondary school qualification** (a national exit exam).
- A **Decision on recognition** of your foreign certificate. Recognition is carried out by UM and starts together with your application if you do not yet have the decision.
- **Language:** some programs require Slovene proficiency. For English-taught programs, English proof may be required (for example B2 for Logistics of Systems).
- **Aptitude tests:** some programs require a test of specific skills. In 2025/26, tests for the first period took place between late June and early July.
- Documents include your **passport**, **final school certificate**, **annual certificates**, a **court-certified translation** into English or Slovene, and a short **education description**. Depending on your country, certificates may need an **apostille** or other legalisation.

### Master's
- A **completed Bachelor's degree** in a relevant subject area, as specified for each program in the call.
- Program-specific requirements and any interview are set by each faculty.

## Costs

UM sets non-EU fees per faculty. Third-party summaries put **non-EU tuition for 2026/27** at roughly:

- **Bachelor's:** about **EUR 2,100 to 5,900** per year
- **Master's:** about **EUR 2,500 to 7,700** per year

Fees vary by program, so **confirm on the faculty page and in the official call**. Dormitory places are available through the university's student dormitories.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the University of Maribor application deadline for 2027/28?
Not yet published. Based on 2026/27, expect **Bachelor's windows around mid-February to mid-March 2027** and **Master's windows around April 2027**, with a second round in late summer. Confirm in the official call.

### Can I study in English at Maribor?
Yes, but options are limited at Bachelor's level and broader at Master's level. Check the language of each program in the call.

### Do I need to speak Slovene?
Not for English-taught programs. Some Slovene-taught programs require a Slovene language certificate.

### How many programs can I apply to?
Up to **three** in the first Bachelor's period, listed in order of preference.

### Is Maribor highly ranked?
It appears in the QS 2027 rankings in the 901-950 band. Its strengths are affordability and practical study options.
