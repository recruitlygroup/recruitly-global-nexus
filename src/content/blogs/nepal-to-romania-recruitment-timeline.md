# Nepal to Romania Recruitment Timeline: How Long Does Hiring Really Take?

Employers in Romania often ask, "How soon can my staff arrive?" There is no single number. Timelines depend on the role, number of workers, work authorization processing, visa appointments, and how complete your documents are. What you *can* do is understand each stage and plan around it.

---

## The 7 Stages of Recruitment

### Stage 1: Requirement Finalization
You confirm job titles, quantities, salary, benefits, accommodation, and contract length. **Clear requirements here prevent weeks of rework later.**

### Stage 2: Sourcing and Shortlisting
Your partner searches databases and networks, then screens CVs against your criteria.

### Stage 3: Interviews and Trade Tests
Interviews can be done online. Technical roles may need practical tests.

### Stage 4: Selection and Offer
You choose candidates, and offer letters and contracts are issued.

### Stage 5: Work Authorization Application
The employer-side application in Romania, alongside the clearances needed in Nepal.

### Stage 6: Visa, Medicals and Pre-Departure Processing
Long-stay visa application, health checks, document verification, and travel preparation.

### Stage 7: Travel, Arrival and Residence Permit
Flights, arrival coordination, residence permit steps, and onboarding at your site.

---

## What Slows Hiring Down

- **Vague job descriptions** that cause repeated shortlisting
- **Slow employer approvals** at the offer stage
- **Missing, inconsistent, or unlegalized documents**
- **Changing salary or terms** after selection
- **Visa appointment availability** and quota or peak-season congestion

---

## How to Hire Faster

- ✓ **Start 4 to 6 months before** your peak season where possible
