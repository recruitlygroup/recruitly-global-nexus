## Study in Italy Series: University of Milano-Bicocca

The University of Milano-Bicocca, based in Milan, welcomes around 2,200 international students every year and offers a growing list of degrees taught fully in English. This guide covers the programmes, application calls, deadlines, scholarships and the practical steps to apply.

**Important:** Dates below follow the 2026/27 admission cycle, which is the latest officially published schedule. Bicocca normally opens its first call in early November, so check the 2027/28 calendar on apply.unimib.it before you plan your timeline.

## Who This Guide Is For

Students from South Asia, including Nepal, India, Sri Lanka and the Maldives, students from the GCC countries, and students already living inside the European Union can all use this guide. The route differs depending on whether you are an EU citizen, a non-EU citizen living abroad, or a non-EU resident of Italy, so read the application section carefully.

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programmes

- **Artificial Intelligence (BSc):** a 3-year, 180 ECTS inter-university programme run with Milano Statale and Pavia, delivered in Milan.
- **Medicine and Surgery (single-cycle):** a 6-year, 360 ECTS programme taught entirely in English, with partners including the University of Bergamo and the University of Surrey.

### Bachelor's Deadlines and Calls (2026/27 pattern)

- **Call 1:** 7 November to 16 January
- **Call 2:** 16 March to 16 April
- **Calls 3 and 4** (May to September) were reserved for EU citizens and non-EU residents of Italy.
- **High school diploma and English certificate:** by 31 July
- **Universitaly pre-enrolment:** by 31 July

Limited-place programmes can follow their own windows and entrance requirements, so always read the individual call for your course.

## English-Taught Master's Programmes

Programmes include Data Science, Marine Sciences, International Economics, Materials Science and Nanotechnology, Artificial Intelligence Science and Technology, and Astrophysics and Space Physics. Availability changes by year, so confirm the current list on the Apply@Unimib website.

### Master's Deadlines and Calls (2026/27 pattern)

- **Call 1:** 7 November to 16 January
- **Call 2:** 16 March to 16 April
- **English certificate and Universitaly pre-enrolment:** by 31 July
- **Bachelor's degree completed:** by 31 August

Some Master's courses, such as International Economics, use open access with an evaluation of entry requirements, and each course call may set its own English-proficiency rules.

## Scholarships

- **Bicocca International Scholarship:** awarded to the top-ranked students in each English-taught Bachelor's, Master's and single-cycle programme. There is no separate application: applying to an eligible programme is enough. Competition is high, and applicants from the first call are prioritised. Confirm the current award value and conditions on the official Bicocca page.
- **Regional right-to-study grant (DSU):** a Lombardy Region financial support option for eligible students.
- **Targeted calls:** Bicocca also publishes special calls, for example for students at risk and refugee-corridor programmes, each with its own eligibility rules.

## Step-by-Step Application Path

1. Choose your programme and read its admission call carefully.
2. Create your profile and apply on Apply@Unimib during Call 1 or Call 2.
3. Prepare your academic documents, translations and English certificate.
4. Complete Universitaly pre-enrolment for the study visa.
5. Accept your offer and pay the required fees on time.
6. Apply for your Italian study visa and arrange accommodation in Milan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to speak Italian?** Not for English-taught degrees, but you must meet the English requirement set by each course call.

**Which call should I choose?** Non-EU applicants living abroad should focus on Call 1 and Call 2, because later calls were limited to EU citizens and non-EU residents of Italy.

**Is the scholarship guaranteed?** No. Only top-ranked applicants per programme receive it.
