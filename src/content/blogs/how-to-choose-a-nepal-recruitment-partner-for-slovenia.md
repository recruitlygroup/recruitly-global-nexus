# How to Choose a Nepal Recruitment Partner for Slovenia: 10 Questions to Ask

Your recruitment partner determines the quality of your workforce, your timeline, and your total cost. Ask these questions before you sign anything.

---

## The 10 Questions

### 1. Are you officially registered, and can you show proof?
Ask for registration and license details, and verify them independently.

### 2. What is your experience with Slovenian or EU employers?
Look for familiarity with EU-style permits, wage rules, and documentation standards.

### 3. How do you source and screen candidates?
Look for real screening, with interviews and skill checks, not just CV forwarding.

### 4. Do you conduct trade tests for technical roles?
Essential for chefs, technicians, drivers, machine operators, and tradespeople.

### 5. Can you give a written, itemized cost breakdown?
Avoid vague quotes and "we will see later" pricing.

### 6. Who handles documentation and approvals?
One partner managing both sides reduces errors.

### 7. What is your realistic timeline?
Be wary of unrealistic promises. See the [recruitment timeline](/blog/nepal-to-slovenia-recruitment-timeline/).

### 8. What happens if a worker does not fit or leaves early?
Ask about replacement and support policies in writing.

### 9. Will you support workers after arrival?
Ongoing coordination protects retention.

### 10. Can you provide references?
Past clients can tell you what a brochure cannot.

---

## Red Flags

- ❌ Guaranteed approvals, visas, or timelines
- ❌ Pressure to pay large amounts upfront without a clear agreement
- ❌ No written contract or quote
- ❌ Unwillingness to share company details
- ❌ Several unnamed sub-agents in the chain
