# Study at Alma Mater Europaea University, Slovenia

**Alma Mater Europaea University (AMEU)** is a private university based in Maribor, with study centers in other Slovenian cities. It offers Bachelor's, Master's and doctoral programs, and publishes a separate **price list for studies in English**. Its website highlights modern online delivery with recorded lectures, and states that it operates under the auspices of the European Academy of Sciences and Arts.

> **Note:** all figures come from AMEU's 2026/27 English price list, application deadlines and enrolment pages. The 2027/28 call is not published yet, and AMEU states that fees can change.

---

## What makes AMEU stand out

- **17 Bachelor's and Master's programs** appear on the English price list: 8 Bachelor's and 9 Master's.
- **Wide range of fields:** health, business, humanities, archives, web technologies, sustainable development and dance.
- **Online study** with recorded lectures, according to the university.

---

## English-taught Bachelor's programs

All are priced in the 2026/27 English price list:

- Business Management
- Nursing
- Physiotherapy
- **Social Gerontology**
- Web and Information Technologies
- Archival Studies
- Humanities
- Dance, Choreography

**Social Gerontology** is a three-year, 180 ECTS professional program accredited by NAKVIS. AMEU lists it as a **concession program** (with Nursing), which affects how you apply in eVŠ and which dates apply.

## What it costs (English delivery, per year)

| Program type | Tuition per year |
|---|---|
| Most Bachelor's programs (Business Management, Social Gerontology, Archival Studies, Humanities, Web and IT) | **€5,500** |
| Nursing (Bachelor's) | €7,700 |
| Physiotherapy (Bachelor's) | €8,000 |
| Dance, Choreography (Bachelor's) | €6,000 |
| Most Master's programs | **€6,600** |
| Health Sciences (Master's) | €6,900 |

**Other costs:**
- **Enrolment fee for foreign students:** €150 (covers student ID, the VIS system, a Microsoft 365 license, e-classroom, library membership, and the diploma and supplement)
- **Thesis with defense:** €2,000 (Bachelor's) and €3,000 (Master's)
- **Recognition of education (nostrification) fee:** €100
- **Repeating a year:** one third of tuition
- **Application fee:** none listed on these pages

Annual tuition covers 60 ECTS. Nursing and Physiotherapy have extra clinical-training and equipment costs. The Slovene-language price list is lower, so make sure you are looking at the English one.

## The Zoom interview

Applicants from countries on the EU visa-required list must take a mandatory **online interview** of about **30 minutes** on Zoom. For 2026/27 the first-period slots were 14 to 16 July, with **morning sessions (09:00 to 11:00 CET) for applicants in Asia**. You need a camera, microphone, stable internet and your passport or ID. Passing the interview does not guarantee enrolment.

## Documents

Submit scanned documents through eVŠ:

1. **Proof of secondary education.** AMEU gets this from Slovenian records, except for candidates with a final exam or secondary education abroad, who must upload it.
2. **Master's applicants** upload the diploma certificate (the Diploma Supplement is optional).
3. The **Form for the Description of Previous Education** from AMEU.
4. Recognition of your foreign education, started through the same application.
5. Check AMEU's international admission guide for authentication (apostille or legalisation), translations and English-language proof, because this guide could not confirm the 2026/27 rules for each.

## Is online study a problem for my visa?

AMEU describes online delivery with recorded lectures, and lists several study locations (Maribor, Koper, Ljubljana and remote for Social Gerontology). Whether the format you choose qualifies you for a Slovenian student residence permit is something to confirm with AMEU's International Office and the authorities **before you pay tuition**.

## Guide by region

### Students already in the EU
- Apply through eVŠ and confirm whether your program is a concession program, because the dates differ.
- Ask about tuition for EU citizens in your program, because AMEU states full-time concession study is free of charge for eligible students while the English price list is separate.

### Nepal and Sri Lanka
- Budget for English-delivery fees of €5,500 to €8,000 per year at Bachelor's level.
- Expect a Zoom interview if your passport is on the EU visa list, with Asian time-zone sessions.

### Indonesia and the Maldives
- The same fees, interview and document rules apply.
- Start apostille or legalisation checks early.

### GCC countries
- Confirm whether your passport requires the interview.
- Ask the International Office how your certificate must be authenticated.

## FAQ

### Is Alma Mater Europaea public or private?
Private, with two concession programs (Nursing and Social Gerontology).

### Is there an application fee?
None is listed. Foreign students pay a €150 enrolment fee.
