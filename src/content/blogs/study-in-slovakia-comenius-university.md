# Study in Slovakia: Comenius University for International Students

Many students from Nepal, Sri Lanka and Indonesia compare the same handful of European destinations. **Slovakia** is often left out, even though it offers EU degrees, English-taught options and a lower cost of living than much of Western Europe. This guide covers **Comenius University in Bratislava** and how to approach an application.

> **Verify before you apply.** Programs, tuition fees, deadlines and entry requirements change by year and faculty. Always confirm them on the official Comenius University website (uniba.sk) and the relevant faculty page.

---

## Why Comenius University?

- **Founded in 1919** and based in the capital, Bratislava
- **Slovakia's largest university**, with many faculties
- Part of the **EU and the Bologna degree system**, so degrees follow a structure European employers and universities recognise
- **Schengen area** location, convenient for travel and future study or work in Europe

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programs

Bachelor's programs in Slovakia typically last **three years**, with some fields (such as medicine) following longer integrated structures. Only **selected programs** are taught fully in English.

**What to check:**

- Which faculty offers the English-taught program
- Entrance exam or document-based admission
- Tuition fees for international students
- English language requirements

---

## English-Taught Master's Programs

Master's programs typically last **two years**. English-taught options appear across fields such as **informatics, natural sciences, management and humanities**, depending on the faculty and year.

### Who is this for?

- Bachelor's graduates from Nepal, Sri Lanka or Indonesia
- Students already in the EU, for example **Nepali students in Finland**, who want to change country, subject or cost level

Check whether your bachelor's degree matches the program's subject requirements.

---

## English-Taught PhD Programs

PhD study usually lasts **three to four years full-time** and centres on research with a supervisor.

### How to approach a PhD application

1. Find a faculty and research topic that fit your background
2. **Contact a potential supervisor first**
3. Prepare your CV, academic transcripts and a research proposal
4. Confirm funding, tuition and stipend rules directly with the faculty

---

## Documents You Will Typically Need

- Valid passport
- Previous diplomas and transcripts, **translated and legalised or apostilled** where required
- Proof of English proficiency
- CV and motivation letter
- Research proposal (PhD)

Document rules differ by country of origin, so check the requirements for Nepal, Sri Lanka or Indonesia early.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I study in English at Comenius University?

Yes, selected Bachelor's, Master's and PhD programs are offered in English. Availability varies by faculty and year.

### Is Comenius University good for international students?

It is Slovakia's largest university, located in Bratislava, within the EU and Bologna system.

### Can students living in Finland apply?

Students already living in Finland or another EU country can apply, but residence and permit rules depend on your situation, so check with official sources.
