# Cyprus Cleaning and Support Staff Shortage: How to Recruit Reliable, Compliant Workers from Nepal

## Executive Summary

Every hotel, office, restaurant, clinic and commercial building in Cyprus depends on one thing that rarely makes headlines: **a reliable cleaning and support team**. It is also where the labor market is under the most visible strain.

A Flash Eurobarometer survey of small and medium-sized businesses found that **cleaning and support services were the most common occupations for which Cypriot SMEs hired third-country workers, cited by 42 percent of respondents**. The same survey found that a majority of Cypriot SMEs that tried to recruit non-EU staff described the process as difficult, and that the main obstacle was the length and complexity of procedures.

For cleaning contractors, facility managers, hotel housekeeping heads and HR teams, the challenge is twofold: **find dependable workers, and hire them in a way that is fully compliant.**

**Recruitly Group** deploys skilled, pre-vetted non-EU workers from Nepal to European enterprises. This guide explains the demand, why Nepali candidates are a strong fit, and how a structured process protects employers from the compliance risks that have drawn regulators' attention in this sector.

## The Problem: Essential Work, Scarce Workers

Cleaning and support roles are easy to overlook until they go unfilled. Then the impact is immediate.

### What gaps look like in practice

- **Hotels and resorts:** Rooms are turned around late, public areas fall below standard, and guest reviews suffer.
- **Offices and commercial buildings:** Service contracts are missed and client relationships are put at risk.
- **Restaurants and food service:** Hygiene standards depend on consistent back-of-house cleaning.
- **Cleaning companies:** Contracts cannot be accepted or scaled without enough trained staff.

### Why the labor pool is not keeping up

- **A changing workforce:** According to the Cyprus Statistical Office, as reported in business press, Cypriot nationals made up 72.0 percent of those in employment in 2025, down from 80.1 percent in 2015. Much of the growth in employment since 2015 has come from EU and third-country nationals.
- **Persistent vacancies:** Recent analysis highlights elevated job vacancy rates in accommodation and food services and in administrative and support services.
- **Business reliance on non-EU hiring:** Employer organizations in Cyprus have described the employment of third-country nationals as an essential route for the private sector to keep growing.

### The hiring process itself is a barrier

In the same Eurobarometer data, **47 percent of Cypriot SMEs that tried to hire third-country nationals cited complex or lengthy recruitment and immigration procedures as the main obstacle**, compared with 31 percent across the EU. Cypriot SMEs also ranked immigration and relocation assistance as the most useful support service, at 38 percent compared with 18 percent at EU level. Only 17 percent said they were familiar with the support services available.

In short, the demand is clear and the process is the bottleneck. This is where a specialist partner adds value.

## Compliance Matters: Hire for the Role You Are Permitted to Fill

Employers in this sector should be aware of the regulatory focus on how cleaning permits are used. Cyprus press reports in early 2026 described cases in which third-country nationals were brought in on permits for cleaning duties and then assigned to other work, including on construction sites. The Labour Relations Department reportedly identified most violations as involving cleaning companies. Commentators and unions have warned that such practices harm workers and distort competition between businesses.

**What this means for employers:**

- **Workers must be employed in the role and sector for which the permit was approved.**
- **Employment terms must follow the applicable collective agreements and legislation** for the sector, such as tourism or food and beverage services.
- **Documentation must be accurate from the start.** Misrepresenting a role to speed approval creates legal and reputational risk for the employer.

Recruitly Group places workers into **genuine, clearly defined roles with transparent contract terms**. This protects the worker, protects your business, and keeps you on the right side of the Department of Labour.

**Important:** Procedures, quotas and sector rules change. Always confirm current requirements with the Cyprus Department of Labour and other competent authorities before submitting applications.
