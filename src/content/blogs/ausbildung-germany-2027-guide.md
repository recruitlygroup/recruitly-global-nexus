# Ausbildung in Germany 2027: Fields, Visa Rules and CV Basics

An **Ausbildung** is a paid dual vocational training program in Germany. You work at a company and study at a vocational school at the same time. Many employers are interviewing international candidates now for the **August/September 2027 intake**.

This guide covers the three things that decide your outcome: **which field to target**, **how the § 16a visa works**, and **how to write a German CV (Lebenslauf)**.

---

## Which Ausbildung Fields Are Hiring International Trainees?

### Healthcare and Nursing (Pflegefachmann/-frau)
Nursing has the **highest demand nationwide**. Year 1 stipends start at roughly **€1,190–€1,340 gross per month**.

### Hospitality and Gastronomy (Hotelfachmann/-frau, Koch/Köchin)
Employers in this sector tend to **interview quickly**, and many help with **staff housing**.

### IT and Technical Trades (Fachinformatiker, Mechatronics, Electronics)
Strong math and science grades plus **B2 German** give you a clear advantage.

### Logistics and Retail (Kaufmann/-frau für Spedition und Logistikdienstleistung)
A good fit if your background is in commerce or management.

## How the § 16a Ausbildung Visa Works

### German Language Requirement
- **B1 German** is the minimum for the visa (Goethe, telc or ÖSD certificates).
- Most **nursing and IT employers require B2** before they sign a contract.

### Is There an Age Limit?
With a **signed training contract, there is no upper age limit** for the § 16a visa. The under-35 limit applies only if you apply for a *training-seeker* visa without a contract.

### Financial Proof: Stipend or Blocked Account?
- **In-company training:** if your contract pays at least **€1,048 gross (about €822 net) per month**, the contract itself proves your livelihood.
- **School-based training:** the threshold is **€959 per month**.
- If your stipend is **below the threshold**, you may need a partial blocked account. The full amount is **€11,904**.

> **Note:** Visa thresholds and rules change. Confirm current figures with the German embassy or consulate responsible for your country before applying.

## 3 Rules for a German CV (Lebenslauf)

1. **Write it in German.** Do not send an English CV unless the employer asks for one.
2. **Keep it tabular and clean.** Use 1–2 pages in reverse-chronological order with a professional headshot. Skip colorful graphics, skill bars and "references available on request".
3. **Sign and date it.** Add your city, the date and a scanned or digital signature at the bottom.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I do an Ausbildung in Germany as an international student?
Yes. With a signed training contract and the required German level, you can apply for the § 16a visa.

### What German level do I need for an Ausbildung?
B1 is the visa minimum, but nursing and IT employers often expect B2.

### Do I need a blocked account for an Ausbildung visa?
Not if your in-company stipend is at least €1,048 gross per month. Below that, a partial blocked account may be required.
