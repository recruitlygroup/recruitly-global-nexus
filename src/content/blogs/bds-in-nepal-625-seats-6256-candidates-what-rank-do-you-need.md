# BDS in Nepal 2083: 625 Seats, 6,256 Qualified Candidates

BDS is the tightest of the three CEE programs. MEC published the BDS result on 19 November 2026: **6,256 candidates qualified** (cut-off 62.25) for **625 seats in 15 colleges**. That is about one seat for every ten qualified candidates.

## What the ranks look like

| BDS rank | Score at that rank | You are ahead of |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | 168 | 98.4% |
| 500 | 148 | 92.0% |
| 625 | 144 | 90.0% |
| 1,000 | 132 | 84.0% |
| 5,000 | 72 | 20.1% |

Rank 625 is the total seat count. That is the rough edge of realistic admission, before you factor in which college you want and whether you need a scholarship.

## Where the 625 seats are

- **Government (scholarship seats):** BPKIHS Dharan (50 seats) and IOM Maharajgunj (25 seats).
- **Private colleges (paying):** the other 13 colleges, including KUSMS, Kathmandu Medical College, Nepal Medical College, People's Dental College, Kantipur Dental College, Chitwan Medical College and others. The full list with seats is on our [BDS colleges in Nepal](/cee/bds-colleges-nepal) page.

## Fees

The reported MEC fee ceiling for Nepali paying BDS seats is about **NPR 20.92 lakh** in total. Confirm the current figure and each college's instalment schedule on the official MEC notice and with the college before you pay.

## If your rank is outside the seat count

- Check whether any scholarship quota applies to you. (BDS quota history is not published, so we cannot give quota cut-offs the way we can for MBBS.)
- Watch for later rounds when seats stay vacant. Nothing is guaranteed.
- Compare dental degrees in India. BDS there requires NEET-UG, and Nepali students must follow MEC's eligibility-letter process before enrolling abroad. See [study in India](/student-recruitment/india).
