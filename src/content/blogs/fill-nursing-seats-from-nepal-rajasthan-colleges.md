# How Rajasthan Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Rajasthan is India's largest state by area, with Jaipur as a major education and healthcare center. It is a Hindi-speaking state with good rail links, and colleges here can **reach Nepali families who value familiarity and affordability.**

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Rajasthan

- **Hindi-speaking environment** familiar to Nepali students
- **Jaipur and other cities** as established education centers
- **Strong rail links** with north India
- **A range of nursing programs** across urban and smaller towns

## What Rajasthan Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and clinical costs
- ✓ **Clinical training hospitals** and exposure
- ✓ **A named contact** and clear travel guidance

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Distance** from Nepal and long journeys
- **Hot summers** that families may ask about
