# Study at the University of Pavia in English (2027/28)

**Quick answer:** The University of Pavia offers **30+ programs taught in English** across Bachelor's and Master's levels. For 2027/28, the **first application window opens on 12 November 2026** and closes on 18 November 2026. The application fee is **€35**, and funding is available through a **first-year tuition fee waiver** and the **EDiSU regional scholarship**.

This guide is for students in **Nepal, Finland and other EU countries** who want to study in Italy in English.

> **Note:** Dates and rules come from the University of Pavia's official pages as of November 2026. Calls and funding change every year, so confirm on [apply.unipv.eu](https://apply.unipv.eu) before you pay any fee.

---

## Why choose the University of Pavia?

Pavia is a **public university in Lombardy**, close to Milan, with a history stretching back to **1361**. It serves around **26,000 students**, with roughly 1,500 international students arriving each year. The university runs a network of historic student colleges and a regional student-aid body (EDiSU) that supports housing and meals.

**Key facts**

- **Type:** Public university
- **English-taught programs:** 30+ (Bachelor's and Master's)
- **Application portal:** apply.unipv.eu (you can apply to up to 3 programs)
- **Application fee:** €35, mandatory and non-refundable

---

## Bachelor's programs taught in English

Most Bachelor's degrees at Pavia are taught in Italian, but a few run in English:

- **B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence** (restricted access, selective entry test)
- **BA in Social Sciences for Global Challenges**
- **Medicine and Surgery** (separate national English-language admission test and its own timeline)

### Eligibility for Bachelor's applicants

- A secondary school diploma after a minimum of **12 years of schooling** in total
- At least 2 years of attendance in the same program when you earned the qualification
- The academic eligibility exam, if your home education system requires it

If you have fewer than 12 years of schooling, Pavia points students to its **Foundation Year** program.

**Tip:** Non-EU applicants who need a visa should apply in the **first or second window**. Seats and visa processing time run out quickly.

## How to apply, step by step

1. **Pick up to 3 programs** and check whether each is restricted or non-restricted.
2. **Register** on apply.unipv.eu as a new applicant.
3. **Fill in your application** and upload your ID and your diploma (in English, Spanish or French, or officially translated into Italian).
4. **Pay the €35 application fee** before the deadline.
5. **Submit.** You can reopen and add documents only before the deadline, then resubmit.
6. **Interview (Master's):** non-restricted Master's programs usually require an interview.
7. **Admission letter,** then follow the "After admission" instructions.

### Documents for Nepali applicants

Pavia publishes a country-specific list of eligible diplomas for each country. Check the Nepal entry before you apply, and keep your transcripts, certificates and translations consistent and complete. Language requirements differ by program, so read each program's page for the accepted English certificates.

## Fees, tuition waiver and scholarships

Public Italian universities set tuition by income and program. For non-EU students, Pavia's fees are commonly listed from around **€400 per year**, and they can be higher depending on your situation. Confirm the figure for your program.

### First-year tuition fee waiver

Pavia has offered **tuition fee waivers for international students** enrolling in non-restricted programs, awarded **first come, first served** and valid for the **first year only**. Past calls gave a small number of places, and holders paid only a small administrative amount. The 2027/28 call has not been confirmed in the sources I could check, so watch the official funding page.

### EDiSU Pavia scholarship

EDiSU is the regional student-aid agency. Its scholarship has included:

- **Tuition exemption**
- **Housing** in a university college or residence
- **One free meal per day** at the canteens
- **A cash contribution**, based on need and merit

The scholarship is based on income and academic requirements, and you **must be admitted first**. The call is usually published in summer, with a 2026/27 deadline in mid-September, so plan your documents early. EDiSU winners lose the separate fee waiver, since tuition is already covered.

> **Reality check:** "Fully funded" is possible but **not guaranteed**. Prepare a budget and a backup plan.

## From admission to visa

1. **Receive your admission letter** from Pavia.
2. **Pre-enroll on Universitaly** (non-EU students living abroad).
3. **Collect your documents:** passport, admission letter, proof of financial means, accommodation, insurance. Requirements and amounts are set by Italian authorities and change, so check the current rules.
4. **Apply for a national student visa (type D)** through the Italian embassy or consulate that covers your country.
5. **After arrival:** apply for the residence permit, get your tax code (codice fiscale), and enroll.

**Students in Nepal:** processing channels and appointment systems change, so confirm which Italian mission and visa center cover Nepal before booking.

**Non-EU students living in Finland:** you generally apply through the Italian mission that covers your country of residence. Verify the current procedure.

**EU citizens:** no student visa is needed, but you complete enrollment steps and register your stay after arrival.

## FAQ
