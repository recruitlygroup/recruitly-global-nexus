# Georgia D3 Student Visa & Temporary Residence Permit Guide (2026/27): EQE Recognition, D3 Visa Steps & Residence Permit Rules

**TL;DR:** International students coming to Georgia for degree study typically follow a two-stage immigration journey: securing a **Category D3 Immigration (Study) Visa** prior to travel (where required by nationality) and then applying for a **Temporary Residence Permit** in Georgia after arrival. Before visa submission, your foreign academic credentials go through **National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement (EQE)** recognition and Ministry enrollment authorization, followed by university acceptance/enrollment documents and tuition payment. Once in Georgia, students apply for their **Temporary Residence Permit** through the **Public Service Development Agency (Public Service Hall / House of Justice)** before their legal stay expires.

## How the Georgia Student Immigration Pathway Works

Unlike countries where a single visa sticker covers your entire degree from day one, Georgia separates **entry/long-term stay authorization (Category D3 Visa)** from **in-country residency status (Temporary Residence Permit)**:

| Stage | Purpose | Where It Happens | Key Requirement | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **1. EQE Recognition & Enrollment** | Validates foreign school/university credentials and authorizes enrollment. | Georgia (via university / EQE) | Notarized & translated academic documents + fee payment. | 
| **2. Category D3 Study Visa** | Grants immigration entry to Georgia for higher education study. | Georgian diplomatic mission / consular portal / VFS (where visa is required) | University acceptance/enrollment documents, proof of funds, health insurance & accommodation. | 
| **3. Temporary Residence Permit** | Establishes legal residence in Georgia for your study period. | Public Service Hall (Public Service Development Agency) in Georgia | Valid legal stay in Georgia, university enrollment proof, proof of funds & photo. | 

## Step-by-Step: From Application to Residence Permit in Georgia

Start the process **2 to 4 months before your intake** (Spring or Fall/Summer depending on the program) so document translation, EQE recognition, and visa processing have plenty of buffer:

1. **Apply Online & Complete the Admissions Interview:** Submit your application and academic records to your chosen university, then complete the scheduled admissions interview to receive your offer/admission steps.

2. **Translate, Notarize & Submit for EQE Recognition:** Have your academic documents and passport copy properly translated and notarized, then submit for recognition with Georgia's **National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement (EQE)** (standard processing takes up to one month, with faster expedited options available for higher state fees).

3. **Ministry Enrollment Authorization & Tuition Payment:** Following recognition, the Ministry/enrollment process enables official university enrollment confirmation—pay your required tuition/program fees so your admission and enrollment documents are ready for visa filing.

4. **Apply for the Category D3 Study Visa:** Complete the online visa application (`geoconsul.gov.ge` / consular channel), book your appointment if required, and submit your passport, acceptance/enrollment proof, proof of sufficient funds, accommodation evidence, and health insurance.

5. **Travel to Georgia & File for Your Temporary Residence Permit:** After arriving in Georgia, submit your **Temporary Residence Permit** application at the **Public Service Hall** well ahead of your visa/legal stay deadline (commonly filing at least 40 calendar days before your legal stay expires), with standard processing tiers available at 30, 20, or 10 calendar days.

## Core Documents You Will Need

Keep both digital PDFs and organized physical copies ready, ensuring names and passport numbers match across every single file:

* **Passport & Biometric Photo:** Valid passport with sufficient validity plus ICAO/passport-standard photographs.

* **Academic Transcripts & Certificates:** Secondary school or higher education documents (translated and notarized as required).

* **University Admission / Enrollment Proof:** Offer/acceptance letter, EQE recognition / Ministry enrollment documentation, and tuition payment proof.

* **Proof of Financial Means:** Bank statements or financial evidence showing sufficient funds for tuition, living expenses, and stay duration.

* **Accommodation & Health Insurance:** Proof of housing in Georgia (such as a rental contract, host letter, or accommodation arrangement) plus valid health insurance.

## Key Rules & Compliance Tips for International Students

* **Stay Actively Enrolled:** A student residence permit is tied to your active enrollment at an authorized institution—dropping out, suspension, or prolonged absence can jeopardize your permit and renewal eligibility.

* **Check Recent Visa & Work Rules:** Immigration regulations in Georgia are updated periodically (including decrees taking effect in September 2026), and a student residence permit does not automatically grant unrestricted employment rights—always verify current work and permit rules before taking on any job or freelance work.

* **Budget for Fees & Living Costs:** Factor in application/consular fees, EQE recognition and residence permit state fees, health insurance, and living costs (whether budgeting independently or via an all-inclusive campus living package).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do all international students need a D3 visa before going to Georgia?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality and current residency status—many nationalities that require a visa for long-term study apply for a **Category D3 Study Visa** prior to travel, while certain passport/residence holders have specific visa-free or entry rules and still apply for a **Temporary Residence Permit** in Georgia for their studies.

### How long does EQE recognition and D3 visa processing take?

Regular EQE recognition of foreign education takes up to **one month** (with faster expedited options available for higher fees), and D3 visa decisions generally take up to **30 calendar days** once a complete application is submitted.

### When should I apply for my Temporary Residence Permit in Georgia?

Apply through the **Public Service Development Agency (Public Service Hall)** promptly after arrival and well before your legal stay expires (typically filing at least 40 calendar days prior to expiration).

### Can I stay in Georgia on a student residence permit after graduating?

No—a student residence permit is tied to your studies and renewal while enrolled, so graduating or ending your studies requires transitioning to another eligible legal status if you wish to remain.
