# How Meghalaya Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Meghalaya, with Shillong at its center, is known as an education destination in the Northeast, with a cool climate and an English-speaking environment. These are **real selling points** for Nepali families.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Meghalaya

- **English commonly used** in daily life and education
- **Cool, hill-station climate** familiar to hill-region students
- **Shillong as a regional education hub**
- **A diverse, welcoming student community**

## What Meghalaya Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and living costs
- ✓ **Clinical training exposure** and hospital partners
- ✓ **Clear travel guidance** and a named family contact

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Long travel** from most of Nepal
- **Unfamiliarity** of the state to many Nepali families
