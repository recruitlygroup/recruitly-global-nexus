# Study in the Philippines for Nepali Students (2026/27): MoEST NOC, MOFA & Philippine Embassy Attestation, Top Courses & Total Cost in NPR/USD

**TL;DR:** For students from Nepal, the Philippines offers **100% English-medium degrees**, transparent tuition without donation fees, and a straightforward immigration pathway. Because Nepal is **not a member of the Hague Apostille Convention**, Nepali applicants must authenticate their +2 / Bachelor’s transcripts and Police Clearance through **Nepal's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) in Tripureshwor** followed by **Embassy of the Philippines legalization in New Delhi** (or consular processing via the Philippine Consulate in Kathmandu). Nepali citizens enjoy **30-day visa-free entry** into the Philippines and convert on campus to a **9(f) Student Visa** through their CHED- and BI-accredited university—provided they first obtain a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from Nepal’s Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST) (`noc.moest.gov.np`) to legally remit tuition fees in USD via Nepali commercial banks.

## Why Nepali Students Are Choosing the Philippines in 2026/27

While Australia, the UK, Canada, and the USA have tightened student visa financial thresholds and admission caps, the Philippines has emerged as a high-ROI alternative for +2 and Bachelor’s graduates from Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar, and Butwal:

* **Zero Language Barrier:** Unlike Japan, South Korea, or non-English European tracks that require 6–12 months of language school, every Philippine university teaches, examines, and conducts hospital/industry internships **100% in English**.

* **No Bank Loan Complications or High Living Costs:** Annual tuition (\$1,500–$4,500 USD) plus monthly living costs (\$260–$550 USD) are **65% to 75% lower** than Western destinations, making self-funding realistic for Nepali families.

* **Global Career Stepping Stone:** Philippine Nursing (BSN), Medicine (MD), and ABET-accredited Engineering degrees are specifically structured for global licensing pathways (**NCLEX-RN for USA/Canada/Australia, UK NMC, USMLE, and GCC opportunities**).

* **30-Day Visa-Free Arrival for Nepali Passports:** Under Philippine immigration rules, Nepali passport holders do not need a pre-departure tourist visa sticker to enter the country—you arrive visa-free for 30 days and convert directly to a **9(f) Student Visa** through your university’s Bureau of Immigration liaison office.

## Step-by-Step Process for Nepali Students (Kathmandu to Manila/Cebu)

Follow this exact sequence to avoid bank remittance blocks at Tribhuvan International Airport or rejected documents in Manila:

### Step 1: University Application & Notice of Acceptance (NOA)

* Submit your **SEE (Grade 10) and NEB +2 (Grade 11 & 12) transcripts, character certificates, and passport bio-page** to a **CHED- and Bureau of Immigration (BI)-accredited university**.

* Once admitted, the university issues your official **Offer Letter / Notice of Acceptance (NOA)** and fee invoice.

### Step 2: Apply for Nepal MoEST No Objection Certificate (NOC) Online

* Under Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) foreign exchange rules, **no Nepali commercial bank can remit tuition fees in USD to the Philippines without a valid NOC**.

* Apply online via the official portal **`noc.moest.gov.np`**:

  * Upload your **Nepali Citizenship Certificate**, **Passport**, **SEE & +2 Academic Transcripts**, **University Offer/Acceptance Letter**, and **Fee Invoice**.

  * Pay the standard government fee of **NPR 2,000** via ConnectIPS, eSewa, or Khalti.

  * Verification typically takes **1 to 5 working days**. *(Note: Since 2026 NRB banking updates, all subsequent semester tuition remittances for the same course must be routed through the same Nepali bank used for your first payment).*

### Step 3: Document Authentication (MOFA Nepal + Embassy of the Philippines)

Because Nepal is **not a Hague Apostille member**, simple notarization is **not accepted** by the Philippine Bureau of Immigration. Your original documents must go through two official authentication stages:

1. **Department of Consular Services (MOFA), Tripureshwor, Kathmandu:** Attest your original **NEB +2 Transcript & Migration/Provisional Certificates**, **Birth Certificate** (issued by Ward Office in English), and **Nepal Police Clearance Certificate** (applied online via `opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np`).

2. **Embassy of the Philippines in New Delhi (accredited to Nepal) / Philippine Consulate in Kathmandu:** Submit the MOFA-attested documents for **Philippine consular legalization ("Red Ribbon" authentication)** so they are legally valid before the Bureau of Immigration in Manila/Cebu.

### Step 4: Flight Preparation, eTravel Pass & Arrival in the Philippines

* Register on the official Philippine government portal **`etravel.gov.ph`** within **72 hours before your flight** to generate your free eTravel QR code.

* Carry printed copies of your **Notice of Acceptance (NOA)**, **MoEST NOC**, **authenticated documents**, **confirmed return/onward ticket** (standard border check for 30-day visa-free entry), and **proof of funds** when departing Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) and clearing immigration in Manila or Cebu.

### Step 5: On-Campus Conversion to 9(f) Student Visa & ACR I-Card

* Within your first **7 to 15 days** on campus, your university’s International Student Office guides you through the **Bureau of Quarantine (BOQ) medical clearance** and files your **Conversion from 9(a) Visa-Free Stay to 9(f) Student Visa** (official BI conversion fee: **₱9,720** + **\$50 USD ACR I-Card**).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do Nepali students need a visa before flying to the Philippines?

No. Nepali passport holders qualify for **30-day visa-free entry** into the Philippines. You travel to Manila or Cebu with your Notice of Acceptance, MoEST NOC, and authenticated documents, and your university converts your 30-day stay into a **9(f) Student Visa** inside the Philippines.

### Is an IELTS or PTE score mandatory for Nepali students applying to the Philippines?

No. Most Philippine universities waive IELTS/PTE for Nepali students who completed their **SEE and +2 in English medium** (you simply provide an English Medium Instruction letter from your +2 college).

### Can Nepali students work part-time in the Philippines while studying?

No. A **9(f) Student Visa** strictly prohibits paid employment in the Philippines. Nepali students should plan their finances using family sponsorship rather than expecting part-time wages.

### How long does Philippine Embassy document legalization take from Nepal?

After completing **MOFA attestation in Tripureshwor** (1–2 working days), consular legalization through the **Embassy of the Philippines in New Delhi** (or authorized courier channel) typically takes **2 to 3 weeks**. Start document authentication at least **2.5 months before your intake**.

### Can I study BS Nursing in the Philippines and take the NCLEX-RN for the USA or Australia?

Yes. That is the #1 reason Nepali +2 Science students choose accredited Philippine nursing schools like Arellano University or SWU PHINMA. After graduating with your BSN and RLE clinical transcript, you can apply directly for **CGFNS verification and the NCLEX-RN exam** without needing a local Philippine nursing license.
