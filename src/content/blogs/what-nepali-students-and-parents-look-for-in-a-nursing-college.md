# What Nepali Students and Parents Look for in a Nursing College in India

Nepali families do not choose a nursing college on brochures alone. They compare, ask relatives, and look for **proof of safety, quality, and value.** Colleges that answer these questions clearly win more admissions.

---

## The 8 Things Families Check First

### 1. Recognition and Approval
Is the program approved by the relevant nursing council and affiliated with a recognised university or board? Families want proof they can verify.

### 2. Total Cost, Not Just Tuition
Hostel, mess, uniforms, clinical fees, exam fees, and caution deposit. Hidden costs destroy trust.

### 3. Hostel Safety and Supervision
Especially important for parents sending young women far from home. Mention warden presence, security, and visiting rules.

### 4. Clinical Training Exposure
Which hospitals do students train in? How many patients and departments? Hands-on experience matters.

### 5. Faculty and Results
Qualified teachers, pass rates, and student outcomes.

### 6. Career Pathways
What can graduates do next: further study, hospital jobs, or opportunities abroad? Be honest, never exaggerate.

### 7. Support for Nepali Students
Orientation, food preferences, festivals, and a named person families can call.

### 8. Reputation and Reviews
Families talk to past students and parents. Real testimonials carry weight.

---

## How Colleges Can Stand Out

- ✓ **Publish a clear, all-in fee sheet**
- ✓ **Show your approvals** prominently
- ✓ **Share hostel photos and safety measures**
- ✓ **List clinical tie-ups and hospital names**
- ✓ **Offer a parent call** with admissions staff
- ✓ **Share a few student stories**, with permission

---

## What Hurts Your Chances

- ❌ Vague or changing fees
- ❌ No proof of recognition
- ❌ Unanswered messages from families
- ❌ Overpromising on jobs or placements
