# Study at the University of Ljubljana School of Economics and Business (UL SEB)

The School of Economics and Business is part of the University of Ljubljana, Slovenia's capital-city university. It teaches a **Bachelor's in Business and Economics** and **ten full-time Master's programs in English**. This guide covers dates, costs and documents for international students.

> **Note:** all figures are from the **2026/27** cycle on the school's official pages. The 2027/28 application periods have not opened. Confirm dates before applying.

---

## What makes UL SEB stand out

The school holds three international business accreditations: **EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA**. The Bachelor's page also reports:

- **70%** of students are international
- **25** nationalities among foreign students
- **30** business projects per year
- **100+** students on exchange each year

---

## English-taught Bachelor's program

### University Degree Programme in Business and Economics

- **Title and length:** BA, 3 years (6 semesters), 180 ECTS
- **English specializations (year 2):** International Business, Marketing, Tourism
- **Double degrees:** Audencia Business School and Chonnam National University (International Business); KEDGE, Pforzheim University and Chonnam (Marketing)
- **Places for non-EU citizens:** 80 full-time and 20 part-time
- **English version cap:** 120 students in total

Students who finished secondary school abroad can choose the English version without restriction. All first-year students share the same core courses, including Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Management, Accounting and Statistics.

## English-taught Master's programs

Full-time, in English:

- Bank and Financial Management
- Business Informatics
- Economics with Data Science
- International Business
- Marketing
- Money and Finance
- Quantitative Finance and Actuarial Sciences
- Supply Chain and Logistics
- Sustainable Tourism Management
- IMB: International Master Programme in Business and Organisation

Part-time options in English: the ONLINE Master's Programme in Business and Organisation and Sport Management.

## Application windows (non-EU citizens, 2026/27 cycle)

| Level | First window | Second window |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's | 18 Feb – 18 Mar 2026 | 2 – 3 Sep 2026 (leftover places, for first-window applicants not accepted) |
| Master's (degree from abroad) | 1 Apr – 30 Jun 2026 | End of July – 28 Aug 2026 (places permitting) |

**For 2027/28:** the school expects the Bachelor's period to open in **mid-February 2027** and the Master's period in **spring 2027**.

Applications go through Slovenia's **eVŠ portal**, which also handles recognition of foreign education.

---

## What it costs (2026/27)

- **Bachelor's tuition:** €3,400 per year, full-time (the same for the English version)
- **Master's tuition:** €4,700 per year, full-time
- **Study contribution:** €61.60 per year
- **Application fee:** none listed on the school's pages

Non-EU applicants who did not finish secondary school in Slovenia pay tuition for full-time study.

---

## Mandatory interview

Slovenian law requires an interview for applicants from countries whose nationals need a visa to cross the EU's external border. It takes place before the application is processed further. The school describes it as in-person. Check the UL list of countries and the interview instructions for your nationality.

## Documents

### Master's (degree completed abroad)

1. Bachelor diploma or graduation certificate
2. Transcript of records
3. Official English or Slovene translation of both
4. Motivation letter (about 500 words)
5. Short chronological description of your education
6. English certificate: TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS 6, Cambridge or Oxford B2, or proof of an English-language exchange
7. Passport copy

### Bachelor's

- Proof of **B2 English** (for example a B2 exam certificate, or a secondary school certificate showing English or English-language instruction)
- General Matura or an equivalent secondary school certificate, assessed through recognition
- Recognition of foreign education, submitted with your eVŠ application
