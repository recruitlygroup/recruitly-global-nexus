# WU Vienna English-Taught Programs 2027: Bachelor, Master's, PhD, Deadlines and How to Apply

**WU Vienna (Vienna University of Economics and Business) is Austria's leading business university, and its English-taught offer is strongest at master's level.** This guide covers the programs, the 2027/28 deadlines, the requirements and the timing risk that catches many non-EU applicants.

> **Verify before you apply.** Programs and dates below come from WU's published pages as of November 2026. WU can change them, so confirm on wu.ac.at before you submit.

---

## 2027/28 Master's Deadlines: Rolling Admission

WU runs rolling admission for its English-taught master's programs, with three priority deadlines. Applications are batched and processed by deadline.

| Priority deadline | Date | Results expected |
|---|---|---|
| I | 8 October 2026 (closed) | Mid-December 2026 |
| II | 8 January 2027 | End of March 2027 |
| III | 8 March 2027 | Mid-June 2027 |

- The **MSc in International Management/CEMS** has only the first two deadlines, so its final deadline is **8 January 2027**.
- Programs start in the **winter semester**.

### Important for non-EU applicants

WU strongly advises non-EU/EEA students to apply by the first priority deadline. Residence permit procedures are complex, and applicants who apply later may not receive their permit in time to start. Deadline I has passed for the 2027/28 intake, so **apply by 8 January 2027 if you can.**

## English-Taught Bachelor at WU

WU's only English-taught bachelor is the **Bachelor's Program in Business and Economics (BBE)**. It starts in the winter semester only and has a selection procedure because demand exceeds places.

**How the 2026/27 cycle worked:**

- Online registration: 2 March – 19 May 2026, with a **€50 registration fee**
- Online self-assessment test (OSA) by late May
- Written entrance exam on 30 June 2026

WU had not published the 2027 BBE dates at the time of writing. Expect a similar spring timeline and check the BBE selection procedure page.

## English-Taught Master's Programs at WU

- **Digital Economy**
- **Economics**
- **Marketing**
- **Quantitative Finance**
- **Supply Chain Management**
- **International Management/CEMS**
- **Strategy, Innovation, and Management Control**
- **Socio-Ecological Economics and Policy**

All are two-year, full-time programs (120 ECTS) leading to an MSc (WU). WU limits you to **one English-taught master's program per application**. If you apply to several, you rank them and receive an offer for your highest-ranked program that accepts you.

## English-Taught PhD Programs at WU

- **PhD in Finance:** taught in English.
- **PhD in International Business Taxation:** a three-year, fully English-taught program.

WU's other doctoral programs are mainly German-taught, some with English tracks. Application periods vary by program, so contact the program before you apply.

## Requirements, Documents and Costs

- **Degree:** a relevant degree of at least 180 ECTS. Some programs, such as Digital Economy, also require exams in economics or business, and in information systems, computer science, mathematics or statistics.
- **Proof of aptitude:** GMAT or GRE, or academic letters of recommendation, depending on the program.
- **English proof:** TOEFL, IELTS or an equivalent accepted test.
- **Application documents:** CV in English and a motivation letter. Only complete applications are processed.
- **Transcripts:** you do not need your final degree certificate at application. A current transcript plus an official confirmation of the courses you still have to complete is enough.
- **Offer acceptance:** you have 10 days to accept an offer and pay the admission deposit.
- **Tuition:** non-EU students pay **€726.72 per semester**, plus the student union fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the next WU master's deadline for 2027/28?
Priority deadline II is 8 January 2027, and deadline III is 8 March 2027. Priority deadline I closed on 8 October 2026.

### Can I study at WU Vienna fully in English?
Yes. The Business and Economics bachelor, eight master's programs and two PhD programs are taught in English.

### Do I need the GMAT for WU?
It depends on the program. Many programs accept the GMAT, the GRE or letters of recommendation as proof of aptitude. Check your program's application guide.

### How much is WU tuition for non-EU students?
€726.72 per semester, plus the student union fee.
