# Cost of Hiring Nepali Workers in Romania: What to Budget For

"How much will it cost?" is the first question every employer asks. The honest answer: it depends on the role, number of workers, and the services included. Here is how to plan.

---

## Typical Cost Categories

### 1. Recruitment Service Fee
Covers sourcing, screening, interviews, and coordination.

### 2. Work Authorization and Government Costs
Work authorization, visa, residence permit, and administrative fees on the Romanian side, plus approvals required in Nepal.

### 3. Medical and Documentation
Health checks, police clearance, certificates, translations, and legalization as required.

### 4. Travel and Arrival
Air tickets, airport transfer, and initial transport to the workplace.

### 5. Employment Costs
Salary at or above legal and sector minimums, social contributions, accommodation, meals where agreed, and insurance.

> **Always verify** current fees and wage requirements with official Romanian sources, as they change.

---

## Where Employers Lose Money

- ❌ **Hidden charges** from multiple middlemen
- ❌ **Rejected applications** caused by incomplete paperwork
- ❌ **Early resignations** from poorly matched candidates
- ❌ **Compliance penalties** when pay, housing, or contracts fall short of the rules
- ❌ **Replacement costs** when workers do not fit the role

A cheaper agent often becomes the **most expensive** choice.

---

## How to Control Your Hiring Costs

- **Ask for a written cost breakdown** before committing
- **Choose one accountable partner** instead of several agents
- **Insist on pre-screening and trade tests**
- **Hire in batches** where possible to reduce per-worker cost
- **Plan early** to avoid urgent-hire premiums and visa delays
