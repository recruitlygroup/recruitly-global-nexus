# Commission Structure That Attracts the Best Student Recruiters in Nepal

A recruitment partner in Nepal represents many colleges. When a family asks "Which college should we choose?", the partner recommends the options that are **clear, trustworthy, and rewarding to work with.** Your commission terms are part of that decision.

---

## Why Commission Terms Affect Your Admissions

Partners invest time in counseling, document checks, and family follow-up. If the reward is unclear, late, or disputed, they will naturally prioritize colleges that treat them fairly. This is not about paying the most. It is about **being the easiest and most reliable partner to work with.**

---

## What Strong Recruiters Look For

- ✓ **A clear written agreement**
- ✓ **A defined trigger** (admission, first fee payment, or completion of a set period)
- ✓ **A fair amount** that reflects the effort of counseling and verification
- ✓ **On-time payment** with a stated timeline
- ✓ **Transparent drop-out terms**
- ✓ **Fast responses** from your admissions team
- ✓ **Recognition of repeat performance**, such as better terms for higher volume

---

## Commission Models Colleges Commonly Use

### Flat Amount Per Student
Simple to understand and budget.

### Percentage of Fees
Scales with program value.

### Tiered or Volume-Based
Higher rewards as admissions grow, which encourages long-term partnership.

### Hybrid Models
A base amount plus a bonus for hitting seat targets or intake deadlines.

> Terms differ by college and program. Choose a model you can sustain, and put it in writing.

---

## Mistakes That Lose Good Partners

- ❌ Changing terms mid-season
- ❌ Slow or unexplained payments
- ❌ Claiming the same student through multiple agents
- ❌ Ignoring emails and calls
- ❌ Vague drop-out rules
