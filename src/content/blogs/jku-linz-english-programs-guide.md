# JKU Linz English-Taught Programs 2027: Bachelor, Master's, PhD, Deadlines and How to Apply

**Johannes Kepler University Linz (JKU) offers English-taught degrees at bachelor, master's and PhD level, but the English offer is much deeper at master's and doctoral level than at bachelor level.** This guide lists the programs, the non-EU deadlines, the requirements and the mistakes that cost applicants a semester.

> **Verify before you apply.** Programs and dates below come from JKU's published pages as of November 2026. JKU can change them, so confirm on jku.at before you submit.

---

## Key Dates for Non-EU Applicants

| Level | Winter semester (starts 1 Oct) | Summer semester (starts 1 Mar) |
|---|---|---|
| **Bachelor** | Apply 6 Feb – 31 Mar | Apply 6 Sep – 31 Oct |
| **Master's** | General admission period until 31 Oct | General admission period until 31 Mar |

**What this means now (November 2026):** the summer 2027 non-EU bachelor window closed on 31 October 2026. The next bachelor window is for **winter 2027/28** and opens on **6 February 2027**.

For master's programs, JKU advises applying well before the deadline. Review takes months, especially for non-EU applicants, and a last-minute application cannot guarantee admission in that semester. Some programs set their own dates, for example the Computer Science master's lists 5 September (winter) and 5 February (summer).

## English-Taught Bachelor Programs at JKU

Most JKU bachelors are taught in German, but these run in English:

- **Artificial Intelligence** (English, level B2): JKU describes it as one of Europe's first AI degree programs.
- **International Business Administration:** taught entirely in English, with a mandatory semester or year at one of JKU's partner universities.
- **Chemistry and Chemical Technology**
- **Biological Chemistry:** a cross-border program delivered in Linz and Budweis, Czech Republic.

If a bachelor is taught in German, you must prove German proficiency, so check each program page.

---

## English-Taught Master's Programs at JKU

JKU is at its strongest for international students at master's level. English-taught options include:

- **Artificial Intelligence:** 4 semesters, 120 ECTS, English at B2.
- **Computer Science:** taught in English, with specializations such as Data Science and Networks and Security.
- **Global Business:** taught in English, studied at JKU and two partner universities.
- **Economic and Business Analytics**
- **Sustainability and Plastics Management**
- **General Management double degrees** with partner universities in France and Taiwan.

Entry usually requires a relevant bachelor's degree. Graduates of JKU's own bachelors often get direct admission, and external degrees are checked for equivalence.

## English-Taught PhD Programs at JKU

- **Engineering Sciences:** the doctoral route for computer science research, with a PhD taught in English (Dr.techn.).
- **PhD in Digital Transformation in Learning:** an English-language program run with IT:U in Linz.
- **PhD in Education:** a structured program at the Linz School of Education, in English or German.
- **LogiCS:** a joint doctoral program with TU Wien and TU Graz in logic and computer science.

**How PhD admission works:** you normally start with a research concept and contact the program or a supervisor first. Requirements and dates differ by program.

## Requirements, Documents and Costs

- **Language:** English proof for English-taught programs, usually B2 or higher. Check the exact level on the program page.
- **Documents:** secondary or university certificates, passport and language proof. Documents not in German or English need translation by a court-sworn translator after legalisation. Depending on your country, you need an apostille or embassy verification.
- **Competence check:** applicants to bachelor programs starting in summer 2027 or later with certificates from India, Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh must complete a competence check at VFS Global.
- **Tuition:** non-EU students pay **€726.72 per semester**, plus the Austrian student union fee.

### Important: you must apply yourself

JKU states that the application is submitted personally and is free of charge. Applications submitted by an agency or placement organisation are unlawful and will not be processed. Any service that offers to submit your JKU application for you is a red flag.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I study at JKU Linz fully in English?
Yes, in selected programs. Master's and PhD options are the broadest. At bachelor level, Artificial Intelligence, International Business Administration, Chemistry and Chemical Technology and Biological Chemistry are English-taught.

### What is the JKU deadline for non-EU bachelor applicants?
Winter semester: 6 February to 31 March. Summer semester: 6 September to 31 October.

### How much is JKU tuition for non-EU students?
€726.72 per semester, plus the student union fee.

### Can an agency apply to JKU for me?
No. JKU does not process applications submitted by agencies. You apply personally, and an advisor can help you prepare.
