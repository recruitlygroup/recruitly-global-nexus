# How to Choose a State in India for Nursing

> **Want advice on the best state for you? WhatsApp us: +977 974 3208282. Free for students.**

Many Nepali students go to the same few cities because friends or agents suggest them. A better way is to choose by **what matters to you.**

---

## Compare States on These Points

### Distance and Travel
Closer states mean cheaper trips and easier family visits. Far states cost more to reach.

### Language
In northern and eastern states, Hindi is widely used. In the south, local languages are used outside campus, though English is common in colleges.

### Climate
Hill-region students may prefer cooler places. Hot plains and humid coasts take adjustment.

### Cost of Living
Big cities cost more for rent, food, and transport.

### Clinical Exposure
Look for teaching hospitals, patient load, and variety of departments.

### Community and Safety
Having other Nepali students nearby helps, but check safety for yourself.

---

## A Simple Way to Decide

1. List your **top three priorities** (for example, budget, distance, safety)
2. Shortlist **two or three states** that fit
3. Compare **two or three recognised colleges** in each
4. **Verify recognition and registration** for every college. See [how to check](/blog/how-to-check-if-a-nursing-college-in-india-is-recognised/)

> No state is automatically "best." Quality varies between colleges in every state.
