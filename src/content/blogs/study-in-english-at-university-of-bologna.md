# Study in English at the University of Bologna: Bachelor, Master & PhD Guide

Want to **study in Italy in English**? The **University of Bologna (Unibo)**, founded in 1088, offers a large range of degrees taught in English at Bachelor, Master and PhD level.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It explains which English-taught programs exist, what applying can cost, and how deadlines work.

> **Heads up:** Unibo sets admission rules **program by program**. The details below come from official Unibo pages for the **2026/27 academic year** and may change for 2027/28. Always confirm on the Admission page of your chosen program.

---

## Why Study at the University of Bologna?

Unibo's own materials describe **more than 260 degree programs, over 90 of them taught in English**. For international students, that means:

- **A very wide choice of subjects**, from economics and political science to engineering, artificial intelligence, chemistry and the humanities
- **International programs** delivered entirely in English, some as double or joint degrees
- **A dedicated International Desk** for students enrolling from abroad

---

## English-Taught Bachelor Degrees at the University of Bologna

Unibo's 2026 Bachelor's virtual fair presented **15 Bachelor's programs taught in English**. Examples include:

- **Business and Economics** (restricted access)
- **Economics and Finance** (restricted access, Bologna)
- **Economics, Politics and Social Sciences** (restricted access, Bologna)
- **Economics of Tourism and Cities** (restricted access, Rimini)
- **European Studies** (open access, delivered entirely in English; Bologna and Forlì)
- **International Studies** (restricted access, Forlì)

### What does "restricted access" mean?

Restricted programs have a limited number of places and an **entrance exam or selection**. Open-access programs do not limit places, but you still need to meet the admission requirements and show English proficiency. Read each program's call for applications carefully.

## English-Taught Master's Degrees at the University of Bologna

The master's offer in English is much larger. Examples listed on Unibo and its department pages include:

- **Automation Engineering**
- **Electric Vehicle Engineering**
- **Economics and Econometrics**
- **Applied Economics and Markets**
- **Economics and Public Policy**
- **Health Economics and Management**
- **International Management**
- **Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge**

Unibo's governing bodies also approved new English-taught master's programs for 2026/27, including **Digital Minds**, **Materials Science and Batteries**, **Pharmaceutical and Industrial Biotechnology** and **Physics of the Earth's Interior, Ocean and Atmosphere**.

### English language proof

English-taught master's programs usually ask for English at about **B2 (CEFR)**. Unibo offers a free **Oxford Online Placement Test (OOPT)** for applicants of some programs who do not hold a certificate. Check your program's call for the accepted proof.

## English-Taught PhD Programs at the University of Bologna

For the **42nd cycle (academic year 2026/27)**, Unibo published its PhD call in **rounds**, with 3-year programs starting on **1 November 2026**. Many programs list **English** (alone or alongside Italian) as a working language, and several run the oral examination in English.

**Scholarships:** Official program tables show gross yearly scholarships that differ by program. Examples: about **€16,983** (Molecular and Cell Biology), **€18,341** (Chemistry) and **€19,701** (Health and Technologies). Some positions are funded by the University, others by departments or projects, and a few positions have no scholarship.

**Typical PhD application elements:**

- Degree documents and exam records
- A research proposal (for many programs)
- Proof of English proficiency
- An oral examination, in person or online

## University of Bologna Application Fees

Fees depend on the program:

- **Open-access programs:** Third-party guides report no general application fee. Confirm in your program's call.
- **Selection-based master's programs:** Some charge a **€50 registration fee** for the selection (for example International Management and Public Affairs). It is non-refundable.
- **Some Bachelor's programs with the CEnT-S test:** Registration through CISIA carries a **€55 fee**.
- **After admission:** The fixed enrolment fee is **€157.04** per year, plus a variable part based on the program and family income.

Always check the call for your specific program.

## University of Bologna Application Deadlines

There is **no single deadline**. Each program publishes its own call, with dates, places and selection rules.

What to expect:

| Step | What to know |
|---|---|
| **2027/28 calls** | Third-party guides expect them from around February 2027. Confirm on unibo.it. |
| **Master's programs** | Some programs use **three intakes**. See your program's call. |
| **Restricted Bachelor's** | An entrance test (such as CEnT-S) or selection comes first. |
| **PhD** | Calls run in rounds each cycle. Check the PhD call page. |
| **Universitaly pre-enrolment** | Required for non-EU applicants needing a visa. Unibo sets no general deadline, but embassy deadlines apply. |

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Choose your program** in the Unibo degree catalogue and filter by English.
2. **Open the "Admission" section** of the program website.
3. **Read the call for applications:** requirements, dates, fees and tests.
4. **Apply on Studenti Online** within the program's deadlines.
5. **Pre-enrol on Universitaly** if you need a student visa.
6. **Enrol after admission** and pay the first instalment by the deadline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the University of Bologna offer programs in English?
Yes. Unibo's materials list more than 90 degree programs in English, including 15 Bachelor's programs for 2026.

### Is there an application fee at the University of Bologna?
It depends on the program. Many open-access programs have no application fee, but some selection-based programs charge €50, and some test-based Bachelor's programs require a €55 test fee.

### When are the University of Bologna deadlines?
Each program sets its own. Calls for 2027/28 are expected to start from around February 2027. Check your program's Admission page.

### Can I do a PhD at Unibo in English?
Many PhD programs list English as a working language. Check the program table in the current PhD call.
