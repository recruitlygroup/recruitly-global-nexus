# Study in English at the University of Florence: Bachelor, Master & PhD Guide

Dreaming of **studying in Italy in English**, in the heart of Tuscany? The **University of Florence (UniFI)** is a large public university with roots going back to 1321, and it offers a growing set of degrees taught entirely in English.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It covers English-taught programs, application costs, tuition and deadlines.

> **Heads up:** Programs, fees and dates change every cycle. The details below reflect the **2026/27 academic year** and come from University of Florence documents and the Apply Unifi portal where possible, with some figures from third-party guides noted in the text. Confirm everything on the official UniFI pages before you apply.

---

## Why Study at the University of Florence?

UniFI has about **51,000 students**, 10 schools and 21 departments. Most degrees are taught in Italian, but a core group of programs run **fully in English**, and many other degrees offer individual English courses.

For international students, that means:

- **A well-established public university** in one of Italy's best-known cities
- **English-taught master's programs** in engineering, economics, finance, tourism, agriculture and computer science
- **Income-based tuition**, with fees for students living abroad set by their country's income group

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Degrees at the University of Florence

English options at bachelor level are limited. For 2026/27, the fully English-taught options are:

- **Sustainable Business for Societal Challenges**, a 3-year bachelor's degree in the School of Economics and Management
- **Medicine and Surgery**, a new 6-year single-cycle degree taught entirely in English from 2026/27, according to a third-party guide. Check the official admission rules for its entrance test.

Most other bachelor's programs are taught in Italian and require Italian proficiency (typically B2 for non-EU applicants abroad).

---

## English-Taught Master's Degrees at the University of Florence

The master's level offers the widest English choice. According to a third-party summary of the university's overview, the fully English-taught master's programs are:

- **Mechanical Engineering for Sustainability**
- **Management Engineering**
- **Geoengineering** (natural-risk mitigation)
- **Advanced Chemical Sciences & Technologies**
- **Design of Sustainable Tourism Systems**
- **Tropical and Subtropical Agriculture**
- **Economics and Development**
- **Finance and Risk Management**
- **Software: Science and Technology** (with IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca)

Apply Unifi also lists **Physical and Astrophysical Sciences** as English-taught for 2026/27. Several other programs are partly in English, such as Energy Engineering and Robotics, Automation and Electrical Engineering. Always check the teaching language on each program's Apply Unifi page.

### English language proof

English-taught programs usually ask for about **B2 (CEFR)**: for example Cambridge First, IELTS, TOEFL, or a bachelor's degree taught entirely in English. Some programs accept only recent certificates, so check the program page.

## English-Taught PhD Programs at the University of Florence

For the **42nd cycle (2026/27)**, the University of Florence published a general PhD call with a deadline of **11 June 2026 (noon, Italian time)**, and a separate **Pegaso** call funded by Regione Toscana with a deadline of **6 August 2026**. Programs last **3 years** and start on **1 November 2026**.

**Scholarship:** Third-party reports cite a gross scholarship of **about €16,243 per year**, with possible mobility top-ups. Confirm the exact figure and which positions have scholarships in the official call annexes.

**Language:** The language of each PhD program varies. Read the program table in the call to see where English is accepted.

---

## University of Florence Application Fee and Tuition

- **Master's application fee:** **€20**, paid on Apply Unifi when you apply (third-party guide citing the university).
- **Tuition for students living abroad (2026/27):** The university assigns a notional income level by your country's World Bank group. Third-party figures put the yearly total between **€536 and about €2,395**, taxes included, with higher-income country groups paying more.
- **Financial aid:** Students who win a DSU Toscana grant or are declared eligible pay only the stamp duty. Applications for the 2026/27 grant ran from 20 July to 7 September 2026.

Bachelor's and PhD application fees can differ. Check the specific call.

## University of Florence Application Deadlines

For master's programs, non-EU applicants living abroad could use two rounds in the 2026/27 cycle:

| Round | 2026/27 dates | Who can apply |
|---|---|---|
| **Round 1** | 10 Dec 2025 – 6 Feb 2026 | Everyone, including non-EU applicants abroad |
| **Round 2** | 16 Feb – 17 Apr 2026 | Everyone, including non-EU applicants abroad |
| **Round 3** | 15 Jun – 31 Aug 2026 | EU citizens and non-EU residents of Italy only |
| **Universitaly pre-enrolment** | By 31 Jul if admitted | Visa applicants |

**PhD (42nd cycle):** General call closed 11 June 2026; Pegaso call closed 6 August 2026.

For **2027/28**, expect a similar calendar. The 2026/27 rounds were announced in early December 2025, so watch the Apply Unifi news page from late November. If you need a visa, applying in the first round gives you a decision around mid-March.

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Choose your program** and confirm its teaching language on Apply Unifi.
2. **Check requirements**, including the Admission Document for open-admission master's programs.
3. **Prepare documents:** transcripts, degree, English certificate and, for enrolment, apostilled or legalised documents.
4. **Apply on Apply Unifi** and pay the application fee within the round dates.
5. **Pre-enrol on Universitaly** if you need a student visa.
6. **Apply for your visa**, then enrol after arrival.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the University of Florence have programs in English?
Yes. There are around 10 programs taught fully in English, mostly at master's level, plus one fully English bachelor's degree and a new English-taught Medicine and Surgery degree for 2026/27.

### How much is the University of Florence application fee?
For master's applicants, third-party guides citing the university report a **€20** fee on Apply Unifi. Check your program's call for other levels.

### When should I apply for a master's at UniFI?
Visa applicants should aim for the first round, which in 2026/27 ran from mid-December to early February.

### Is the University of Florence PhD funded?
Many positions come with scholarships of about €16,243 gross per year, but not every position does. Check the program table in the official call.
