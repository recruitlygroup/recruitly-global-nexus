# 6 Tuition-Free Public Universities in Germany for English-Taught Master's

Want a world-class German education without paying **€1,500–€6,000 per semester** in non-EU tuition fees? Here are **6 top-ranked German public universities with €0 tuition** for international students, plus how to apply to each.

## 1. RWTH Aachen University (North Rhine-Westphalia)

- **Best for:** Mechanical Engineering, Automotive, Data Science, Electrical Engineering
- **Tuition:** €0. Semester fee about **€320**, including a transit ticket.
- **How to apply:** Direct via **RWTHonline**. Most Master's programs do not need a uni-assist VPD, but the **GRE** is required for several engineering programs.

## 2. LMU Munich (Bavaria)

- **Best for:** Economics, Data Science, Biology, Physics, Psychology
- **Tuition:** €0. Unlike TU Munich, LMU does not charge non-EU tuition. Semester fee about **€85**, plus an optional Deutschlandticket.
- **How to apply:** Direct via the LMU International Office and the specific department portal.

## 3. TU Dresden (Saxony)

- **Best for:** Computer Science, Nanoelectronic Systems, Hydro Science, Computational Modeling
- **Tuition:** €0. Semester fee about **€300**. Dresden is also one of Germany's more affordable student cities.
- **How to apply:** Via **uni-assist** for non-EU degrees.

## 4. FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg (Bavaria)

- **Best for:** AI, Autonomy Technologies, Clean Energy Processes, International Business
- **Tuition:** €0. Semester fee about **€72**, plus a transit pass.
- **How to apply:** Direct via the **FAU campo** portal. Many programs have no application fee.

## 5. University of Bonn (North Rhine-Westphalia)

- **Best for:** Mathematics, Computer Science, Agricultural and Environmental Sciences, Economics
- **Tuition:** €0. Semester fee about **€330**, including nationwide local transit.
- **How to apply:** Direct via the University of Bonn online portal.

## 6. TU Berlin (Berlin)

- **Best for:** Computer Science, Urban Management, Industrial Engineering, Energy Engineering
- **Tuition:** €0. Semester fee about **€310**, including a Berlin and Germany transit pass.
- **How to apply:** Via **uni-assist**.

---

## Smart Shortlisting Tip

Don't apply only to the large technical universities. Pair **2–3 of these research universities** with **Universities of Applied Sciences (Hochschulen / HAWs)**. They offer smaller class sizes, mandatory industry internships, and often higher acceptance rates for practical fields.

If your application goes through uni-assist, remember it takes **4–6 weeks** to issue your VPD, so build that time into your plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these universities really free for non-EU students?
They charge **€0 tuition**, but you pay a semester fee that covers student services and often a transit ticket.

### Which German universities charge non-EU tuition?
Public universities in **Baden-Württemberg** and **TU Munich** charge non-EU students.

### Do I need uni-assist for every university?
No. Some of the universities above use their own portals. Check each program page.
