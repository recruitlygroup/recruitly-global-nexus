# Study at the University of Bergamo in English (2027/28)

**Quick answer:** The University of Bergamo (UniBg) offers **11 Master's programs taught in English**. Non-EU applicants must pass an **online pre-evaluation** first. For 2027/28, **Call One opens on 26 November 2026**. Bachelor's programs are mainly taught in Italian, so the English-language route is the Master's.

This guide is written for students in **Nepal, Finland and other EU countries** who want to study in Italy, and it covers admission, funding and the visa.

> **Note:** Dates and rules below come from the University of Bergamo's official pages as of November 2026. Universities update their calls, so always confirm on [en.unibg.it](https://en.unibg.it) before paying any fee.

---

## Why study at the University of Bergamo?

Bergamo is a **public university in Lombardy, Northern Italy**, founded in 1968, with roughly **21,000 students**. The city is close to Milan, which makes it attractive for internships and jobs. English-taught degrees sit mainly in economics, management, engineering, psychology and humanities.

**Key facts**

- **Type:** Public university
- **English-taught degrees:** 11 Master's programs
- **Application system:** DreamApply (online pre-evaluation)
- **Pre-evaluation fee:** €30 per program, up to 2 applications

## Bachelor's programs at Bergamo: what to know

If you searched for an **English-taught Bachelor's in Italy at Bergamo**, here is the honest answer: UniBg's undergraduate programs are **mainly taught in Italian**, and its English-taught degrees are at **Master's level**.

### What this means for you

- **Italian-taught Bachelor's:** non-EU applicants must show **B2 Italian** for open-access Bachelor's programs, and many courses require an entry test.

## Admission requirements: documents you must upload

Your application is **rejected if any of these five documents is missing**. Only colour digital copies of originals, in PDF, are accepted.

1. **Bachelor's degree** (translated into English or Italian)
2. **Official transcript** or Diploma Supplement
3. **English certificate** (minimum **B2**), or a Medium of Instruction certificate from your university
4. **Valid passport**
5. **Major Area form**, fully filled in for your chosen program

### Important for Nepali, Indian and Pakistani graduates

Bergamo checks your qualification before enrollment. For Bachelor's degrees from **India and Pakistan**, a general Bachelor's does not give access to a Master's, and an **Honours Bachelor** is required. For degrees from **Nepal**, eligibility is assessed in the pre-evaluation, so submit the Major Area form carefully and keep your transcripts clear.

### Declaration of Value or CIMEA

At the visa stage on **Universitaly**, a **Declaration of Value** or a **CIMEA Statement of Comparability** is compulsory. Without one of them, the university cannot validate your Universitaly application. Start this early.

## Scholarships and funding at Bergamo

Many students search for a **fully funded scholarship in Italy**. Be careful with that phrase: at Bergamo, funding is competitive and usually partial unless you combine several sources.

### Top International Student Grant

- **Value:** €8,000 gross per year (reported as up to €16,000 over two years)
- **Who:** EU and non-EU students entering the first year of a listed English-taught Master's
- **Selection:** based on your **GRE General Test** score, so the GRE is compulsory
- **Last call:** 6 grants, age 30 or under
- **Eligible programs in the last call:** Accounting, Governance and Sustainability; Clinical Psychology; Economics and Data Analysis; and others listed in the call

The university's page lists the grant for the academic years 2026/27 and 2027/28. **Confirm the exact call and deadline for your intake on the official page.** If you plan to apply, book your GRE early.

### Other funding to explore

- **University scholarships and accommodation:** handled by Bergamo's Student Aid Service (Servizio Diritto allo Studio)
- **Regional right-to-study aid (DSU):** Italian regions fund scholarships, housing and meals for eligible students; amounts and eligibility rules differ, so check the current call
- **Italian Government scholarships** for foreign citizens, listed on the Ministry of Foreign Affairs website

> **Reality check:** Do not rely on one scholarship. Plan your budget with a backup, and check the proof of funds the Italian authorities require for your visa.

## From admission to visa: step by step

1. **Check your profile.** Degree level, GPA, English score and program fit.
2. **Choose up to two Master's programs.**
3. **Register on DreamApply** and pay the €30 fee per program.
4. **Upload the five compulsory documents.**
5. **Attend the online interview** if invited.
6. **Receive your ranking result** by email.
7. **Complete pre-enrollment on Universitaly**, with your Declaration of Value or CIMEA document.
8. **Apply for the student visa** (see the next section).
9. **After arrival:** residence permit application, tax code (codice fiscale), and health coverage.

## Visa notes by country

### Students in Nepal

Non-EU students need a **national student visa (type D)** before travelling. Visa processing, appointment channels and required documents (financial means, accommodation, insurance) can change, so check the Italian embassy and its visa center for the current rules before booking.

### Students in Finland (non-EU nationals)

A Finnish residence permit does not replace an Italian study visa for stays over 90 days. Non-EU nationals living in Finland generally apply through the Italian mission covering their country of residence. Verify the current process before you apply.

### EU citizens

EU citizens do not need a student visa. You still complete **pre-evaluation or enrollment steps** and register your stay in Italy after arrival.

## FAQ

### Does the University of Bergamo teach Bachelor's programs in English?

Its English-taught degrees are at Master's level. Bachelor's programs are mainly in Italian, so non-EU applicants need Italian B2 for open-access Bachelor's courses.

### When does the 2027/28 application open?

Call One for free-access English Master's opens on **26 November 2026** and closes on **15 January 2027**. Call Two runs from **1 to 31 January 2027**.

### How many programs can I apply to?

Up to **two** pre-evaluation applications, with a **€30** fee for each.

### Is the Bergamo scholarship fully funded?

The Top International Student Grant is €8,000 gross per year and is based on your GRE score. It is not a full ride, so plan additional funding.

### Do I need Italian to study in English at Bergamo?

For English-taught Master's programs, you need **English at B2**, not an Italian certificate.

### Do Nepali students need a Declaration of Value?

A Declaration of Value or a CIMEA Statement of Comparability is compulsory at the Universitaly visa stage for non-EU students with foreign degrees.
