# How Jharkhand Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Jharkhand sits in eastern India, near Bihar and West Bengal. It is within reach of Nepal's southern and eastern regions and offers colleges a chance to **compete on value and clarity.**

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Jharkhand

- **Reasonable distance** from southern and eastern Nepal
- **Hindi-speaking environment**
- **Ranchi and other cities** as education and healthcare centers
- **Potentially lower living costs** than metro cities

## What Jharkhand Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and clinical costs
- ✓ **Clinical training hospitals** and exposure
- ✓ **A named contact** and orientation for Nepali students

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Competition** from neighboring Bihar and West Bengal
- **Trust and quality concerns** that proof of approval can address
