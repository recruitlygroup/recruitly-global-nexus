# Study at Kaunas University of Technology (KTU) in Lithuania

If you want a **technology-focused degree in Europe, taught in English**, **Kaunas University of Technology (KTU)** is one of the strongest options in Lithuania. This guide explains what KTU offers, how its Bachelor's and Master's programmes differ, and how **Recruitly Group** supports you from first enquiry to arrival.

> **Verify before applying:** Tuition, deadlines, English requirements, and programme lists change each intake. Confirm everything on KTU's official admissions pages.

---

## Why Choose Kaunas University of Technology?

KTU is a public university in **Kaunas**, founded in **1922**, and is widely regarded as Lithuania's leading technical university. It is best known for **engineering, informatics (IT), business, architecture, and natural sciences**. KTU states that it offers **more than 50 English-taught Bachelor's and Master's programmes** for international students.

**Why students choose KTU:**

- Strong focus on **technology, engineering, and IT**
- **50+ programmes taught in English**
- Applications made **online** through KTU's admissions platform
- **Merit-based scholarships and tuition fee waivers** for eligible applicants

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programmes at KTU

A **Bachelor's degree in English at KTU** suits students who have completed secondary school and want a practical, career-oriented international education.

### What to expect

- **Format:** Full-time study, roughly 3 to 4 years depending on the programme
- **Fields:** Engineering, informatics, business, architecture, humanities, and more
- **Typical requirements:** Secondary school diploma and proof of English (recent guidance pointed to around IELTS 5.5 for Bachelor's; confirm the current level)
- **Funding:** All international applicants are considered for merit-based scholarships for first-year students, according to KTU materials

### Who it suits

Students who want **hands-on, technology-driven study in English**. Older KTU materials listed Bachelor's tuition in roughly the **€4,000 to €6,000 per year** range, but fees are set per intake, so always check the current figure for your programme.

---

## English-Taught Master's Programmes at KTU

A **Master's degree in English at KTU** is designed for graduates who want to deepen their expertise or move into a new field.

### What to expect

- **Format:** Full-time study, commonly 1.5 to 2 years
- **Fields:** Informatics, business, architecture, sciences, public policy, and more
- **Typical requirements:** A Bachelor's degree with a solid average grade (some programmes list a minimum around 60%) and proof of English (recent guidance pointed to around IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 85, or CEFR C1; confirm the current level)
- **Funding:** International applicants may be considered for scholarships

### Who it suits

Graduates seeking **career growth, specialisation, or an international qualification** in a technology-driven university environment.

## Scholarships and Tuition Fee Waivers

KTU advertises **merit-based scholarships and tuition fee waivers** for eligible international applicants. Details, percentages, and rules change each year, so check the current call on KTU's admissions site.

**Tip:** Apply early and prepare strong academic records and a clear motivation.
