# Study at EMUNI in Slovenia: Master's Guide for Students from Nepal, Sri Lanka & the EU

If you hold a Bachelor's degree and want a **Master's in Europe taught in English**, the **Euro-Mediterranean University (EMUNI)** in **Koper, Slovenia** is worth a close look. This guide covers EMUNI's programmes, entry requirements, and costs, and explains how **Recruitly Group** supports applicants from **Nepal, Sri Lanka, and EU countries**.

> **Important:** EMUNI's published study offer is **postgraduate**: Master's and PhD programmes (plus executive programmes and summer schools). We did not find a Bachelor's programme on its website, so this guide is for **Bachelor's graduates** applying to a Master's. Always confirm current details on the official EMUNI pages before applying.

---

## Why Choose EMUNI in Slovenia?

EMUNI is based in **Koper**, on Slovenia's Adriatic coast, and has a **Euro-Mediterranean focus** in its programmes and research. Its Master's offer spans law, human rights, business communication, geopolitics, environment, and kinesiology.

**Key reasons students consider EMUNI:**

- **Master's programmes taught in English**
- Programmes that link **Europe and the Mediterranean region**
- **Blended learning** options in some programmes
- **Scholarships** awarded competitively based on profile and motivation

## EMUNI Master's Programmes (Taught in English)

EMUNI's Master's list currently includes:

- **Business and Digital Law (LL.M.)**
- **Digitalisation and Human Rights**
- **Intercultural Business Communication**
- **Mediterranean Geopolitics and Geoeconomics**
- **Ecoremediations and Sustainable Development**
- **Environment**
- **Kinesiology of Human Performance**

### Spotlight: Business and Digital Law (LL.M.)

- **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters), 120 ECTS
- **Mode:** Full-time, blended learning
- **Location:** Koper
- **Start:** October
- **Tuition:** €5,900 per academic year
- **Focus areas:** Artificial intelligence law, European data law, digital contracts, fintech and blockchain law, comparative corporate law
- **Careers:** Corporate or commercial legal advisor, data protection officer, compliance officer, digital technology law consultant, policy advisor

---

## Entry Requirements for Master's Study

Requirements vary by programme. For **Business and Digital Law**, EMUNI lists:

- A completed **first-cycle degree (180 ECTS, three-year Bachelor's)** in law, or an equivalent programme in another field
- **Bridging exams** if your Bachelor's is in a different field (Introduction to Law, Corporate Law, Contract Law, and International Business Law)
- **English at B2 level**, shown through a recognised certificate such as IELTS (about 5 to 6.5), TOEFL iBT (87 to 109), or Cambridge FCE, or through a Bachelor's taught in English
- **Recognition of your foreign qualification** under Slovenian rules, which allows admission to the first year

If places are limited, selection is based on your **previous GPA and Bachelor's thesis grade**.

---

## Tuition Fees and Scholarships

For 2026/2027, EMUNI's published Master's tuition ranges from about **€4,900 to €6,100 per year**, depending on the programme, plus an **€80 enrolment fee**. Tuition can be paid in full or in **two instalments** (65% at enrolment, 35% in February). The tuition page does not list separate fees for EU and non-EU students.

**Scholarships** can partly cover tuition, are awarded competitively, and funds are limited, so apply early.

---

## Visa and Residence Notes: Nepal, Sri Lanka & EU Students

- **Nepal and Sri Lanka (non-EU):** Students generally need a **temporary residence permit for studies**. EMUNI publishes guidance on this process. Start early, because document legalisation and embassy appointments can take time.
- **EU citizens:** You do not need a student visa, but you should check Slovenia's **registration requirements** for longer stays.

Rules change, so verify them with EMUNI and official Slovenian authorities.
