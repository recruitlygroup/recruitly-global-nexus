# BSc Nursing in Nepal vs India: What It Really Costs for Nepali Students

MEC published the BSc Nursing/Midwifery result on 21 November 2026. **10,805 candidates qualified** (cut-off 48) for **2,640 seats in 59 colleges**. If you did not get the rank you hoped for, or your family wants to compare costs, here is a fair comparison.

## Nepal

- **Government campuses** (Maharajgunj, Lalitpur, Biratnagar, Birgunj, Pokhara, Nepalgunj, NAMS and others) give about 75% of seats as scholarships, so the competition is on rank.
- **Private colleges:** the reported MEC fee ceiling for Nepali paying seats is about **NPR 10.36 lakh** for the programme. Hostel, books and other costs are separate, so ask each college for the full list.

See every college with seats on our [BSc Nursing colleges in Nepal](/cee/bsc-nursing-colleges-nepal) page.

## India: two Bangalore examples (RGUHS-affiliated)

These figures come from the colleges' own fee circulars that Recruitly Group holds. Both include food and accommodation.

| College | Year 1 | Years 2–4 | 4-year total (INR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bangalore College of Nursing, Chandapura (female) | ₹3.70 lakh | ₹1.65 lakh a year | ₹8.65 lakh |
| Bangalore College of Nursing (male) | ₹3.90 lakh | ₹1.75 lakh a year | ₹9.15 lakh |
| Gloria Group of Institutions, Electronic City | ₹2.75 lakh | ₹1.75 lakh a year | ₹8.00 lakh |

**Extra charges:** university fee ₹10,000, uniform ₹6,000, hostel deposit ₹10,000, and for Bangalore College of Nursing books at ₹5,000 a year.

## Converting to NPR

The Nepali rupee is pegged at NPR 1.60 per Indian rupee, so ₹8.00 lakh is about **NPR 12.8 lakh**, ₹8.65 lakh about **NPR 13.84 lakh** and ₹9.15 lakh about **NPR 14.64 lakh**. Add the extra charges above.

## How to compare fairly

- India's figure **includes food and accommodation**. Nepal's ceiling does not, so add hostel and living costs for the Nepal option.
- Nepal's scholarship seats cost far less, but depend on rank.
- India admits BSc Nursing on merit and does not require NEET.
- Fees change every year. Ask for the current fee circular in writing.
