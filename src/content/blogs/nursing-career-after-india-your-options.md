# Nursing Career After India: Your Options

> **Planning your career? Talk to us: +977 974 3208282. Free guidance for students.**

Nursing opens many paths. But your options depend on **your program, your recognition, and your registration.** Plan early.

---

## Possible Paths

### 1. Work in India
Opportunities may depend on registration rules for foreign nationals. Confirm what applies to you.

### 2. Work in Nepal
This usually needs registration with the Nepal Nursing Council. Read [what to verify](/blog/will-your-indian-nursing-degree-work-in-nepal-what-to-verify/) **before** you join a college.

### 3. Work Abroad
Many countries have their own licensing exams and requirements. Strong clinical training and English help, but every country has its own rules.

### 4. Further Study
A BSc can lead to a master's in nursing or specialisation. Diploma holders may have bridging options.

### 5. Teaching and Other Roles
Nursing education, community health, and administration can follow with experience and further study.

- ✓ **Choose a recognised program.** See [how to check](/blog/how-to-check-if-a-nursing-college-in-india-is-recognised/)

- ✓ **Research licensing rules** in the country where you want to work
