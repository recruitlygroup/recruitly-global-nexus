# How Goa Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Goa is India's smallest state by area, best known for tourism. A smaller number of institutions means **limited seats, but also a distinctive pitch**: a safe, coastal, English-friendly environment far from the crowded education hubs.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Goa

- **English widely spoken** in daily life
- **A well-known, welcoming destination**
- **A smaller, more personal campus environment**
- **Healthcare and hospitality exposure** in a tourism-driven economy

## What Goa Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **Honest living costs.** Families will compare Goa's expenses carefully
- ✓ **Hostel safety and supervision**
- ✓ **A named contact** and orientation for students far from home

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Distance and travel cost** from Nepal
- **Higher perceived living costs** for families on a tight budget
