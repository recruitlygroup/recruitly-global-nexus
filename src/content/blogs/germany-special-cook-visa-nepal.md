# Work in Germany as a Special Cook: Visa Guide for Nepali Chefs

Dreaming of a cooking career in Germany? The **Speciality Cook (Special Cook) visa** is a route for experienced cooks from Nepal who have a job offer from a German employer. This guide summarises the requirements published by the **German Embassy in Kathmandu** and explains where applicants most often run into trouble.

> **Important:** Embassy guidelines can change without prior notice. Always confirm the latest checklist on the official German Embassy Kathmandu website before you apply.

---

## What Is the Special Cook Visa for Germany?

It is a long-term national visa for cooks who will work in Germany under a contract with a German employer. The embassy forwards each application to the competent immigration authorities in Germany, which is why this process takes longer than many people expect.

**Key facts at a glance:**

- **Personal appearance** at the embassy is mandatory
- **Online appointment** is advised before applying
- **Visa fee:** EUR 75, paid in Nepali Rupees (the NPR amount varies with the exchange rate)
- **Fee is non-refundable** if the visa is rejected
- **Processing:** at least 6 to 8 weeks after document verification is complete

---

## Core Requirements for Nepali Applicants

### Six Years of Work Experience

The embassy asks for **proof of six years of work experience** as a cook. Prepare employer letters and any records that support your timeline.

### Chefs Association of Nepal Certificate

A **certificate from the Chefs Association of Nepal** is part of the required documents.

### Work Contract from a German Employer

You need a **work contract** confirming employment conditions in Germany, including **monthly salary and social insurance payments**. You also need the **Erklärung zum Beschäftigungsverhältnis** (employment declaration form) from the German Federal Employment Agency.

---

## Full Document Checklist

### Personal Documents

- Valid passport with **at least 12 months validity** after your return date and **two empty pages**
- **One recent biometric photo** (not older than six months)
- Application form completed through **VIDEX**
- Signed **Declaration on True and Complete Information**
- A4 copy of your passport data page
- **Citizenship certificate** and **birth certificate**
- **Motivation letter** and **CV (bio data sheet)**

### Professional Documents

- Information about your **current restaurant** (menu and pictures)
- Information about **all restaurants** where you have worked (menus and pictures)
- Chefs Association of Nepal certificate
- Proof of six years of work experience

### After Visa Approval

- **Health insurance**
- **Flight booking**

---

## How to Submit Your Application

1. **Book an online appointment** with the embassy.
2. Prepare **one application set plus one copy** of every document, and bring all **originals**.
3. **Do not staple or use paper clips.** Your file is scanned immediately.
4. Provide documents in **German or English**, or with a **certified translation**.
5. Pay the fee in NPR at application.

**Warning:** Incomplete documentation may lead to rejection. The embassy may request extra documents or an interview, and it reserves the right to forward fake documents to the police. Submitting everything does not guarantee approval.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Missing proof of the full six years of experience
- Menus and photos provided for only one restaurant
- A work contract without salary or social insurance details
- Passport with less than 12 months validity or without two blank pages
- Stapled documents or no spare copy set

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does the Germany Special Cook visa take?

The embassy states at least 6 to 8 more weeks after document verification, because your file goes to immigration authorities in Germany.

### How much is the visa fee?

EUR 75, paid in Nepali Rupees at the time of application.

### Do I need to attend the embassy in person?

Yes. Personal appearance is mandatory, and booking an online appointment beforehand is advised.

### When do I need health insurance and a flight booking?

Both are provided after the visa is approved.

### Can my visa be rejected even with all documents?

Yes. The embassy notes that submitting the documents does not guarantee that a visa is granted.
