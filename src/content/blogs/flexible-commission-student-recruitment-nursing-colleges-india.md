# Flexible Commission for Student Recruitment: Pay for Results, Not Promises

"What will it cost us?" is the first question every college asks. With the right model, the answer is simple: **you pay when students actually enroll.**

---

## How Commission-Based Recruitment Works

Instead of paying for advertising that may not convert, you agree on a commission with your recruitment partner. The partner earns when students are admitted and enrolled, which aligns incentives on both sides.

> **Important:** Terms vary by college and program. Always put the agreement in writing.

---

## What a Good Commission Agreement Covers

### 1. Trigger Point
When is commission earned: on admission, on fee payment, or on completion of a set period?

### 2. Structure
Flat amount per student, percentage of fees, or a tiered model that rewards volume.

### 3. Payment Timeline
Clear dates and conditions for payout.

### 4. Refund and Drop-Out Terms
What happens if a student withdraws early.

### 5. Program Scope
BSc Nursing, GNM, and Diploma in Nursing can carry different terms.

### 6. Student Fee Policy
Confirm in writing that families are **not charged** by the partner.

---

## Why Colleges Prefer Flexible Models

- ✓ **Lower upfront risk.** No large marketing spend with uncertain results
- ✓ **Scalable.** Terms can adjust by program, season, or volume
- ✓ **Aligned incentives.** The partner succeeds only when your seats fill
- ✓ **Predictable cost per admission**

---

## Where Colleges Lose Money

- ❌ **Vague terms** that lead to disputes
- ❌ **Agents who also charge families**, which creates reputation risk
- ❌ **High drop-out** without clear refund or replacement conditions
- ❌ **Paying several agents** for the same student
