## Why Employers Hire From Nepal

- **Overseas work experience** among many candidates
- **Scalable numbers** for large orders and projects
- **Flexible skill levels** from helpers to supervisors
- **Willingness to work shifts** and structured schedules

---

## What to Specify in Your Request

- ✓ Role and skill level (helper, skilled, supervisor)
- ✓ Number of workers and start date
- ✓ Contract length and shift pattern
- ✓ Salary, overtime, and accommodation terms
- ✓ Safety and equipment requirements

---

## Quality Controls That Protect Your Output

- **Trade tests** to confirm real skills, not just certificates
- **Experience verification** with past employers where possible
- **Medical fitness checks** before deployment
- **Pre-departure briefing** on workplace rules and safety
