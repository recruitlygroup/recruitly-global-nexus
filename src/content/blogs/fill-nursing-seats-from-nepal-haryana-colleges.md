# How Haryana Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Haryana surrounds much of Delhi and shares its connectivity. For Nepali families, that means **good transport links** and access to the wider Delhi NCR healthcare and education ecosystem.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Haryana

- **Close to Delhi NCR**, with strong rail, road, and air links
- **Hindi-speaking environment** familiar to Nepali students
- **Large healthcare sector** nearby for clinical exposure
- **A wide choice of institutions** and programs

## What Haryana Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and clinical costs
- ✓ **Hostel safety and supervision**
- ✓ **Easy travel directions** from Nepal and a named contact

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **High competition** among institutions in the NCR region
- **Families comparing many options**, so clarity and trust decide
