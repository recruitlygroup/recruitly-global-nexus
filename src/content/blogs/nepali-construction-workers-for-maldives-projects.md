# Hiring Nepali Construction and Skilled Workers for Maldives Projects

Island construction has tight schedules, remote logistics, and high quality expectations. A shortage of skilled labor can delay a whole project. Nepal is a proven source of tradespeople for overseas projects.

---

## Trades You Can Hire

- **Masons and bar benders**
- **Carpenters and formwork specialists**
- **Electricians**
- **Plumbers and pipe fitters**
- **Welders and fabricators**
- **Painters and finishers**
- **Tile and flooring installers**
- **AC and HVAC technicians**
- **Heavy equipment operators and drivers**
- **General laborers and helpers**

---

## Why Contractors Hire From Nepal

- **Overseas project experience** among many tradespeople
- **Scalable numbers** for large projects
- **Flexible skill levels** from helpers to foremen
- **Willingness to work on remote sites**

---

## What to Specify in Your Request

- ✓ Trade and skill level (helper, skilled, foreman)
- ✓ Number of workers and start date
- ✓ Project duration and contract length
- ✓ Salary, overtime, and accommodation terms
- ✓ Safety and equipment requirements

---

## Quality Controls That Protect Your Project

- **Trade tests** to confirm real skills, not just certificates
- **Experience verification** with past employers where possible
- **Medical fitness checks** before deployment
- **Pre-departure briefing** on site rules and safety
