# Recruitment Agency vs. Direct Hiring: What's Best for Slovenian Employers?

Some employers consider hiring directly from Nepal to save money. Others prefer an agency. Both can work, but they suit different situations.

---

## Direct Hiring: Pros and Cons

**Pros**
- Full control over candidate selection
- No agency service fee

**Cons**
- You must understand **Nepal's foreign employment rules** and approvals
- Heavy time cost for sourcing, screening, and paperwork
- Higher risk of document errors and permit delays
- No local support if problems occur
- Difficult to verify candidates remotely

---

## Using a Recruitment Agency: Pros and Cons

**Pros**
- Ready candidate pools and faster shortlisting
- Screening, trade tests, and document checks handled for you
- Support with compliance on both sides
- One point of accountability

**Cons**
- Service fee applies
- Quality varies, so **partner choice matters**

---

## Which Is Right for You?

| Your situation | Better option |
|---|---|
| Hiring 1 to 2 workers, with Nepal contacts and experience | Direct hiring may work |
| Hiring multiple workers or repeat batches | Agency |
| Technical or specialized roles needing testing | Agency |
| Tight deadlines | Agency |
| No experience with Nepal's process | Agency |
