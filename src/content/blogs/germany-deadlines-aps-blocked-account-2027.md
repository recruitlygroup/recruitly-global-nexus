# Study in Germany 2027: Application Deadlines, APS Certificate and Blocked Account Guide

Planning to apply to a German public university? Three milestones decide whether your application moves smoothly or gets delayed: **application deadlines**, the **APS certificate**, and the **blocked account (Sperrkonto)**. This guide covers each one for the Summer 2027 and Winter 2027/28 intakes.

---

## 1. German University Application Deadlines

### Summer Semester (starts April)
- **Standard deadline:** 15 January
- Some courses close earlier, in **December**

### Winter Semester (starts October)
- **Standard deadline:** 15 July

### Applying through Uni-Assist? Submit early
Uni-Assist verifies your documents before forwarding them to the university. **Submit 6–8 weeks before the deadline.**

- **Summer intake:** by late November
- **Winter intake:** by 31 May

> **Pro tip:** Submitting on the final day often means your verified documents reach the university too late.

---

## 2. APS Certificate: Who Needs It?

The APS certificate is a mandatory credential check for applicants from specific countries.

### Who must get it
- Applicants whose previous school or university degree is from **India, China or Vietnam**

### Who is exempt
- Purely **Nepali** board or university degrees are exempt from APS
- Individual universities may still require **Uni-Assist VPD evaluation**

### Timeline
- Processing takes **6–12 weeks** in peak season
- **Apply for APS before you finalize your deadline shortlist**

---

## 3. Blocked Account (Sperrkonto) Requirements

A blocked account proves you can fund your living costs in Germany.

- **2026/27 requirement:** **€11,904 per year** (**€992 per month**)
- **Buffer tip:** transfer an extra **€100–€150** above €11,904 to cover provider setup fees and international SWIFT charges

If even **€1** arrives short, your blocking confirmation (*Sperrbescheinigung*) will be delayed.

---

## Quick Checklist

1. Choose your intake: Summer 2027 or Winter 2027/28
2. Check whether your degree requires APS and start early
3. Send your Uni-Assist file 6–8 weeks before the deadline
4. Fund your blocked account with a buffer for fees

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is the deadline for the Summer 2027 semester in Germany?
Usually **15 January**, though some courses close in December.

### When is the winter semester deadline?
Usually **15 July**.

### Do Nepali students need an APS certificate?
Purely Nepali board or university degrees are exempt, but universities may still require Uni-Assist VPD evaluation.

### How much money is required for a German blocked account in 2026/27?
**€11,904 per year (€992 per month)**, plus a recommended €100–€150 buffer.
