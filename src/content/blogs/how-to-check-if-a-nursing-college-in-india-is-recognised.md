# How to Check if a Nursing College in India Is Recognised

> **Want us to help you verify a college? WhatsApp: +977 974 3208282. Free for students.**

Recognition is the most important thing to check. A college can look impressive and still lack the approvals you need. Do this **before** paying any fee.

---

## The Three Things to Verify

### 1. Indian Nursing Council (INC) Approval
Ask for the college's approval letter for the **specific program** (BSc, GNM, or other) and the **current academic year.**

### 2. State Nursing Council
Many states also have a state council that approves colleges and registers nurses. Ask for the state council's recognition.

### 3. University or Board Affiliation
The college should be affiliated with a recognised university or board. Ask which one, and confirm it with that university.

---

## How to Verify

- ✓ **Ask for documents in writing**, not just photos or verbal promises
- ✓ **Check the council's official website or contact the council** to confirm the college is listed
- ✓ **Confirm the exact program and year**, since approval can differ by program and can change
- ✓ **Contact the university** to confirm affiliation
- ✓ **Ask other students** from Nepal who are studying there

> Rules and approvals change. Confirm everything directly with the official bodies.

---

## Warning Signs

- ❌ The college refuses to show approval documents
- ❌ Approval is for a different program or old year
- ❌ The name of the university cannot be confirmed
- ❌ Pressure to pay quickly before you can check
