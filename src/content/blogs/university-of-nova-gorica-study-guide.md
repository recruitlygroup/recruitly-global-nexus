# Study at the University of Nova Gorica, Slovenia

The University of Nova Gorica (UNG) is a small public research university in western Slovenia, close to the Italian border. Unlike larger Slovenian universities, **UNG teaches many programs in Slovene**, so the first step is checking which ones offer English.

> **Note:** all figures are from UNG's **2026/27** pages and price list. The 2027/28 call is not published yet. Confirm details with the Student Office (studentska.pisarna@ung.si).

---

## What makes UNG stand out

Students of **Physics and Astrophysics** can take part in real research in **five laboratories and centers**: the Center for Astrophysics and Cosmology, the Center for Atmospheric Research, the Laboratory of Organic Matter Physics, the Materials Research Laboratory and the Laboratory for Quantum Optics. The program is research-oriented from the first year.

---

## Bachelor's programs and language of teaching

| Program | Language, per UNG |
|---|---|
| **Physics and Astrophysics** | Slovene and English (B2 English required) |
| Environment | Slovene only |
| Engineering and Management | Slovene only |

UNG also offers Cultural History, Language and Literature in the Digital World, Viticulture and Enology, and Digital Arts and Practices. This guide did not confirm their teaching language, so check before applying. UNG warns that not all programs have English courses.

**Places for non-EU citizens (first round, Physics and Astrophysics):** 3, plus 5 for Western Balkan citizens. The program page showed 0 places for other non-EU countries in the last round, so apply early.

---

## Master's programs and language of teaching

- **Physics and Astrophysics:** courses available in English (B2). Confirm with the School of Science.
- **Environment:** some courses available in English (B2).
- **Engineering and Management:** English only for part-time study as individual study.
- **Materials Science, Viticulture and Enology, Humanities Studies, Media Arts and Practices, Leadership in Open Education, EMMIR:** confirm the teaching language with UNG.

---

## Application rounds (non-EU citizens, 2026/27 cycle)

| Round | Bachelor's | Master's |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 18 Feb – 18 Mar | 18 Feb – 18 Mar |
| 2nd | 1 Apr – 1 Jul | 1 Apr – 1 Jun |
| 3rd | 1 Aug – 1 Sep | 1 Aug – 1 Sep |
| Final | 10 – 24 Sep (10 am) | 10 – 24 Sep |

**If too many candidates apply in one round, the next round stays closed.** Applications go through the Slovenian **eVŠ portal**. Master's applicants with foreign degrees need recognition of their education.

---

## What it costs

From UNG's price list (valid from 1 March 2026):

- **Physics and Astrophysics:** €3,000 per year (Bachelor's and Master's)
- **Environment Master's:** €3,000 per year
- **Engineering and Management:** €2,800 (Bachelor's) and €3,000 (Master's) per year
- **UNG fee:** €15 per year (e-services and library)
- **Interview fee:** €30 for candidates from visa-required countries, paid for each program applied to, non-refundable
- **Application fee:** none listed

Citizens of the EU and the Western Balkan countries do not pay tuition for full-time study in state-funded programs.

---

## Mandatory interview

Slovenian law requires an interview for applicants from countries whose nationals need a visa to cross the EU's external border. UNG sends instructions to the email address in your application.

---

## Documents (Bachelor's)

1. Certificate of education
2. Court-certified translation into Slovene or English, if UNG requests it
3. Report cards, transcripts or diploma supplement
4. Short chronological description of your education, signed
5. Motivation letter and CV

Candidates with a foreign certificate go through **recognition of education**. B2 English is the general minimum for English-taught courses.

## FAQ

### Is UNG fully English-taught?
No. Check each program. Physics and Astrophysics is the clearest English option for Bachelor's applicants.

### Is there an application fee?
UNG lists none. Visa-required nationalities pay a €30 interview fee.
