# Study at the University of Genoa in English (2027/28)

**Quick answer:** The University of Genoa (UniGe) offers roughly **15 degree courses taught in English**, mostly at **Master's level**, plus **PhD programs** with scholarship positions. Students with a foreign qualification must pass a **pre-evaluation on UniGeApply** before anything else. For 2027/28 the university says the opening is **expected in November 2026**.

This guide is for students in **Nepal, Finland and other EU countries** who want to study in Italy in English.

> **Note:** The official deadline page I could check covers the **2026/27** cycle. The 2027/28 dates below are marked as expected, based on last year. Confirm everything on [unige.it](https://unige.it) before you apply.

---

## Why choose the University of Genoa?

Genoa is a **public university on the Italian Riviera** with **130+ degree courses**, more than **36,000 students** and over **3,000 international students**. It is known for engineering, robotics, marine and maritime studies, and life sciences, and it works closely with research institutes in the city.

## Bachelor's programs taught in English

English-taught Bachelor's options are **very limited**. Most Bachelor's degrees are taught in Italian, and non-EU applicants need **B2 Italian** for open-access Italian-taught courses.

Programs reported in English:

- **Computer Engineering** (Imperia campus). UniGe lists it as requiring a mandatory pre-evaluation.
- **Maritime Science and Technology.** Check the course page to confirm the teaching language for your intake.

**Eligibility:** a secondary diploma after 12 years of education, and English at B2 (certificate or medium-of-instruction document).

## PhD programs at the University of Genoa

UniGe runs a **university-wide PhD call** each year covering many doctoral programs, with **scholarship positions** whose number and value vary by program.

- **Duration:** 3 years minimum
- **Selection:** evaluation of qualifications and an interview (online participation is often possible)
- **Open to:** EU and non-EU graduates, with some places reserved for **non-EU candidates living abroad**
- **Language:** depends on the program. Some are taught fully in English, such as the Biotechnologies in Translational Medicine program, whose recent call was in English.
- **Last call:** 42nd cycle, application deadline **9 July 2026**. The previous call's deadline was 9 July 2025.
- **Next call:** expected around **mid-2027** for the 43rd cycle, based on the pattern of earlier years. This is an estimate, not an announcement.

**How to prepare now:** you need a second-cycle degree (Master's or equivalent) accepted for PhD access, a research proposal or CV, and a program that matches your field. Start contacting potential supervisors and gathering recommendation letters well before the call opens.

### The process in four steps

1. **UniGeApply:** pre-evaluation of your qualification. It is mandatory for all UniGe courses if you hold a non-Italian qualification.
2. **Universitaly:** submit your visa request after a positive UniGeApply result. UniGe validates it and forwards it to the embassy if complete.
3. **Pre-enrollment** on UniGe Online Services, after your Universitaly request is approved.
4. **Enrollment** after you arrive in Italy, with your final documents uploaded online.

**Important:** UniGe cannot influence embassy appointment times, and admission does not guarantee a visa.

## Requirements

- **English B2** certificate recognized by UniGe, or a document showing English was the medium of instruction of your qualification
- **No Italian certificate** for English-taught courses
- **CIMEA Statement of Verification or Declaration of Value** for your qualification. Enrollment with reserve is possible if some of these documents are still missing, but you cannot take exams until the reserve is lifted.
- Passport data entered exactly as it appears on your passport
- **Documents:** PDF, JPG or JPEG files, up to 3 MB each

Some courses also ask for tests or extra verification, so read your course page.

## Tuition and the Aliseo scholarship

Tuition at UniGe is the sum of a **€16 stamp duty**, a **regional tax (minimum €120)** and a **university contribution** based on the family-income indicator (ISEEU) that you request after arrival. No tuition is due before you arrive.

**Aliseo scholarship:** UniGe states that students receiving an Aliseo scholarship pay **only €16 per year** in tuition. Aliseo is the regional right-to-study aid. Reports also link it to housing and meal support, but amounts and eligibility change each year, so check the current call.

> **Reality check:** Scholarships are competitive. Prepare a budget and a backup plan, and check the proof of funds required for your visa.

## Visa notes by country

- **Nepal:** Non-EU students need a national study visa. Check which Italian mission and visa center cover Nepal and book early.
- **Finland (non-EU nationals):** Apply through the Italian mission covering your country of residence, and verify the current process.
- **EU citizens:** No student visa, but you still complete pre-evaluation and enrollment steps and register your stay.

## FAQ

### When does UniGeApply open for 2027/28?

UniGe says the expected opening is **November 2026**. Last year it ran from 26 November to 20 March.

### Does Genoa offer Bachelor's programs in English?

Only a few, such as Computer Engineering at the Imperia campus. Most Bachelor's courses are in Italian.

### Do I need Italian to study at Genoa in English?

No, for English-taught courses. You need English at B2.

### Is the PhD funded?

UniGe's PhD calls include scholarship positions, but the number and value vary by program, so read each program's table in the call.

### When is the next PhD deadline?

The last call closed on 9 July 2026. The next is expected around mid-2027 but is not yet announced.

### How much will tuition cost?

It depends on your family-income indicator, with a €16 stamp duty and a minimum €120 regional tax, plus the university contribution. Aliseo recipients pay only €16.
