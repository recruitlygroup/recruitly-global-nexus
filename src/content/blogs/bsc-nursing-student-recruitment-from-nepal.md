# BSc Nursing Student Recruitment From Nepal

BSc Nursing is the flagship program for most Indian nursing colleges and the one Nepali families ask about most. Filling it with the right students protects your results, your accreditation standing, and your reputation.

---

## Who the Students Are

- Recent school graduates with a science background
- Families seeking an affordable, respected degree close to home
- Students with an interest in clinical careers, in Nepal or abroad

---

## What to Screen For

- ✓ **Academic eligibility** per current Indian Nursing Council and state council rules
- ✓ **Subject background** (typically science subjects)
- ✓ **Entrance and eligibility requirements** applicable to foreign or cross-border applicants
- ✓ **English communication** for classroom and clinical work
- ✓ **Genuine motivation** for a four-year program

> **Always verify** current eligibility and entrance rules with the Indian Nursing Council and your state nursing council.

---

## What Families Ask Before Choosing a College

- Is the college and program recognized?
- What is the total cost, including hostel and clinical fees?
- What are the clinical training and hospital tie-ups?
- Is the hostel safe and supervised?
- What happens after graduation?

Colleges that publish clear answers win more admissions.

---

## Tips for Better Results

- **Share complete fee and hostel details** upfront
- **Offer virtual counseling sessions** for families
- **Highlight clinical exposure and safety**
- **Provide a clear point of contact** for parents
