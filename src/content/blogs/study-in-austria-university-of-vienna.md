# Study in Austria: University of Vienna English-Taught Programmes Guide

The **University of Vienna** (Universität Wien) was founded in 1365 and is the oldest university in the German-speaking world. It is also the top-listed university in Austria in the QS-based Studyportals ranking of English-taught universities. But there is one thing every international applicant should know up front: **the University of Vienna teaches mostly in German at Bachelor's level, while its English offering is strongest at Master's level.**

This guide gives you an honest overview of English-taught options at Bachelor's, Master's and PhD level, plus costs, deadlines and how **Recruitly Group** can help.

---

## Why Study at the University of Vienna?

- **Historic and large**: founded in 1365, with 15 faculties and 5 research centres.
- **Affordable**: public university fees are low compared with the UK, US or Australia.
- **Wide English Master's range**: the university lists **38 Master's programmes taught completely in English**.
- **International network**: the university takes part in university alliances and partnership networks such as Circle U.
- **Vienna itself**: a capital city with strong public transport and a high quality of life.

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programmes: What to Expect

In general, the language of instruction at the University of Vienna is German. Many Bachelor's programmes also require German at a high level. The English-friendly options are limited:

- **Mathematical Foundations of Data Science**: listed by the university as taught in English (English B2 is an admission requirement).
- **Economics**: taught in German and English (German required, English B2 recommended).
- **International Legal Studies**: taught in German and English (German and English B2 are admission requirements).
- **Physics**: taught in German and English (German required, English B2 recommended).

> **If you are an international Bachelor's applicant:** compare this list with English-taught Bachelor's programmes at other Austrian universities, or consider a Master's pathway at Vienna after completing your Bachelor's elsewhere.

## PhD and Doctoral Studies

The University of Vienna runs doctoral schools in collaboration with its faculties and centres. As a general rule:

- You need a **relevant Master's degree**.
- In most cases you also need a **confirmed supervisor**.
- The language of supervision and coursework depends on the faculty and supervisor, so confirm this before applying.

Start by identifying researchers whose work matches your interests and contacting them with a clear research outline.

## Tuition Fees and Costs

- **Non-EU/EEA students**: EUR 726.72 tuition per semester from the first semester, plus the EUR 26.20 Students' Union (ÖH) fee for 2026/27, a total of **EUR 752.92 per semester**. Some exemptions exist, so check the university's fee page.
- **EU/EEA students**: tuition-free within the standard programme duration plus two tolerance semesters. After that, EUR 363.36 tuition plus the ÖH fee.

> **Tip:** Fees and exemptions change, so always confirm on the official University of Vienna website before you pay.

---

## Application Deadlines

For Master's programmes without a selection procedure, the University of Vienna's published deadlines for non-EU/EEA applicants with a degree from another university were:

- **Winter semester 2026/27:** application from 22 June to 3 August 2026, with admission through 31 October 2026.
- **Summer semester 2027:** the university lists a non-EU application window of 16 November 2026 to 7 January 2027 (confirm that this applies to your specific programme).

Programmes with a **selection procedure** or entrance exam run on separate, often earlier, timelines. Non-EU applicants should also start early because credential checks and residence permit steps take time.

## How to Apply: A Simple Checklist

1. **Shortlist programmes** and confirm the language of instruction.
2. **Prove your English level** (B2 for most English-taught Master's programmes).
3. **Check academic prerequisites**, since some programmes require specific Bachelor's backgrounds.
4. **Prepare documents**: degree certificates, transcripts, ID and any programme-specific materials.
5. **Check whether the programme has a selection procedure** and note the earlier deadline.
6. **Apply, then plan** your visa, accommodation and arrival.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the University of Vienna taught in English?
Mostly in German at Bachelor's level. At Master's level, the university lists 38 programmes taught completely in English.

### How much does it cost to study at the University of Vienna?
Non-EU/EEA students generally pay EUR 752.92 per semester in 2026/27 (EUR 726.72 tuition plus the Students' Union fee). EU/EEA students are tuition-free within the standard period plus two tolerance semesters.

### What English level do I need?
Most English-taught Master's programmes require English at B2 level. English and linguistics programmes at the Department of English require C1.

### Can I do a PhD in English at the University of Vienna?
It depends on the faculty and supervisor. You generally need a relevant Master's degree and, in most cases, a confirmed supervisor, so confirm the language of supervision directly.
