# Study Logistics at NOA Visoka Šola, Ljubljana

**NOA** (*NOA visoka šola*, formerly the College of Transport and Logistics) is a **private** higher education institution in Ljubljana. It teaches two accredited programs, a Bachelor's and a Master's, in **Slovenian or English**. Every graduate also receives an **ELA (European Logistics Association) certificate** alongside the diploma.

> **Read this first:** NOA describes its studies as taking place in an **online environment**, with lectures on weekday afternoons. Whether online or blended study qualifies international students for a Slovenian student residence permit is something to confirm with NOA and the Slovenian authorities **before you apply**.

All details come from NOA's own 2026/27 pages and price list. The 2027/28 call is not published yet.

---

## What makes NOA stand out

- **ELA certificate** with every degree, which NOA says helps graduates compete in international job markets
- **Flexible, online delivery** designed for working students
- **Two levels in one school**: a Bachelor's and a Master's in logistics and mobility
- **Mandatory work placement** in the Bachelor's (12 ECTS)

---

## English-taught Bachelor's program

### International Logistics Management (professional Bachelor's)

- **Length:** 3 years, 180 ECTS (EQF level 6/2)
- **Delivery:** online, weekday afternoons, in modular blocks of two or three subjects
- **Curriculum highlights:** Global Supply Chain Management, Circular Economy and Logistics, Digital Business in Logistics, Project Management, and case studies in logistics and transport
- **Academic year:** early October to end of June

**Entry requirements (Year 1):** general matura, vocational matura, the final examination of any four-year secondary school program, or the International Baccalaureate.

---

## English-taught Master's program

### Sustainable Mobility and Logistics

- **Length:** 2 years, 120 ECTS (EQF level 7/2)
- **Focus:** supply chain management, transport digitalisation, intelligent mobility, smart city technologies
- **Master's thesis:** 16 ECTS
- **Progression:** direct entry to doctoral studies

**Entry requirements (Year 1):** a completed first-cycle program of at least **180 ECTS** in a related field: business and administration, law, economics, information and communication technologies, security, or transport services. Graduates of other fields can enrol but must complete additional study obligations in *Fundamentals of Logistics and Sustainable Mobility* and *Business Economics*.

## Application windows (2026/27 cycle)

| Level | Window 1 | Window 2 | Window 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's | 18 Feb – 12 Jul 2026 | 13 Jul – 31 Aug 2026 | 1 – 30 Sep 2026, or until places are filled |
| Master's | 18 Feb – 12 Jul 2026 | 13 Jul – 30 Sep 2026, or until places are filled | None |

Applications go through Slovenia's **eVŠ portal**. NOA states that calls run from **mid-February to the end of September**. Enrolment is digital, and students receive access to the e-classroom in the second half of September before classes start in early October.

## What it costs (price list valid from 2026/27)

| Item | English delivery | Slovene delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's tuition (per year) | **€3,300** | €2,600 |
| Master's tuition (per year) | **€4,000** | €3,150 |
| Master's thesis or exam (billed at Year 2 enrolment) | **€700** | €500 |
| Application fee | None listed | None listed |

Tuition includes the enrolment fee (10% of tuition), teaching, three attempts at each exam, basic e-classroom materials and library membership. A **€100 discount** applies for paying in one instalment, and discounts do not stack. Extras include a €250 differential exam and a €200 recognition of non-formal education fee in English delivery.

## Documents for applicants with foreign qualifications

1. **Scanned certificate or diploma.** NOA verifies it with the issuer. If that is not possible, you must provide a legalised original.
   - **No legalisation:** Austria, Bulgaria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Czechia, France, Greece, Croatia, Hungary, Romania, North Macedonia, Serbia
   - **Apostille or authentication through a Slovenian diplomatic mission:** all other countries
2. **Sworn translation** of the certificate into Slovenian or English
3. **Proof of the content and duration** of your education, such as report cards, with a sworn translation of the last two years
4. **Chronological summary of education**, with years, and the address, website and contact email of each institution
5. **ID card or passport** (EU citizens) or **passport** (non-EU citizens)

Master's applicants with foreign credentials must also submit an **application for recognition of education** with their enrolment application.

## Guide by region

### Students already in the EU
- Apply through eVŠ with an ID card or passport.
- Certificates from the countries listed above need no legalisation.
- Choose English or Slovene delivery, because fees differ.

### Nepal and Sri Lanka
- Budget for English-delivery fees: €3,300 (Bachelor's) or €4,000 (Master's) per year.
- Prepare apostille or legalisation and sworn translations early.
- Ask NOA about the interview that applies to applicants whose countries are on the EU visa-required list.

### Indonesia and the Maldives
- The same non-EU document rules and fees apply.
- Check your country's apostille route, because certificate authentication can take weeks.

### GCC countries
- Apply as a non-EU applicant with a passport copy.
- Ask NOA whether your passport requires an interview, and how your certificate should be authenticated.

## FAQ

### Is NOA taught in English?
NOA states its programs run in Slovenian or English, with a separate, higher price for English delivery.

### Is NOA public or private?
Private. Slovenia's national call lists it as a private institution.

### Is there an application fee?
None is listed. Check the interview requirement for your nationality.
