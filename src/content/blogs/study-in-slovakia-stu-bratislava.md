# Study at the Slovak University of Technology (STU) in Bratislava

If you want an **engineering or IT degree in the EU**, Slovakia deserves a place on your shortlist. The **Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (STU)** offers **300+ study programmes across seven faculties**, and many are accredited for teaching in English. Slovakia is a member of the EU, the Eurozone and the Schengen Area.

> **Verify before you apply.** The programs below come from faculty pages published for the **2026/2027** admission cycle. Programs, fees, deadlines and entry exams change every year. Confirm everything for **2027/2028** on stuba.sk and the faculty website.

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Program

### Informatics (B-INFOxA), Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies (FIIT STU)

- Field: Computer Science
- Full-time, on-site, **3 years**
- Teaching language: **English**

Other faculties may or may not open English bachelor's programs in a given year. For example, the Faculty of Chemical and Food Technology stated that it would not open English-language bachelor's programs in 2026/2027, so always check the current year.

---

## English-Taught Master's Programs

Master's studies last **two years**.

### Joint program: FIIT STU and TUKE

- **Artificial Intelligence** (Computer Science), taught in English. The first and fourth semesters are in Bratislava, and the second and third are at the Technical University of Košice.

### Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (FEI STU)

- Applied Electrical Engineering
- Applied Informatics
- Applied Mechatronics and Electromobility
- Electrical Power Engineering
- Electronics and Photonics
- Nuclear and Physical Engineering
- Space Engineering
- Robotics and Cybernetics

FEI's 2026/2027 admission page states that an **entrance examination is compulsory** for English-language master's applicants, and that there was **no tuition fee** for master's programs offered in English. Fee rules can change, so confirm them for your intake.

---

## English-Taught PhD Programs

FEI STU lists these doctoral programs as available in English:

- Applied Informatics
- Electrical Power Engineering
- Electronics and Photonics
- Physical Engineering

PhD candidates need a **completed master's degree** (engineer or equivalent). Dissertation topics and supervisors are central to admission, so **contact a supervisor before applying**.

---

## Documents You Will Typically Need

- Valid passport
- Previous diplomas and transcripts, **translated and legalised or apostilled** where required
- Proof of English proficiency
- CV and motivation letter
- Research proposal (PhD)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does STU offer programs in English?

Yes. Selected Bachelor's, Master's and PhD programs are taught in English, mainly in informatics and electrical engineering fields. Availability varies by faculty and year.

### Is there an entrance exam?

FEI STU requires an entrance exam for its English-language master's programs. Requirements differ by faculty, so check each one.

### Can I apply from Finland or another EU country?

Yes, you can apply while living elsewhere in the EU, but permit and residence rules depend on your situation.
