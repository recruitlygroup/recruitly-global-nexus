# How Kerala Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Kerala is widely known for producing nurses who work across India and around the world. That reputation is a **powerful draw** for career-focused Nepali students, and also means colleges here must prove they deliver quality.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Kerala

- **Strong, long-standing reputation** in nursing education
- **High awareness** of Kerala nurses in careers abroad
- **English widely used** in education and healthcare
- **Many institutions** offering BSc Nursing, GNM, and Diploma options

## What Kerala Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **Clinical training exposure** and hospital partnerships
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** with hostel, clinical, and travel costs
- ✓ **Honest career information.** Never overstate job outcomes

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Very long distance** from Nepal, with higher travel cost
- **Language and food adjustment** for students far from home
- **Competition** among many Kerala institutions
