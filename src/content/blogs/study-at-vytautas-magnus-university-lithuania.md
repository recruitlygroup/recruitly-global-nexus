# Study at Vytautas Magnus University (VMU) in Lithuania

Studying in Europe in English is more reachable than many students think. **Vytautas Magnus University (VMU)** in Kaunas, Lithuania is a public university that offers a large selection of full-time **Bachelor's and Master's programmes taught in English**. This guide covers what VMU offers, how the two degree levels differ, and how **Recruitly Group** supports you from first enquiry to arrival.

> **Please verify before applying:** Tuition, deadlines, and programme lists change every intake. Always confirm details on the official VMU admissions pages.

---

## Why Choose Vytautas Magnus University?

VMU was founded in 1922 and is known for its **liberal arts approach**, which encourages students to combine disciplines. According to VMU, it offers **around 40 or more full-time English-taught Bachelor's and Master's programmes**, from Engineering, Informatics and Biotechnology to Arts, Music and Humanities. The university also hosts **over 1,000 international students**.

**Key reasons students pick VMU:**

- Public university located in **Kaunas**, Lithuania's second-largest city
- A wide range of **English-taught degrees**
- A growing international community
- **Tuition fee waivers and scholarships** for eligible applicants

## English-Taught Bachelor's Programmes at VMU

A **Bachelor's degree in English at VMU** is a good fit if you have finished secondary school and want an international start to your career.

### What to expect

- **Format:** Full-time study, typically 3 to 4 years
- **Fields:** Engineering, informatics, biotechnology, environmental science, humanities, arts, and more
- **Typical requirements:** Secondary school diploma and proof of English proficiency (often around B2 level)
- **Funding:** VMU has offered partial tuition waivers for certain groups, so ask about current options

### Who it suits

Students who want a **European degree taught in English**, at a tuition level that is often lower than in many Western European countries. Published estimates for international Bachelor's fees have ranged from roughly **€3,400 to €9,600 per year** depending on the programme (third-party figure; confirm with VMU).

---

## English-Taught Master's Programmes at VMU

A **Master's degree in English at VMU** suits graduates who want to specialise or change fields.

### What to expect

- **Format:** Full-time study, commonly around 2 years
- **Fields:** Applied informatics, biotechnology, humanities, and more
- **Typical requirements:** Bachelor's degree and proof of English proficiency
- **Tuition:** Published estimates range from about **€5,000 to €10,400 per year** (third-party figure; confirm with VMU)

### Who it suits

Graduates seeking **career progression, research exposure, or an international qualification**.

## Scholarships and Tuition Fee Waivers

VMU has advertised **tuition fee waivers** for international students on English-taught degrees, including partial waivers for specific groups such as VMU graduates. Waiver percentages, eligibility, and application rules change by year, so check the official scholarship page for the current call.

**Tip:** Apply early. Some benefits depend on holding an acceptance letter and meeting English requirements.
