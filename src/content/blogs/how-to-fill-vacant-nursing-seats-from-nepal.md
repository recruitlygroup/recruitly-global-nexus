# How to Fill Vacant Nursing Seats From Nepal Before Your Intake Closes

Empty seats cost a college money, momentum, and reputation. If your intake is approaching and seats remain open, Nepal can be a fast and relevant source of qualified applicants, if you move early and move clearly.

---

## Why Seats Stay Empty

- **Late outreach** to families who need time to decide
- **Unclear fees** that make families hesitate
- **Slow admissions replies** that lose students to competitors
- **Weak local presence** in Nepal
- **Unverified or incomplete applications** that stall at the last step

---

## A Practical Plan to Fill Seats

### 1. Confirm What Is Open
List the exact seats by program, with deadlines.

### 2. Publish Complete Information
Total fees, hostel details, eligibility, and approvals, in one clear document. See our [brochure checklist](/blog/how-to-prepare-a-nursing-college-brochure-for-nepali-students/).

### 3. Appoint One Fast Contact
Students lost to slow replies are the easiest to prevent.

### 4. Offer Flexible Counseling
Virtual calls with parents help families decide faster.

### 5. Work With a Nepal Partner
A local partner counsels families directly and verifies documents before submission.

### 6. Set Clear Deadlines
Tell families the last date to confirm. Honest urgency helps decisions.
