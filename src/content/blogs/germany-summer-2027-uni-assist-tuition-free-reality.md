# Summer 2027 Germany Intake: uni-assist Deadline and the Tuition-Free Reality

Aiming for the **Summer 2027 intake** (classes start April 2027)? Most public universities close applications on **15 January 2027**. But if your university uses uni-assist, your real deadline is **Friday, 20 November 2026**.

---

## Why 20 November Matters for Summer 2027

uni-assist takes **4–6 weeks** to evaluate your grades and issue a **VPD (Vorprüfungsdokumentation)**. Submitting by 20 November gives you a buffer to fix any missing document requests **before the university portal locks**.

### uni-assist cost
- **€75** for your first university application
- **€30** for each additional program in the same semester

---

## Are Public Universities in Germany 100% Tuition-Free?

Mostly, yes.

- **14 of 16 states:** **€0 tuition** for international students
- You still pay a **semester contribution (Semesterbeitrag)** of roughly **€150–€350 per semester**, which usually includes your **Deutschlandticket** for nationwide local public transport

### The exceptions to watch
1. **Baden-Württemberg** (for example KIT, Heidelberg, Stuttgart, Freiburg): **€1,500 per semester** for non-EU students
2. **TU Munich (TUM):** **€4,000–€6,000 per semester** on most Master's programs for non-EU students

LMU Munich and other Bavarian public universities remain tuition-free for most programs.

---

## Quick Eligibility Check Before You Pay

- Check your university's status in the **Anabin database**. It must be rated **H+**.
- Confirm your Bachelor's **ECTS credits** match the Master's subject prerequisites.
- Cross-stream switches, such as Business to Computer Science, are rarely accepted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the uni-assist deadline for Summer 2027?
If your university uses uni-assist, aim for **20 November 2026**, even though the university's own deadline is usually 15 January 2027.

### How much does uni-assist cost?
**€75** for the first application and **€30** for each additional program in the same semester.

### Is Germany really free for international students?
In most states, tuition is €0, but you pay a semester contribution. Baden-Württemberg and TUM charge non-EU students.
