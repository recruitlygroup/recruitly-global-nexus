# University of Macerata 2027/28 Admission Guide: Programs, Deadlines & How to Apply

The **University of Macerata (UniMC)**, founded in **1290**, is one of the oldest universities in Europe and the only Italian university that specialises fully in the **humanities and social sciences**. It offers a focused set of **English-taught degrees**, very low tuition for many international students, and a calm, walkable student city in Italy's Marche region.

**Pre-admission applications for 2027/28 open in November 2026**, and the first intake for some programs closes on **30 November 2026**. This guide covers the programs, intake dates, requirements and costs.

> **Quick answer:** The online pre-admission portal runs from **3 November 2026 to 30 April 2027**. Each program has several intakes. For International Relations, the first closes **30 November 2026** and the last closes **30 April 2027**. Classes start on **13 September 2027**.

---

## Why Study at the University of Macerata?

- **Heritage:** established in **1290**, UniMC is among the oldest universities in the world.
- **Focus:** programs in **law, political science, international relations, economics, education, cultural heritage, tourism, languages and communication**.
- **European network:** UniMC belongs to **ERUA (European Reform University Alliance)**, a group of eight European universities in Bulgaria, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, Lithuania, Poland and Spain.
- **Double and multiple degrees:** selected Master's programs let you earn a second degree with partners in Germany, Brazil, Poland and France.
- **Student city:** most departments, libraries, canteens and residences sit in or near Macerata's historic centre.
- **Honest context:** UniMC is a small, specialised university. It does not appear in the QS global top rankings, so its strength is **subject focus, teaching quality and affordability**, not global league-table position.

## English-Taught Bachelor's Program

UniMC currently offers **one Bachelor's degree entirely in English**:

- **International, European and Comparative Legal Studies** (3 years, Department of Law)

All other Bachelor's programs, such as Communication, Cultural Heritage and Tourism, Economics and Management, and Political Science and International Relations, are **taught in Italian**.

**Entry requirement:** a secondary school diploma or an equivalent foreign qualification. Non-EU applicants living abroad who choose an English-taught course do **not** need Italian proficiency and are exempt from the Italian entry test.

## English-Taught Master's Programs

UniMC offers a small set of **two-year Master's degrees in English**:

### International Relations (LM-52)
Taught entirely in English with three tracks: **International Politics and Economic Relations**, **International Economic Relations**, and **International Politics**. Double degrees are available with the European University Viadrina in Germany and the State University of Campinas in Brazil.

### International Finance and Economics (LM-16)
A curriculum of the Finance and Markets Master's, with multiple degree options with partners in Poland and France.

### International Tourism and Destination Management (LM-49)
Taught entirely in English, preparing graduates for tourism planning, marketing and destination management.

### Management and International Marketing (LM-77)
Listed with English-language study options. **Confirm the English curriculum on the official program page** before applying.

## 2027/28 Pre-Admission Deadlines

International students with a foreign qualification must complete an **online pre-admission** at **apply.unimc.it**. Each program has its own intakes. The official **International Relations** schedule for 2027/28 is:

| Intake | Application opens | Application closes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 9 Nov 2026 | **30 Nov 2026** |
| 2 | 1 Dec 2026 | **8 Jan 2027** |
| 3 | 11 Jan 2027 | **1 Feb 2027** |
| 4 | 2 Feb 2027 | **1 Mar 2027** |
| 5 | 2 Mar 2027 | **31 Mar 2027** |
| 6 | 1 Apr 2027 | **30 Apr 2027** |

All deadlines close at **23:59:59 Central European Time**, and studies begin on **13 September 2027**.

### How the process works
1. **Submit your pre-admission** on apply.unimc.it in one of the program's intakes.
2. **Receive your result by email** after the intake closes. Expect to wait at least **two weeks**. If you are eligible, a **pre-admission letter** is included.
3. **Complete pre-enrolment on Universitaly** (non-EU students needing a visa).
4. **Apply for your student visa** at the Italian embassy or consulate.
5. **Finalise enrolment** at UniMC.

> **Tip:** Choosing an earlier or later intake does not change how your application is judged. The evaluation depends on whether you meet the requirements. Applying earlier simply gives you more time for the visa.

Other programs may use different intake dates, so **check each program's page on apply.unimc.it**.

## Entry Requirements

- **Academic qualification:** an undergraduate degree (or higher) for Master's programs. Some programs require a **minimum number of credits in specific subject areas**. For example, International Relations asks for credits in areas such as Law, Politics and Sociology, or Economics and Statistics, depending on your track.
- **English at B2:** shown by an international certificate, a degree taught in English, or being a native speaker. Some programs, such as International Tourism and Destination Management, list **IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90** as accepted proof.
- **Motivation letter:** required, original and concise, explaining why you chose UniMC and your goals.
- **References:** at least **two** for International Relations.
- **Italian:** not required for English-taught programs for non-EU applicants abroad.
- **Documents:** unofficial translations are accepted for the preliminary evaluation if your documents are not in English or Italian. Official translations are needed for final enrolment.

## Tuition Fees and Scholarships

UniMC applies **country-based tuition reductions** for international students living abroad. Per the International Relations program page (figures for 2025/26, so verify for 2027/28):

- Students from **developing and non-OECD countries** pay about **EUR 156 per year**.
- Students from **OECD countries** pay about **EUR 807 per year**.
- The maximum full-time fee is **EUR 1,600 per year**.

Scholarship options listed by the university include **UniMC scholarships, Invest Your Talent in Italy, Study in Italy (MAECI) grants, and regional DSU (ERDIS) scholarships**.
