# Nursing College Scams and Red Flags: How to Protect Yourself

> **Not sure if an offer is real? WhatsApp us before you pay: +977 974 3208282. Free to check.**

Nepali media have reported cases where institutions claimed Indian affiliation but their certificates were not recognised, and where students lost money to misleading consultancies. Most colleges and consultancies are honest, but you must check.

---

## Red Flags in Colleges

- ❌ **No approval documents** or only photos of certificates
- ❌ **Approval for a different program** or old year
- ❌ **University name you cannot verify**
- ❌ **Different fees** in brochure, website, and conversation
- ❌ **Pressure to pay now** to "reserve a seat"
- ❌ **Refusing to let you visit** or talk to students

## Red Flags in Consultancies

- ❌ **Guaranteed admission or guaranteed job**
- ❌ **Large upfront fees** with unclear services
- ❌ **No written agreement or receipt**
- ❌ **Refusing to name the college** until you pay
- ❌ **Only one college recommended** regardless of your profile

---

## How to Protect Yourself

- ✓ **Verify recognition** yourself. See [how to check](/blog/how-to-check-if-a-nursing-college-in-india-is-recognised/)
- ✓ **Confirm registration** questions with the councils. See [what to verify](/blog/will-your-indian-nursing-degree-work-in-nepal-what-to-verify/)
- ✓ **Pay only against receipts** in the college's name
- ✓ **Pay the college directly**, not through an individual, where possible
- ✓ **Keep records** of every message and document
