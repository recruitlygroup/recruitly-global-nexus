# How Mizoram Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Mizoram is a hill state in the far Northeast with a calm, community-oriented culture and widespread English. Its remoteness means **clear communication and trust-building** matter more than anywhere else.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Mizoram

- **English widely used** in education and daily life
- **Hill environment** familiar to students from Nepal's hills
- **A safe-feeling, close-knit community**
- **Almost no competition** for Nepali student attention

## What Mizoram Institutions Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **Clear travel routes and costs** from Nepal
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** with hostel and living costs
- ✓ **A named contact** and a structured orientation

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by institution and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Very long and costly travel**
- **Very low awareness** of the state among Nepali families
