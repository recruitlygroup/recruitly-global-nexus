# Study in English at Marche Polytechnic University (UNIVPM): Bachelor, Master & PhD Guide

Looking for an English-taught degree in a smaller, less crowded Italian city? **Marche Polytechnic University** (Università Politecnica delle Marche, **UNIVPM**) is a public university based in **Ancona**, on the Adriatic coast, with additional campuses including Pesaro. It offers **12 degree programs taught entirely in English** across economics, engineering, science, agriculture and medicine.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It covers programs, costs and deadlines.

> **Heads up:** Dates, fees and programs change each cycle. The details below come from UNIVPM's official international admissions guide for **2026/27**, updated in May 2026. Confirm everything on the official site before applying.

---

## Why Study at Marche Polytechnic University?

UNIVPM's international guide lists its programs by language. Most bachelor's degrees are taught in Italian, but there is a **wide offer of master's programs in English**. For international students, that means:

- **12 programs entirely in English:** 2 bachelor's, 9 master's and 1 single-cycle degree
- **11 free-access programs** where you apply without a selective admission test (Medicine is restricted access)
- **A single online portal**, MoveIn, for pre-evaluation applications

---

## English-Taught Bachelor's Degrees at UNIVPM

For 2026/27, two bachelor's degrees are taught entirely in English, both in Ancona, both free access, both requiring **English B2**:

- **Digital Economics and Business** (Faculty of Economics)
- **Environmental Sciences and Civil Protection** (Faculty of Science)

Other bachelor's degrees are taught in Italian and require **Italian B2**.

---

## English-Taught Master's Degrees at UNIVPM

The guide lists **nine master's degrees entirely in English**, all free access with English B2:

- **International Economics and Commerce**
- **Data Science for Finance and Economics**
- **Food and Beverage Innovation and Management** (interview)
- **Biomedical Engineering**
- **Environmental Engineering**
- **Green Industrial Engineering** (Pesaro campus)
- **Electronic Engineering**
- **Environmental Hazard and Disaster Risk Management** (interview)
- **Marine Biology** (interview)

**Medicine and Surgery (MedTech)** is also taught in English as a **restricted-access single-cycle degree**. Check the competition notice for the admission test.

Electronic Engineering is offered with Italian and English curricula starting from the second call, and new programs or curricula can be added from the second call.

## PhD Programs at UNIVPM

UNIVPM runs PhD programs through yearly calls. A third-party listing describes the **42nd-cycle (2026/27)** call as covering engineering, life sciences, biomedical sciences, food science and economics, open to national and international candidates, with a deadline around **11 August 2026**. I could not confirm details such as scholarships or language of instruction from the official call, so check the official PhD page for your program.

---

## UNIVPM Application Fee

- **Application fee:** **€10 per application**, paid by credit card in the MoveIn portal.
- **Limit:** International students may submit **a maximum of two applications** for different courses.
- **Outcome:** Because of the high number of applicants, results can take **a minimum of 45 days**.
- **Tuition:** Set separately. Third-party listings show about €156 per year for some master's programs, but confirm current fees on univpm.it.

---

## UNIVPM Application Deadlines

For **2026/27**, international students could apply in several calls:

| Call | Dates | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **1st call** | 16 Dec 2025 – 30 Jan 2026 | All international students (free access) |
| **2nd call** | 1 Mar – 30 Apr 2026 | All international students; new programs added |
| **3rd call** | 8 – 30 Jun 2026 | All international students (free and restricted access) |
| **4th call** | 1 – 31 Jul 2026 | Students from listed countries |
| **5th call** | 15 Jul – 5 Nov 2026 | EU students and non-EU residents in Italy |

**Visa applicants** had to complete their Universitaly pre-enrolment by **30 November 2026** for the 2026/27 intake.

For **2027/28**, the first call last year opened on **16 December**, so watch the international admissions site from mid-December.

---

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Pick your program** and check its language, access type and requirements.
2. **Register on the UNIVPM MoveIn portal** with your name exactly as in your passport.
3. **Complete the pre-evaluation application** and upload your qualifications and language certificate.
4. **Pay the €10 fee** for each application.
5. **Attend the interview or test** if your program requires one.
6. **Pre-enrol on Universitaly** if you need a visa, then enrol after admission.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Marche Polytechnic University offer programs in English?
Yes. It lists 12 programs entirely in English: 2 bachelor's, 9 master's and 1 single-cycle degree.

### Is there an application fee at UNIVPM?
Yes, €10 per application for 2026/27 admissions.

English B2 (CEFR) for English-taught programs. The proof accepted can vary by program.

For 2026/27 the first call opened on 16 December 2025. Check the site for the 2027/28 calendar.
