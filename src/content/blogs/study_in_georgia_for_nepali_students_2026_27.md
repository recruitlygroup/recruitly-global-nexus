## Why Georgia Appeals to Nepali Students

Georgia has grown popular among Nepali students seeking an international degree without heavy upfront barriers:

* **English-Medium Degree Tracks:** Many programs in medicine/dentistry, computer science/IT, engineering, business/management, and social sciences are taught in English.
* **Clear Program & Fee Structure:** Tuition fees are published upfront by program and university, allowing students and families to compare costs clearly before applying.
* **Two Main Intakes:** Universities commonly run **Spring** and **Fall/Summer** intakes (note that Georgia's September 2026 regulatory updates also set defined enrolment windows and tightened academic progression/residence permit rules—always verify current intake and permit rules when applying).
* **No Mandatory IELTS/TOEFL at Most Universities:** English proficiency is typically evaluated via a university interview and academic background rather than a mandatory external English test score.

## Popular Programs & Tuition Overview

Depending on your academic background (+2 / Grade 12, Bachelor's, or Master's), programs span **Bachelor's (3–4 years; 5–6 years for medicine/dentistry)**, **Master's (1–2 years)**, and **PhD** tracks:

* **Medicine (MD / MBBS) & Dentistry:** Typically **6 years (72 months)** for MD/MBBS and **5 years (60 months)** for Dentistry, with tuition commonly in the **$4,500 to $6,500+ USD/year** range depending on the university and program.
* **IT, Computer Science & Engineering:** Bachelor's programs (typically **48 months**) often range around **$3,500 to $5,000 USD/year** (with select technical programs quoted in **GEL**).
* **Business, Management & Master's / PhD:** Bachelor's business/analytics tracks often sit around **$3,500 to $4,500 USD/year**, while Master's and PhD tuition varies widely by university and currency (**USD** or **GEL**).

## Budget Planning: Living Costs, Packages & Nepal Forex/NOC Steps

Planning your full budget early prevents last-minute delays in Nepal or Georgia:

* **Independent Monthly Living:** Budget roughly **$300 to $500 USD/month** for shared housing, meals/groceries, local transport, and utilities (adjusting for city, lifestyle, and winter heating).
* **All-Inclusive Living Option:** A **$9,000 USD/year** package covers **3 meals a day, accommodation (with cleaning twice a week), Wi-Fi, and utility bills**.
* **Nepal-Side Compliance (NOC & Banking):** Nepali students typically arrange **Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST) No Objection Certificate (NOC)** where applicable, complete bank foreign exchange / outward remittance procedures through licensed banks in Nepal, and keep organized PDF copies of all academic and identity documents.
* **Visa, Insurance & Residence Permit:** Factor in application/consular fees, EQE recognition and residence permit fees, health insurance meeting Georgia's coverage requirements, and timely filing for your **Temporary Residence Permit** after arrival.

## Step-by-Step Application & Visa Flow for Nepali Applicants

1. **Shortlist Course & University:** Pick an authorized program matching your eligibility, budget, and career/licensing goals.
2. **Apply Online & Complete Interview:** Submit your application file and complete the admissions interview to receive your offer/admission steps.
3. **Document Translation, Notarization & EQE Recognition:** Complete required translations and notarizations, submit for **EQE recognition**, and follow the Ministry enrollment authorization process.
4. **Fee Payment & Invitation / Enrollment Docs:** Remit required fees per instructions and obtain your admission/enrollment and visa supporting documents.
5. **Category D3 Visa & Residence Permit:** Apply for your **Category D3 Study Visa** for entry (where required) and submit your **Temporary Residence Permit** application through the **Public Service Hall** in Georgia before your legal stay expires.

## Practical Tips for Nepali Students Before You Fly

* **Keep File Naming & PDFs Clean:** Use clear, consistent PDF naming (such as `LastName_FirstName_TypeOfDocument.pdf`) so your application and verification files stay organized.
* **Verify Home-Country Rules Early:** If you plan to return to Nepal (or move elsewhere) after graduation—especially in healthcare/medicine or regulated fields—confirm recognition, licensing, and equivalence rules in advance.
* **Respect Residence Permit & Work Rules:** Maintain active study enrollment and credit progress, and verify Georgia's work permit rules before taking on any paid or remote work.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do Nepali students need an NOC from Nepal to study in Georgia?
Students from Nepal typically obtain a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST) for banking/remittance and official record purposes—check the latest MoEST checklist for your level of study.

### How much does it cost to live in Georgia as a student?
Independent living generally averages **$300 to $500 USD per month** (excluding tuition), or you can opt for a **$9,000 USD/year** all-inclusive package covering **3 daily meals, accommodation with twice-weekly cleaning, Wi-Fi, and utilities**.

### How do I apply for the Georgia student visa and residence permit?
After admissions, EQE recognition, and fee/enrollment steps, applicants apply for a **Category D3 Study Visa** prior to travel (where required) and apply for a **Temporary Residence Permit** at Georgia's **Public Service Hall** after arrival.
