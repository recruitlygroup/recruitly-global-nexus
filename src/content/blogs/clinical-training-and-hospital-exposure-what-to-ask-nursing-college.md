# Clinical Training and Hospital Exposure: What to Ask a Nursing College

> **Need help comparing training quality? WhatsApp us: +977 974 3208282. Free for students.**

Nursing is a practical profession. A college with weak hospital training leaves you without skills, even if the classroom teaching is good.

---

## Questions to Ask

- ✓ **Which hospitals** will I train in? Are they owned by the college or tied up through agreements?
- ✓ **How many beds and departments** does the hospital have?
- ✓ **How many students** share the same clinical posting?
- ✓ **Will I rotate** through different departments (medical, surgical, maternity, pediatrics, community)?
- ✓ **Who supervises** students during practicals?
- ✓ **Is the internship** arranged and included?
- ✓ **Can I visit** the hospital and labs before joining?

---

## Signs of Strong Training

- Real hospital with real patients, close to the college
- Clear schedule for postings
- Qualified clinical instructors
- Well-equipped practical labs
- Students who describe real experience

## Signs of Weak Training

- ❌ Hospital far away or only "on paper"
- ❌ No clear posting schedule
- ❌ Too many students per ward
- ❌ The college avoids a hospital visit
