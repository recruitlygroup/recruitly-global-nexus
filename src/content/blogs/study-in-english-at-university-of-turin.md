# Study in English at the University of Turin: Bachelor, Master & PhD Guide

Looking to **study in Italy in English**? The **University of Turin (UniTo)** is one of Italy's historic public universities, and it offers degree programs taught in English at every level: Bachelor, Master and PhD.

This guide is part of the **Study in Italy University Series** by Recruitly Group. It covers which English-taught programs exist, what the application costs, and when to apply.

> **Heads up:** Programs, fees and deadlines change each academic year. The figures below come from the University of Turin's official pages and calls for the **2026/27 academic year**. Always confirm current details on the official UniTo site and on Apply@UniTo before you apply.

---

## Why Study at the University of Turin?

The University of Turin offers **more than 165 undergraduate and postgraduate programs**, of which **28 are taught entirely in English**, alongside joint and double degree programs. The university community counts **over 83,000 students**.

For international students, this means:

- **A large, established public university** with a broad choice of subjects
- **English-taught options** across economics, law, data science, biotechnology, humanities and more
- **A single online application portal**, Apply@UniTo, for international applicants

---

## English-Taught Bachelor Degrees at the University of Turin

For academic year 2026/27, UniTo lists **three undergraduate degrees in English**:

- **Business & Management** (Department of Management)
- **Economics and Finance with Data Science** (Department of Social Studies, Applied Mathematics and Statistics)
- **Global Law and Transnational Legal Studies** (Department of Law)

### Who should consider a bachelor in English at UniTo?

These degrees suit students who want an international classroom, a degree from a major Italian public university, and a program in business, economics, data or law. Check each program's page for entry requirements, English-level proof and any admission test.

## English-Taught Master's Degrees at the University of Turin

The master's offer is much broader. UniTo lists **20 postgraduate degrees in English** for 2026/27, plus several courses with an English-language curriculum. Examples include:

- **Artificial Intelligence and High Performance Computing Technologies**
- **Stochastics and Data Science**
- **Business Administration**
- **Economics** and **Economics of Innovation for Sustainable Development**
- **Quantitative Finance and Insurance**
- **Molecular Biotechnology** and **Cellular and Molecular Biology**
- **Food Science and Technology**
- **European Legal Studies**
- **English and American Studies**
- **Area and Global Studies for International Cooperation**
- **Language Technologies and Digital Humanities**

**English-language curricula** are also available within degrees such as Philosophy, Smart Agriculture, Viticulture and Enology Sciences, Animal Nutrition and Feed Safety, and Urban and Political Geography. UniTo also lists **Medicine and Surgery** in English as a 6-year degree.

### Master's scholarships

For 2026/27, the university funded **40 two-year "Talents 4 UniTo" scholarships** for international students enrolling in the first year of an English-taught, open-access master's program. Check whether a similar call exists for 2027/28.

## English-Taught PhD Programs at the University of Turin

UniTo runs a yearly PhD call across many fields: sciences, medicine, law, economics, humanities, social sciences, data science and biotechnology. For the **42nd cycle (2026/27)**, third-party reports describe a call with **331 funded positions** and a scholarship of about **€17,500 per year**. Verify these figures in the official call.

### Is every PhD taught in English?

No. The **language requirement depends on the specific PhD program**. Some programs list English as the language of instruction, while others require English proficiency for research work. Read the program section of the official call carefully.

**Typical PhD application elements:**

- CV and academic records
- A research proposal (most programs use an official template)
- Proof of English proficiency, where required
- Possible interview or evaluation

---

## University of Turin Application Fee

International students apply through **Apply@UniTo**. The application fee is **€60**, which allows you to apply for **up to two degree programs**. Depending on the program, you might also face an interview with the faculty or an entrance test.

PhD application fees and procedures are set in the PhD call, so check the call for your program.

---

## University of Turin Application Deadlines

Deadlines vary by citizenship and program level. For **academic year 2026/27**, the international application calls were:

| Level | Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Postgraduate (Master's)** | Nov 25, 2025 – Jan 29, 2026 | First admission call |
| **Undergraduate and 5-6 year degrees** | Feb 19 – Apr 15, 2026 | Second admission call |
| **Free-access postgraduate** | Aug 25 – Sep 8, 2026 | Reserved for Italians, EU/EU-equivalent, and non-EU citizens legally residing in Italy |
| **PhD (42nd cycle)** | Closed June 9, 2026, 12:00 CEST | Check the official call |

For **academic year 2027/28**, expect a similar pattern but **confirm the exact dates on the official UniTo website**.

## How to Apply: Quick Steps

1. **Pick your program** from the English-taught list on the UniTo website.
2. **Read the admission requirements**, including English proof, documents and any test.
3. **Check the deadline** for your citizenship and level.
4. **Create your Apply@UniTo account** and complete the application.
5. **Pay the €60 fee** (covers up to two programs).
6. **Track your result** and prepare for enrolment and visa steps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is the University of Turin application fee?
The fee for international applicants is **€60**, covering up to two degree programs through Apply@UniTo.

### Does the University of Turin offer programs in English?
Yes. UniTo offers **28 programs entirely in English**, across Bachelor, Master's and other levels.

### Are there English-taught bachelor's degrees at UniTo?
Yes. For 2026/27: Business & Management; Economics and Finance with Data Science; and Global Law and Transnational Legal Studies.

### When is the deadline for a master's at the University of Turin?
For 2026/27 the first call for postgraduate programs ran from **November 25, 2025 to January 29, 2026**. Confirm 2027/28 dates on the official site.

### Can I do a PhD at the University of Turin in English?
Some programs are taught in English, and language requirements differ by program. Review the specific program in the official PhD call.
