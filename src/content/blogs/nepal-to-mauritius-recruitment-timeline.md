# Nepal to Mauritius Recruitment Timeline: How Long Does Hiring Really Take?

Employers in Mauritius often ask, "How soon can my staff arrive?" There is no single number. Timelines depend on the role, number of workers, and how complete your documents are. What you *can* do is understand each stage and plan around it.

---

## The 7 Stages of Recruitment

### Stage 1: Requirement Finalization
You confirm job titles, quantities, salary, benefits, and contract length. **Clear requirements here prevent weeks of rework later.**

### Stage 2: Sourcing and Shortlisting
Your partner searches databases and networks, then screens CVs against your criteria.

### Stage 3: Interviews and Trade Tests
Interviews can be done online. Technical roles may need practical tests.

### Stage 4: Selection and Offer
You choose candidates, and offer letters and contracts are issued.

### Stage 5: Permits and Approvals
Work permit applications and approvals on the Mauritius side, plus the required clearances in Nepal.

### Stage 6: Medicals and Pre-Departure Processing
Health checks, document verification, and travel preparation.

### Stage 7: Travel and Arrival
Flights, arrival coordination, and onboarding at your site.

---

## What Slows Hiring Down

- **Vague job descriptions** that cause repeated shortlisting
- **Slow employer approvals** at the offer stage
- **Missing or incorrect documents**
- **Changing salary or terms** after selection
- **Peak-season congestion** in permit processing

---

## How to Hire Faster

- ✓ **Start 3 to 4 months before** your peak season where possible
- ✓ **Prepare company documents in advance.** See our [documents checklist](/blog/documents-checklist-hiring-nepali-workers-mauritius/)
- ✓ **Assign one decision-maker** for approvals
- ✓ **Use one partner** who handles both sides of the process
