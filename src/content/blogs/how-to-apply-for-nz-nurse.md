# How to Become a Registered Nurse in New Zealand from Nepal: Step-by-Step Guide for BSc & GNM Nurses

## Introduction

If you are a qualified nurse in Nepal—whether holding a **BSc Nursing / Post Basic Nursing (BN/BSc)** or a **PCL Nursing (GNM)** diploma—New Zealand presents one of the most lucrative and high-demand career paths globally. 

The Nursing Council of New Zealand (NCNZ) transitioned fully to an **Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)** model, replacing the older Competency Assessment Programme (CAP). In addition, **CGFNS** rebranded its global credentials service to **TruMerit**. 

This comprehensive guide details qualifications, exact score requirements, application steps, exam locations, visa requirements, timeframes, and estimated costs.

---

## Qualification Eligibility Criteria

| Nepali Qualification | NZ Pathways & Eligibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **BSc Nursing / BN** | **Direct RN Route:** Highly straightforward pathway for degree holders with active NNC (Nepal Nursing Council) registration. |
| **PCL Nursing / GNM** | **Level Assessment Pathway:** Eligible for conversion; depending on experience and education, NCNZ assesses qualification levels (often Enrolled Nurse / Level 4 or requiring top-up pathways before full RN registration). |

## English Language Requirements

You must meet NCNZ’s language proficiency standard before initiating credential evaluation. Scores can be combined over two sittings within a 12-month window.

* **IELTS Academic:** Minimum **7.0 in Reading, Listening, and Speaking**, and **6.5 in Writing**.
* **OET (Occupational English Test - Nursing):** Minimum **Grade B (350)** in Reading, Listening, and Speaking, and **Grade C+ / B (minimum 300)** in Writing.

## Step-by-Step Registration & OSCE Process
[Step 1: TruMerit (CGFNS)] ➔ [Step 2: NCNZ & Theory Exam] ➔ [Step 3: Visa & Travel] ➔ [Step 4: Christchurch OSCE]

### Step 1: TruMerit Credentials Verification (CVS-NCNZ)
Before NCNZ evaluates your application, your academic and professional documents must undergo primary-source verification through **TruMerit** (formerly CGFNS).

1. Create an account on the **TruMerit CVS-NCNZ portal**.
2. Upload notarized color copies of your passport and secondary identity documents.
3. Direct your university (TU, KU, POU, etc.) and the Nepal Nursing Council (NNC) to send your transcripts and license verifications directly to TruMerit.
4. Submit proof of minimum **1,800 clinical hours** of nursing experience.

* **TruMerit Cost:** ~**USD $380** (~NPR 51,000).
* **Duration:** 1 to 3 months (depending on how quickly your Nepalese institutions respond).

---

### Step 2: NCNZ Application & Pearson VUE Theoretical Exam
Once TruMerit issues your verification report, you approve it and submit your formal application to the Nursing Council of New Zealand (NCNZ) via the **MyIQN portal**.

1. **NCNZ Portal Registration:** Pay the application fee and complete two free mandatory online cultural safety courses (*Ngā Paerewa Te Tiriti* and *Foundations in Cultural Competence*).
2. **International Criminal History Check (ICHC):** Processed via Fit2Work.
3. **Theoretical Examination (NCLEX-style online exam):**
   * NCNZ emails an invitation link to book your theoretical exam through **Pearson VUE**.
   * **Exam Centers:** Computer-based test center. Candidates in Nepal can sit for the exam at Pearson VUE centers in Kathmandu (or nearby international centers in India if slots are limited).
   * **Result Time:** Extremely fast—preliminary results are typically available shortly after completion or within 10–15 minutes online.

* **NCNZ Fee:** ~**NZD $485** (~NPR 39,500).
* **Fit2Work ICHC:** ~**NZD $169** (~NPR 13,800).
* **Theory Exam Fee:** ~**NZD $140** (~NPR 11,400).
* **Duration:** 3 to 6 weeks.

---

### Step 3: Visa Application & Financial Documentation
After passing the theoretical exam, NCNZ issues an authorization letter to book your clinical practical exam (OSCE) in New Zealand. You will apply for an **Occupational/Visitor Visa** to enter NZ.

* **Bank Balance Statement:** You must show proof of funds in your or your sponsor's bank account (typically **NPR 15–16 Lakhs** shown as unencumbered liquid funds) to cover your stay, exam, and return travel.
* **Income & Financial Proof:** Income verification, tax clearance certificates, and official salary slips.
* **Overall Upfront Expense Allowance:** Expect roughly **NPR 10–12 Lakhs** in direct costs (exam fees, clinical orientation bookings, airfare, accommodation, visa fees, and living expenses).

---

### Step 4: Traveling to NZ & Taking the OSCE
You will fly to New Zealand to take the mandatory practical assessment.

* **Orientation & Preparation:** Mandatory 2-day preparation course.
* **Location:** Held in-person at the **Nurse Maude Simulation & Assessment Centre in Christchurch, New Zealand**.
* **Format:** A 3-hour practical exam where you demonstrate real-time clinical assessment, nursing procedures, communication, and patient care scenarios before evaluators.

* **OSCE Exam & Orientation Fee:** ~**NZD $3,500** (~NPR 285,000) combined.
* **Duration in NZ:** 2 to 4 weeks for preparation and examination.

---

### Step 5: Results, Registration & Job Placement
1. **OSCE Results:** Results are released within **1 to 1.5 months**.
2. **Job Search Period:** While waiting for your official registration output, construct an NZ-standard CV and targeted cover letter.
3. **Full Registration & Work Visa:** Upon passing, NCNZ issues your **Annual Practising Certificate (APC)**. You can now transition onto an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) to work as a full Registered Nurse.
