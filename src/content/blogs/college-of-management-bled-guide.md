# Study Hospitality and Tourism at the College of Management Bled, Slovenia

The **College of Management Bled** (VM Bled, also written CM Bled) is an independent higher education institution founded by the Municipality of Bled. It has operated since 2010 and teaches in Bled, a place the college describes as having a strong tradition of tourism and hotel management.

> **Important:** the most recent call published on the college's English website is for **2025/26**. Newer dates and fees were not on the English site when this guide was written, so treat every date below as a pattern, not a promise, and confirm with the college.

---

## What makes the college stand out

The 2025/26 call set aside **90 places for foreigners (non-EU citizens) in the Hospitality and Tourism program**, plus 50 for Slovenian and EU citizens. That is a large international quota compared with many Slovenian institutions. Other points from the college:

- **Same tuition for foreigners and EU citizens** in the published price list
- An **emphasis on foreign languages**: English, German and Italian
- Programs accredited by the Slovenian Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education (SQAA)

---

## Bachelor's programs

All three are first-cycle programs of **3 years and 180 ECTS**, taught in Bled.

| Program | Mode | In the 2025/26 call? |
|---|---|---|
| **Hospitality and Tourism** | Part-time | Yes (90 places for foreigners, 50 for Slovenian and EU citizens) |
| **Health Tourism** | Part-time | Yes (50 places for foreigners, 50 for Slovenian and EU citizens) |
| Management in Food Industry and Nutrition | Listed on the college site | No |

**The first year runs only if at least 30 students enrol.**

Hospitality and Tourism graduates are prepared to run a hotel or restaurant, design tourism products, organise corporate events and congresses, and manage staff. Graduates receive a Slovenian first Bologna degree (level 6/2).

## Is it taught in English?

This is the first question to settle. The college's English pages **do not state the language of instruction**. They do show that the 2025/26 call asks applicants for an **English language certificate, IELTS band 6 to 9**. Ask the college in writing:

1. Is the program delivered in English for foreign students?
2. Will an English group run in 2027/28, and what is the minimum group size?

## Application windows (latest published call, 2025/26)

| Period | Applications | Supporting documents (education abroad) |
|---|---|---|
| First | 18 Feb – 28 Mar 2025 | By 17 Mar 2025 |
| Second | 5 May – 6 Jun 2025 | By 30 May 2025 |
| Third | 11 Aug – 30 Sep 2025 | By 25 Aug 2025 |

Apply online through Slovenia's **eVŠ portal**. If your documents are incomplete, you must submit the missing ones **within 5 days** of the notification.

## What it costs

From the college's price list (approved December 2022, **VAT not included**):

- **Hospitality and Tourism:** €2,950 per academic year
- **Health Tourism:** €2,950 per academic year
- **Management in Food Industry and Nutrition:** €3,950 per academic year
- **Enrolment fee:** €50
- **Application fee:** none listed
- **Repeating a year:** €400. **Enrolment for the graduate year:** €500

The fee for foreigners is the same as the standard fee. Check whether the price list has been updated since 2022.

---

## Documents

Submit through the eVŠ portal:

1. Certificate or diploma of completed (or partly completed) education: a photocopy, and the original if you have it
2. **Certified Slovene translation** of the certificate
3. Proof of the **contents and duration** of your education (transcripts, report cards, certificate supplement)
4. A **short chronological description** of your whole education, signed by you
5. **English certificate:** IELTS band 6 to 9
6. **Apostille** where required, in line with the Hague Convention. Check the status of your country.

Applicants with a foreign certificate must also request **recognition of education** together with their application.

## Guide by region

### Students already in the EU
- You compete for the Slovenian and EU places (50 in each program in 2025/26).
- Have your certificate and a Slovene translation ready for upload.
- Check your country's apostille rules.

### Nepal and Sri Lanka
- You apply for the places set aside for foreigners (90 in Hospitality and Tourism, 50 in Health Tourism in 2025/26).
- Prepare IELTS and certified translations early, and check whether your country uses an apostille.
- Ask the college about any interview for applicants who need a visa. The call page does not describe one.

### Indonesia and the Maldives
- The same foreigner places and document rules apply.
- Plan the apostille or legalisation route well before the document deadline.

### GCC countries
- Apply in the foreigner category and ask whether your passport needs an interview.
- Confirm how your certificate is authenticated for use in Slovenia.

## FAQ

No. It offers three Bachelor's programs.

None is listed. The enrolment fee is €50.

### Is the study part-time?
Yes. The Hospitality and Tourism and Health Tourism programs in the call are part-time.
