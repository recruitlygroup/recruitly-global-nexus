# Will Your Indian Nursing Degree Work in Nepal? What to Verify Before You Join

> **Questions about registration? WhatsApp us: +977 974 3208282. Free for students.**

Many students ask: "Can I work as a nurse in Nepal after studying in India?" This is one of the most important questions, and **you should get the answer from official sources before you join any college.**

---

## What We Know

- Nursing is regulated by councils in both countries. In Nepal, that is the **Nepal Nursing Council (NNC).** In India, there is the **Indian Nursing Council (INC)** and state councils.
- Registration rules can differ by program, year, and country.
- Nepali media and student groups have reported problems in the past around registration and recognition for Nepali students in India.
- Rules change over time. What was true for older batches may not apply to you.

> We are not the authority on this. **Please confirm directly with the Nepal Nursing Council and the Indian council before paying fees.** Do not accept a verbal promise from anyone, including us.

---

## Questions to Ask

### Ask the Nepal Nursing Council
- Is my specific program (BSc, GNM, or other) accepted for registration or equivalence?
- What documents and steps are needed?
- Are there exams or conditions for graduates of Indian colleges?

### Ask the College
- Will you give me INC and state council approval in writing?
- Do Nepali graduates from your college register successfully? Can I speak to some?
- Will I be able to complete internships and clinical postings?

### Ask the Indian Council
- Can foreign students registered in my program get registered in India, and under what conditions?
