# Nursing Visa Germany 2026: § 16d vs. § 18a

If you hold a nursing diploma (PCL/GNM) or a BSc Nursing, you don't always have to wait in your home country until full recognition (**Anerkennung**) is finished. Germany offers two main visa routes for nurses. Which one fits depends on your current stage.

---

## Quick Comparison

| | **Route A: § 16d (3)** | **Route B: § 18a** |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Recognition Partnership | Skilled Worker Visa |
| Recognition status | Not yet complete | Complete |
| Minimum German | A2 | B2 |
| Work on arrival | Paid nursing assistant | Registered nurse |
| Validity | 1 year, extendable up to 3 | Up to 4 years (or contract + 6 months) |

---

## Option A: Recognition Partnership (§ 16d (3))

**Travel first, finish recognition in Germany.** This route suits you if you have a job offer from a German hospital or care facility before your degree is fully recognized.

### How it works

You enter Germany, work as a nursing assistant on a paid contract, and complete your recognition while employed. That means the **Kenntnisprüfung** (knowledge test) or an **adaptation course**.

### Minimum requirements

- State-recognized nursing qualification (minimum 2 years of full-time training)
- Concrete job offer and a written **Recognition Partnership Agreement** with a suitable employer
- A2 German for the visa

> **Note:** Most hospitals still ask for B1/B2 at interview. A higher level helps you progress faster.

### Validity

Issued for 1 year, extendable up to 3 years while you complete licensing.

---

## Option B: Skilled Worker Visa (§ 18a)

**Enter directly as a registered nurse.** This route suits you if you already have B2 German and your full recognition decision (**Gleichwertigkeitsbescheid**).

### How it works

You arrive ready to receive your professional license (Berufsurkunde / Erlaubnis) and work immediately as a fully recognized nurse (Pflegefachmann/-frau) on a full nurse's salary.

### Minimum requirements

- Full equivalence decision from the relevant German state authority
- B2 German certificate (Goethe, telc or ÖSD)
- Employment contract approved by the Federal Employment Agency (BA)

### Validity

Up to 4 years, or contract duration plus 6 months.

---

## Pro Tip: Speed Up Both Routes with § 81a

Your German employer can apply for the **Accelerated Skilled Worker Procedure (§ 81a)** at their local Foreigners' Authority (Ausländerbehörde). The fee is **€411**. Once they issue pre-approval (**Vorabzustimmung**), your VFS visa appointment is fast-tracked.

---

## Which Route Fits You?

- **Job offer, recognition still pending:** Route A
- **B2 German and full recognition in hand:** Route B

## FAQ

### Do I need German to apply for a nursing visa?
Yes. A2 for the Recognition Partnership, B2 for the Skilled Worker Visa.

### Can I work while my recognition is processed?
Under § 16d (3), yes, as a paid nursing assistant.
