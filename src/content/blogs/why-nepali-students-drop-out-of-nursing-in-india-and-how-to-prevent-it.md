# Why Nepali Students Drop Out of Nursing Programs in India, and How Colleges Can Prevent It

Every student who leaves in the first year costs a college tuition, effort, and reputation. The good news: most drop-outs trace back to a handful of **preventable causes.** Fix these, and your Nepal pipeline becomes more stable and more profitable.

---

## The Common Causes

### 1. Mismatched Expectations
Students were told one thing before admission and found another on arrival: fees, hostel standards, clinical hours, or career outcomes.

### 2. Financial Surprises
Unexpected charges, fee increases, or underestimated living costs push families into stress.

### 3. Homesickness and Isolation
Young students far from home, with limited support, struggle in the first months.

### 4. Academic and Language Adjustment
Different teaching styles, medical terminology, and clinical communication can overwhelm new students.

### 5. Hostel and Safety Concerns
Poor food, weak supervision, or unsafe conditions make families pull students out.

### 6. Wrong Program Choice
A student who wanted a shorter route enrolled in a four-year degree, or the reverse.

### 7. No Contact Person
When problems arise and nobody responds, small issues become exits.

---

## How Colleges Can Prevent It

- ✓ **Honest pre-admission counseling.** Say clearly what the program demands
- ✓ **All-in fee clarity** with no surprises
- ✓ **A structured orientation** in the first week
- ✓ **A named contact** for Nepali students and their families
- ✓ **Peer support**, such as senior students who guide newcomers
- ✓ **Academic support** for early difficulties
- ✓ **Regular check-ins** during the first semester
- ✓ **Clear grievance channels** for hostel and academic issues

---

## What a Good Recruitment Partner Does

- **Counsels honestly** before admission, including the difficult parts
- **Matches the student to the right program**, not just the available seat
- **Keeps in touch with families** after arrival
- **Flags problems early** to the college

Partners who earn trust protect your retention, not just your intake numbers.
