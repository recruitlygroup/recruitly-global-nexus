# Study in Georgia (2026/27): Complete List of Universities, Bachelor's, Master's & PhD Courses, Tuition Fees & Visa Process

**TL;DR:** Georgia offers internationally recognized, **100% English-taught** Bachelor's (36–72 months), Master's (12–48 months), and PhD (36 months) degrees across top institutions including **Alte University (AU)**, **University of Georgia (UG)**, **Ilia State University (ISU)**, **Georgian National University SEU**, **Caucasus University (CU)**, **Caucasus International University (CIU)**, **Georgian Technical University (GTU)**, and **Grigol Robakidze University (GRUNI)**. Annual tuition ranges from **USD 3,000 to USD 6,500** for most Bachelor's programs (and **GEL 12,000** at GTU), with Spring and Fall intakes open for international students. Students can also opt for an **all-inclusive living package at USD 9,000/year** covering three daily meals, accommodation with twice-weekly cleaning, WiFi, and utilities.

## 5. Step-by-Step Application & Student Visa Process

Our admissions team manages every milestone from initial submission to student visa approval:

1. **Apply Online:** Submit your academic transcripts and passport copy in PDF format.
2. **University Interview Scheduled:** Complete your online admissions interview with the university panel.
3. **Document Translation & Notarization:** Your educational documents are officially translated and notarized in Georgia.
4. **Offer Letter & Initial Payment:** Receive your conditional Offer Letter and settle the initial processing/application fee.
5. **Ministry of Education (MOE) Submission & Recognition:** Documents are submitted to Georgia's Ministry of Education for official foreign qualification recognition.
6. **Ministry Order & Official Acceptance Letter:** Once MOE issues the recognition and ministerial enrollment order to the university, your formal **Acceptance Letter** is released.
7. **Tuition Fee Payment & Visa Invitation Letter:** Pay your tuition fee to receive your official **University Invitation Letter**.
8. **Student Visa Issued:** Submit your visa application with full support (including legal assistance for visa extensions or appeals if ever required) and prepare for your flight to Georgia.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What intakes are available for universities in Georgia?
Most universities offer **Spring** and **Fall** intakes, while **Caucasus University (CU)** offers **Summer** and **Fall** intakes for its MBA, IT Management, and PhD programs.

### How much are the application fees for Georgian universities?
Application fees are **USD 200** for Alte University, University of Georgia (UG), and Ilia State University (ISU); **USD 300** for Georgian National University SEU, Caucasus University (CU), Caucasus International University (CIU), and Grigol Robakidze University (GRUNI); and **GEL 200** for Georgian Technical University (GTU).

### Is an all-inclusive food and hostel package available for international students?
Yes. Students can choose an all-inclusive living plan for **USD 9,000 per year** that covers accommodation, 3 meals daily, twice-weekly cleaning, WiFi, and all utility bills.

### How long does a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD degree take in Georgia?
Bachelor's degrees typically run **36 to 48 months** (3 to 4 years), Dentistry runs **60 months** (5 years), Medical Doctor (MBBS/MD) runs **72 months** (6 years), Master's programs run **12 to 24 months** (with Pharmaceutical Chemistry at UG running 48 months), and PhD programs run **36 months** (3 years).
