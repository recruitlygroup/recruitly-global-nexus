# Nursing Admission Requirements and Documents for Nepali Students in India

> **Need help preparing your documents? WhatsApp us: +977 974 3208282. Free for students.**

Missing documents delay admission and can make you lose a seat. Prepare early using this checklist.

---

## Typical Eligibility

- **Class 12 (+2) in Science** with subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
- **English** studied at school level
- **Minimum marks** set by the council and the college. Check the current requirement
- **Age limits** that apply to each program

> Rules differ by program, state, and year, and may differ for foreign students. Many colleges admit Nepali students without Indian entrance exams, but this depends on the college and state. **Confirm before applying.**

---

## Documents Checklist

- ✓ **Passport or citizenship certificate**, as required
- ✓ **Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates**
- ✓ **Equivalence documents**, if your board needs them
- ✓ **Character or migration certificate**, where required
- ✓ **Passport-size photographs**
- ✓ **Medical fitness certificate**, where required
- ✓ **Offer or admission letter** from the college
- ✓ **Fee receipts**, kept safely

---

## Tips

- **Keep name spellings the same** on all documents
- **Make clear scans and copies**
- **Check deadlines** early
- **Ask the college for its full document list** in writing
