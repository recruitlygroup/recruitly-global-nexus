## What is the Ausbildung Program?

Ausbildung is a formal German dual-vocational training system. Unlike traditional university courses, it combines classroom instruction at a vocational school (*Berufsschule*) with practical work experience at a host company (*Ausbildungsbetrieb*).

- **Tuition-free training.** Host companies sponsor your tuition, so you don't face heavy university tuition fees.
- **Earn while you learn.** Trainees receive a monthly stipend from day one to cover rent, food, and personal living expenses.
- **Program duration.** Typically runs between 2 to 3.5 years, depending on the field of study.
- **High employment rate.** Over 80% of trainees transition directly into full-time employment contracts upon graduation.

## Requirements for Candidates from Nepal

To qualify for an Ausbildung contract through our recruitment channels, candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:

### 1. Educational Qualification
- Minimum completion of **Grade 12 (10+2)** or equivalent qualification.
- A diploma or educational background in healthcare, hospitality, IT, or technical trades is highly advantageous.

### 2. Language Proficiency
- **German Language Skills:** Minimum **B1 level** certified by Goethe-Institut, ÖSD, or TELC.
- Specialized sectors such as Nursing or IT prefer or require **B2 level** for patient safety and technical communication.
- Strong communication skills in English are a plus.

### 3. Age & Background
- Recommended age between **18 and 30 years old**.
- A clean criminal record and verified academic transcripts.

### 4. Adaptability & Commitment
- A strong commitment to learning the German language and adapting to European workplace standards.

## Monthly Salary & Benefits

One of the greatest advantages of the Ausbildung route is financial independence. Host companies pay a monthly stipend (*Ausbildungsvergütung*), which increases with each completed year of training.

| Training Year | Average Monthly Stipend (Gross) |
|---|---|
| **1st Year** | €900 – €1,100 / month |
| **2nd Year** | €1,000 – €1,200 / month |
| **3rd Year** | €1,100 – €1,400 / month |

*Note: Actual stipends vary based on profession and location; sectors like Nursing and IT typically offer higher starting rates.*

### Additional Benefits
- **Subsidized Health & Social Insurance** covered through standard workplace contributions.
- **Pathway to Permanent Residency:** Graduates can secure long-term work visas and qualify for permanent residency after gaining post-graduation work experience.
