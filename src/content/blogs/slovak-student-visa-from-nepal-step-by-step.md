# Slovak Student Visa From Nepal: Step by Step

Nepali students often ask where to apply for a Slovak student visa, because Slovakia has **no embassy in Kathmandu**. This guide follows the Slovak Ministry of Foreign Affairs' published rules for applicants from Nepal. Rules change, so always check the embassy's current page before you book.

> **Verify first.** The embassy pages we used may not show the latest changes. Confirm each step with the Embassy of the Slovak Republic in New Delhi (mzv.sk/dilli-en) before you travel or pay.

## 1. Know where you apply

Citizens of Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and the Maldives apply at the **Embassy of the Slovak Republic in New Delhi**. For stays over 90 days you may either apply for temporary residence at the embassy or apply for a **national D visa** so that you can lodge the residence application in Slovakia. Your university will tell you which route applies.

## 2. Book an appointment by email

Appointments are requested by email to **cons.delhi@mzv.sk**. Include your name, passport number, intended travel date and the purpose of travel. The embassy confirms the date and time by email.

**You must attend in person, and agents cannot submit applications for you.** Be careful with anyone who promises to "handle" your Slovak visa without you.

## 3. Prepare and legalise your documents

According to the embassy, documents issued in **Nepal must first be legalised by Nepal's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and then superlegalised by the Slovak Embassy in New Delhi** before any other action such as copying or translation. Typical documents include:

- passport, and your admission decision and confirmation of study from the university,
- secondary school or Bachelor's certificates and transcripts,
- a police clearance certificate from Nepal,
- proof of funds and health insurance,
- passport photos.

Documents for the residence application must **not be older than 90 days** when you submit.

## 4. Timing

A national D visa application for the purpose of a residence application in Slovakia must be submitted **at least 30 days before your intended travel**, and the embassy states it decides **within 30 days**. Because legalisation takes time, start early. Your university may also need to recognise your earlier certificate before enrolment.

## 5. Short trips are different

Short Schengen visas (up to 90 days) for Nepal were handled by the Embassy of Switzerland in Kathmandu. That is for visits, not for studies, so confirm the current arrangement before relying on it.
