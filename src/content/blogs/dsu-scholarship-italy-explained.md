**"Study in Italy for free and get paid €7,000 a year" is the most repeated claim in Italian study-abroad marketing, and it is only partly true.** The DSU scholarship is real, it is generous, and it can cover most of a student's costs. But it is awarded through regional competitions, it depends on a document that takes months to prepare, and in some regions most eligible students receive no money at all.

This guide explains how DSU actually works for international nursing applicants: what you can receive, how the amount is calculated, what the ISEE Parificato is, which deadlines matter, and the risk almost nobody mentions. It is part of our **Study Nursing Abroad: Italy** series. For the full overview of the degree, admission and visa process, start with the [complete guide to studying nursing in Italy](/blog/study-nursing-in-italy).

Every region runs its own call each year. Calls for 2027/28 have not been published, so treat 2026/27 as a guide to the pattern, not a promise.

## What DSU Is

**DSU** stands for *Diritto allo Studio Universitario*, the right to university study. It is a system of financial support run not by universities but by **regional bodies**, such as DiSCo in Lazio, ER.GO in Emilia-Romagna, EDISU in Piedmont, DSU Toscana in Tuscany and ERSU in Sicily. Each body publishes an annual call (*bando*), sets its own deadlines and administers its own funds, using income ceilings and minimum amounts guided by national rules.

Depending on the region and your ranking, DSU can include:

- a **cash scholarship**, paid in instalments
- **free or subsidised canteen meals**
- a **place in a student residence** (separate ranking and often limited)
- **refund of the regional tax** and, depending on university rules, a **tuition exemption or reduction**
- contributions for international mobility and other benefits

You apply to the DSU body of the region where your **university** is located. If you study in Rome you apply to DiSCo, and if you study in Palermo you apply to ERSU Palermo.

> A source of confusion: **DSU is different from the ISEE-based tuition discount** that public universities give. The ISEE (or ISEE Parificato) determines your tuition bracket, and a separate DSU application can give you the scholarship on top. They use similar documents but they are separate processes. See our [cost guide](/blog/cost-of-studying-nursing-in-italy) for tuition.

## Four DSU Myths, Corrected

| Myth | What the official rules show |
|---|---|
| **"Every student from India, Pakistan or Bangladesh gets it."** | Nationality does not decide. Income, family documents, merit and available funds do. Some special documentation rules apply to students from particularly disadvantaged countries, but they still apply and are ranked. |
| **"The scholarship is €7,000 for everyone."** | The amount depends on your status (in-town, commuter or out-of-town), your ISEE and the region. In Lazio in 2026/27 the base figure ranged from about €2,290 to €6,571 in cash, plus meals. |
| **"If you meet the criteria you automatically get paid."** | Not always. Students who meet every requirement but run out of funds are classed as **eligible but not beneficiary** (*idoneo non beneficiario*). |
| **"You apply after you are admitted and enrolled."** | In Lazio's 2026/27 call, the application window closed on 22 July, **before** the September nursing admission tests. Deadlines differ by region, so check early. |

## What You Can Receive

The most reliable numbers come from DiSCo Lazio's 2026/27 call. Its scholarship has two parts: a cash amount and **two free meals a day, valued at €600 a year**.

| Student type (Lazio, 2026/27) | Base cash amount | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| **In-town** (lives in the university's municipality) | €2,290.16 | 2 free daily meals (€600 value) |
| **Commuter** (lives within 50 km) | €3,590.71 | 2 free daily meals (€600 value) |
| **Out-of-town** | €6,571.11 | 2 free daily meals (€600 value) |

### Where the "€7,000" claim comes from

Italy's ministry sets minimum scholarship values that regional bodies must guarantee. For 2026/27, a ministerial decree reported in the Italian press put the out-of-town minimum at **€7,171.11** and the commuter minimum at **€4,190.71**. In Lazio, that out-of-town figure is made up of €6,571.11 in cash plus the €600 value of the meals. So "up to about €7,000" is a fair description of the **maximum-type** package for an out-of-town student, but **not** a guaranteed cash payment, and not available to every applicant.

### What international students are treated as

Under DiSCo's call, an international student who lives abroad and whose household lives at least 50% abroad is **automatically classified as out-of-town**, with no requirement to sign a rental contract. That gives students from abroad access to the highest category, as long as they are not enrolled in an online university or a mostly distance-learning course.

### Increases and reductions

DiSCo's 2026/27 call adds or reduces the amount in these cases:

- **+15%** for students with a low ISEE, at roughly €14,170 or below (the call's text and table quote slightly different figures, so check the current call)
- **Full amount** up to an ISEE of about €18,893, then **gradual reduction** to as little as half the base amount as your ISEE rises toward the ceiling
- **+10%** for students with a certified disability of 66% or more
- **50% of the grant** for students enrolled one year beyond the standard length of the course

### What about the regional tax?

Every student at a Lazio university pays a regional tax for the right to study (€140 plus a €16 stamp, so €156, as quoted for Lazio). DiSCo's call says scholarship beneficiaries have the regional tax **refunded automatically**.

## How the Amount Is Calculated: Lazio 2026/27

DiSCo's ISEE bands for the out-of-town cash scholarship in 2026/27:

| ISEE (or ISEEUP) | Out-of-town | Commuter | In-town |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to about €14,170 | €7,556.78 (115%) | €4,129.32 | €2,633.68 |
| About €14,170 to €18,893 | €6,571.11 (100%) | €3,590.71 | €2,290.16 |
| Above €18,893, up to the ceiling | Reduced gradually to a minimum of €3,285.60 | Down to €1,795.36 | Down to €1,145.08 |

All three include the two free daily meals. Meals cannot be converted to cash except in special cases where no accessible canteen exists.

**Note on other regions:** Regions publish their own amounts. For example, a 2026/27 summary for the Marche region lists €7,171.11 for out-of-town, €4,190.71 for commuters and €2,890.16 for in-town students. Do not assume Lazio's figures apply elsewhere.

## The Income Test: ISEE and ISPE Limits

To qualify you must be **under both** an income ceiling (ISEE) and an assets ceiling (ISPE). Exceeding either one excludes you.

| Region and body (2026/27) | ISEE ceiling |
|---|---|
| Lazio (DiSCo) | €28,339.88 (ISPE €61,608.48) |
| Tuscany (DSU Toscana) | €27,000 |
| Lombardy (e.g. Politecnico di Milano) | €26,887.93 |
| Piedmont (EDISU) | €26,306.25 |

DiSCo's housing place has a higher ceiling (€40,000 ISEE) than the scholarship.

### What goes into the calculation

For Lazio's 2026/27 call, the ISEE is calculated from household income earned in **2024** plus 20% of assets held at 31 December 2024, divided by a parameter that reflects the number of family members.

### Independent students

Students can only be treated as independent from their parents if they have lived apart in a home not owned by a family member for at least two years **and** have at least €9,000 of declared work income per year. Most first-year international applicants will be assessed on their family's income.

## The ISEE Parificato for Students Abroad

Italian families use the standard ISEE, which is computed from Italian tax data. A family living outside Italy cannot, so the system uses the **ISEE Parificato** (called ISEEUP in DiSCo's call), an equivalent indicator built from your home-country documents.

### What DiSCo requires (2026/27)

- File it at a **CAF recognised by DiSCo** (CAFs are tax assistance centres, and DiSCo publishes a list). Recognised CAFs file the declaration for students **free of charge**.
- Bring documentation **issued by the Italian diplomatic authorities in your country**, **translated into Italian**, covering:
  - family composition (names and dates of birth of each member)
  - **gross annual income of each family member in 2024**, with any lack of income stated explicitly
  - real estate owned at 31 December 2024, including surface area, and movable assets (or an explicit statement that there are none)
  - rent paid for the family home
- **Self-declarations, affidavits and sworn statements are not accepted.**
- Foreign currency is converted at the **Bank of Italy's exchange rate**.
- Total household income **cannot be declared as zero**, and the figures must be consistent with the financial requirements for residence permits.
- The Italian diplomatic documentation may need to be **legalised by the Italian Prefecture**, and costs for corrections or integrations are paid by the student and are not reimbursed.

### Special rule for students from certain countries

The call identifies students from "particularly disadvantaged countries" listed under a ministerial decree dated 24 February 2026 and in an annex to the call. For them, income is assessed through documentation from the Italian diplomatic representation certifying that the student does not belong to a high-income or high-status family. They still need to file the ISEEUP at a recognised CAF. **Check the current annex to see whether your country is on the list.**

### Deadline

For 2026/27, students not residing in Italy had to file the ISEEUP by **10 December 2026**. Missing that date means exclusion, and later documents are not accepted even if they are originals.

### How early should you start?

**At least six months before the deadline.** Obtaining embassy-issued family and income documentation, translating it and arranging legalisation are the slowest parts of the process, and the result must reflect 2024 incomes for the 2026/27 call. Income year changes with each call, so confirm which year the next call uses.

## Eligible Does Not Mean Funded

This is the part most guides leave out.

DiSCo's call defines three outcomes: **beneficiary** (receives the benefit), **eligible** (meets every requirement but is not funded because money or places ran out) and **excluded**. Benefits are only paid to beneficiaries.

### How first-year rankings work

In DiSCo's 2026/27 call:

- First-year rankings are **in ascending order of income**: the lowest ISEE (or ISEEUP) ranks first, and ties go to older students.
- **EU and non-EU first-year students are ranked separately**, with resources shared in proportion to the number of regular applications from each group.
- Only **25% of the funds** are allocated to first-year students and 75% to students in later years.

So a low-income first-year applicant from abroad competes in a ranking of non-EU first-years, and the number of funded places depends on how many applicants there are and how much money the region has.

### A warning from Palermo

Palermo is one of the universities offering English-taught nursing. On 9 November 2026, a regional union statement, citing figures from a student union, said that among **9,956 eligible first-year students in ERSU Palermo's 2026/27 ranking, only 281 were assigned the scholarship, fewer than 3%**. This comes from an advocacy statement rather than an official publication, so verify against ERSU Palermo's own ranking. But it illustrates why "eligible" can still mean "no money".

### What this means for you

- **Do not plan your finances around DSU alone.** Treat it as upside, not as the base case.
- **Compare regions.** Coverage rates differ widely, so ask each regional body how many eligible students were funded in recent years.
- **Ask about housing separately.** Housing and scholarships can be ranked and funded independently.

## Merit Requirements and Payment Timing

The scholarship is not a one-off prize. Students must keep earning academic credits.

- **First-year students** must reach a minimum number of credits by a set date, which in Lazio's 2026/27 call was **10 August 2027**, to keep the scholarship. If they do not, benefits are revoked and, depending on the case, may have to be repaid.
- **Later years** require higher credit thresholds, and bonus credits can sometimes be used. In Lazio, merit is also part of the ranking formula for students after the first year.
- **Payment is staged.** A third-party scholarship guide reported that DiSCo pays first-year scholarships in three tranches, 20% by 10 November 2026, 30% by 27 December 2026 and the balance after credit requirements are met in 2027. Confirm the schedule in the current call.
- **Final rankings arrive late.** DiSCo published the definitive scholarship ranking on 12 October 2026 and the housing ranking on 16 September 2026.

**Practical implication:** you must fund your first months in Italy yourself. Deposits, rent, travel, and your first tuition instalment fall due before the scholarship arrives.

## The Deadline Problem for Nursing Applicants

Here is the timing trap, using Lazio's 2026/27 calendar:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 10 June to 22 July 2026 (12:00) | DiSCo application window, first phase |
| 30 July to 11 August 2026 | Second phase for corrections |
| 17 September 2026 | English-taught nursing admission test (Sapienza, Palermo) |
| 23 September 2026 | Sapienza ranking list published |
| 12 October 2026 | Definitive DiSCo scholarship ranking |
| 10 December 2026 | ISEEUP deadline for students abroad |
| 10 February 2027 | Deadline to complete university enrolment to keep the scholarship |

**The application closed before the nursing test took place.** In practice, you may have to apply for the scholarship **without knowing whether you will be admitted**, with enrolment confirmed afterwards, and with the ISEEUP due several months later.

Two details make this harder for applicants abroad:

1. **First-time access.** Students with only a foreign passport register on DiSCo's platform and must be **identified face to face at an authorised CAF**. Ask DiSCo how candidates who are not in Italy are expected to complete this before the application deadline.
2. **PEC address.** International students not resident in Italy must provide a certified email address (PEC) or an elected domicile, otherwise payments are suspended.

Other regions run on different calendars. A 2026/27 overview reported that regional calls opened between June and July and closed between late July and 30 September, and some universities set their own separate ISEE Parificato deadlines in the autumn. **Never assume the Lazio calendar applies to another region.**

For 2027/28, one guide expects calls to open from about June 2027. We will update this post when the official calls appear.

## Other Rules That Can Cost You the Scholarship

Based on DiSCo's 2026/27 call, these are typical reasons for exclusion or revocation:

- Missing the application deadline, or submitting an incomplete application
- Not filing the ISEEUP at a **recognised** CAF by the deadline, or filing it for the wrong income year
- **Not completing university enrolment** by 10 February 2027 (10 December 2026 for housing)
- Not uploading your **residence permit** or the proof of application for it by the deadline
- Receiving **another scholarship** for the same year. DiSCo's scholarship is incompatible with scholarships from other public or private bodies, including Ministry of Foreign Affairs scholarships and university merit scholarships, with limited exceptions such as Erasmus and the 150-hour student work grants
- Not communicating changes (course, university, residence, other grants) within the deadlines, usually 30 days, through the correct form
- **False declarations**, which lead to exclusion and repayment
- Failing to enter a **bank account** (an Italian or SEPA IBAN) within six months of being awarded the scholarship
- Withdrawing from your studies before the date set in the call

DiSCo's call also says it can check declarations with other authorities **even after the final ranking**, and revoke the benefit if inconsistencies are found.

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply

1. **Choose your university first,** because it decides which regional body and call apply.
2. **Download the current call** (the English version exists for some regions, including Lazio) and read the deadlines and the section for international students.
3. **Begin the ISEE Parificato documents immediately:** family composition, income and assets certificates from your country, issued or certified through the Italian embassy or consulate, then translated.
4. **Identify the recognised CAFs** and book appointments. Ask how students outside Italy complete identification and filing.
5. **Register on the regional platform** and, if required, complete identity verification.
6. **Submit the application** by the first deadline, including any second-phase corrections.
7. **File the ISEEUP** at a recognised CAF by the deadline in the call.
8. **Complete university enrolment** and upload your residence permit documentation on time.
9. **Add your bank details** and your PEC or elected domicile.
10. **Track your position** in the personal account. Communications there count as official notification.

## How to Budget Without Relying on DSU

- **Model three scenarios:** full scholarship, partial scholarship and no scholarship. Plan to be able to cover the third.
- **Keep a buffer** for the first three to four months, because payments arrive after you have paid rent and fees.
- **Include one-off costs:** test fees, document legalisation and translation, visa and permit costs, insurance, vaccinations and health checks.
- **Compare cities,** since housing is usually the biggest cost after tuition.
- **Apply to more than one university** and compare funding as well as admission chances.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Believing "free education" headlines.** DSU is competitive and partial in many regions.
2. **Starting the ISEE Parificato too late.** Embassy and translation steps can take months.
3. **Using the wrong income year or an unrecognised CAF.** The call defines both, and errors can mean exclusion.
4. **Waiting for admission results before researching DSU.** Deadlines can come first.
5. **Skipping the residence permit upload** or missing the enrolment date.
6. **Stacking scholarships** that are declared incompatible.
7. **Paying a "guaranteed scholarship" service.** No agent can guarantee DSU, and the application is free of charge for the student.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a DSU scholarship?

A regional right-to-study scholarship for students at Italian universities. It can include a cash grant, free meals, housing and a refund of the regional tax, and it is awarded by the regional body based on income and merit.

### Can international students apply for DSU?

Yes. Non-EU students living abroad can apply, using the ISEE Parificato to document their family's income and assets.

### How much is a DSU scholarship?

It depends on the region, your status and your ISEE. In Lazio in 2026/27, the cash scholarship ranged from about €2,290 (in-town) to €6,571 (out-of-town) at the base level, plus two free daily meals valued at €600, with increases for low-income students.

### Is DSU guaranteed if I meet the requirements?

No. Students who meet the requirements but are not funded are classed as eligible but not beneficiary. In Palermo, a union statement reported fewer than 3% of eligible first-year students received the scholarship in 2026/27.

### What is the ISEE Parificato?

An equivalent-income indicator for students whose families live outside Italy. It is calculated at a recognised CAF from documents issued or certified by the Italian diplomatic authorities in your country.

### Can I get DSU for a private university?

It depends on the regional call and the university's inclusion. Check the call's list of covered universities and the private university's own scholarship rules.

### Can I combine DSU with another scholarship?

Often not. DiSCo's call treats most other scholarships, including MAECI and university merit scholarships, as incompatible, with limited exceptions.

### Do I have to repay the scholarship?

You may have to if it is revoked, for example for missing merit requirements, false declarations or withdrawing, as set out in the call.

### When will 2027/28 calls open?

Not yet published as of 10 November 2026. One guide expects calls from about June 2027, but each region sets its own dates.

### Can an agency apply for me?

Recruitly Group (@recruitlygroup) and other advisers can help you organise documents and track deadlines, but the application is made by you through the regional platform, and no one can guarantee an outcome.
