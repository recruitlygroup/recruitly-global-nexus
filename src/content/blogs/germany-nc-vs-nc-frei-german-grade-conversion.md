# NC vs NC-Frei in Germany: Admission Types and How to Convert Your GPA

Shortlisting programs on the **DAAD portal**? Next to every degree you will see one of two terms: **Zulassungsfrei (NC-Frei)** or **Zulassungsbeschränkt (NC)**. Knowing the difference, and your converted German grade, helps you build a smarter shortlist.

---

## NC-Frei vs. NC: What Do They Mean?

### Zulassungsfrei (NC-Frei / Open Admission)
- **No competitive GPA ranking** against other applicants
- If you meet the **formal entry requirements** (relevant Bachelor's credits plus IELTS or TestDaF), you are admitted

### Zulassungsbeschränkt (NC / Restricted Admission)
- **Seats are limited**
- Applicants are ranked by their **converted GPA**, and sometimes by aptitude tests or interviews
- Only top-ranked applicants receive offers

> **Smart strategy:** Include **2–3 NC-Frei programs** on your shortlist as safe backups alongside competitive NC programs.

---

## The German Grading Scale (1.0 Is Best)

Germany uses an inverted scale from **1.0 to 5.0**, where lower is better.

| German grade | Meaning | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 – 1.5 | Sehr gut (Very good) | Competitive for top NC courses |
| 1.6 – 2.5 | Gut (Good) | Meets the 2.5 cutoff many Master's programs use |
| 2.6 – 3.5 | Befriedigend (Satisfactory) | Target NC-Frei courses without strict GPA cutoffs |
| 3.6 – 4.0 | Ausreichend (Pass) | Minimum pass |

## How to Calculate Your German Grade (Modified Bavarian Formula)

**German Grade = [(Max Grade − Your Grade) ÷ (Max Grade − Min Pass Grade)] × 3 + 1**

### Worked example
You scored **3.2 out of 4.0**, and your university's minimum pass grade is **2.0**:

`[(4.0 − 3.2) ÷ (4.0 − 2.0)] × 3 + 1 = 2.2`

Your estimated German grade is **2.2**.

> **Note:** This is an estimate. The university or uni-assist makes the official conversion, and some universities use their own methods.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does NC-Frei mean?
**Zulassungsfrei** means open admission. There is no GPA ranking, only formal entry requirements.

### What does NC mean in Germany?
**Zulassungsbeschränkt** means restricted admission. Limited seats are given to the best-ranked applicants.

### Is 2.5 a good German grade?
Yes. 2.5 is "Gut" and meets the cutoff many Master's programs use, though requirements vary by program.
