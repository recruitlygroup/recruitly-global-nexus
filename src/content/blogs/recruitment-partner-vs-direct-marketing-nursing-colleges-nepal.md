# Recruitment Partner vs. Direct Marketing: What's Best for Nursing Colleges?

Some colleges try to reach Nepali students directly through ads, social media, and visits. Others use a local partner. Both can work, but they suit different situations.

---

## Direct Marketing: Pros and Cons

**Pros**
- Full control over your message
- No commission to a partner

**Cons**
- **Upfront spend** on ads and travel with uncertain results
- No local presence for counseling and trust-building
- Hard to verify students and documents remotely
- Families often want **face-to-face guidance**
- Slow to build credibility in new districts

---

## Using a Recruitment Partner: Pros and Cons

**Pros**
- Local presence and family trust
- Counseling, shortlisting, and document checks handled for you
- **Pay for results** through commission
- One point of accountability

**Cons**
- Commission applies
- Quality varies, so **partner choice matters**

---

## Which Is Right for You?

| Your situation | Better option |
|---|---|
| Strong existing brand in Nepal and local staff | Direct marketing may work |
| New to Nepal or building a pipeline | Partner |
| Multiple programs and seasonal intakes | Partner |
| Limited marketing budget | Partner |
| Concerned about drop-outs and verification | Partner |
