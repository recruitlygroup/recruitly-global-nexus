# Study Entrepreneurship at GEA College in Ljubljana, Slovenia

Do you want a **practical business degree in Europe**? **GEA College, Faculty of Entrepreneurship** in **Ljubljana** offers a **three-year Bachelor's programme in Entrepreneurship** with a strong focus on real business projects. This guide covers programme details, fees, entry requirements, the application process, and how **Recruitly Group** supports international applicants.

> **Please verify:** The information below comes from GEA College's published programme pages. The pages do not clearly state the **language of instruction**, so confirm with GEA College whether your intake is taught in English. The programme is listed as **part-time**, so also check how this affects any **visa or residence permit** you need.

---

## Why Choose GEA College?

GEA College is a **private higher education institution** in Ljubljana. The Faculty of Entrepreneurship was established in **1996**. GEA describes its approach as practical: students work on **real business challenges for actual companies**, meet **experienced entrepreneurs**, and can use the college's **Career Center** and **startup incubator**.

**Key points:**

- Practice-led study with **guest lectures and company projects**
- A **Career Center** offering counselling and information on internships and jobs
- An **incubator** for developing business ideas
- Support from an **international office**

---

## Programme at a Glance: Entrepreneurship (Bachelor's)

- **Type:** Undergraduate professional degree programme
- **Duration:** 3 years, **180 ECTS**
- **Degree:** Bachelor of Economics, BSc (Economics)
- **Study mode:** Part-time, traditional on-campus study (weekday afternoons and Saturday mornings for part-time students), with an online learning platform available 24/7
- **Graduation:** Thesis (12 ECTS) or final exam (12 ECTS)
- **Topics:** Entrepreneurship, marketing, management, and other business fields, with emphasis on innovation, creativity, communication, and leadership

### Who it suits

Ambitious students who want to **start a business**, take over a **family business**, or move into **project or department management**.

GEA's Faculty of Entrepreneurship also lists other undergraduate programmes (Entrepreneurship and Management, Digital Marketing, Information and Cyber Security) and postgraduate programmes. Check each programme page for its language of instruction.

## Tuition Fees

According to GEA College's published fee table:

| | Per year (3 years) |
|---|---|
| **Slovenia, EU, EEA, and ex-Yugoslav non-EU countries** | €5,950 |
| **Non-EU citizens** | €7,350 |
| **Enrolment fee** | €270 per year |

**Important for foreign students:** GEA states that foreign students pay **for the whole programme in advance at the start of the first year**, at the first-year fee level. Confirm the exact amount and payment schedule with GEA before applying.

GEA also offers a **€500 credit** for students who pass all subjects in the first exam period with an average grade of 8.5, and **€700** for students with athlete status.

## Entry Requirements and Selection

You can enrol with:

- A **general matura**
- A **vocational matura**
- A four-year secondary school programme completed before 1 June 1995

If places are limited, selection is based on **matura results (60%)** and **grades in the third and fourth years of secondary school (40%)**.

**Foreign secondary education** must go through **recognition**, which the application triggers automatically. GEA states the procedure takes up to two months from a complete application. Documents include a copy of your certificate, **certified Slovene translations** of your diploma and transcripts, a short chronological description of your education, and **proof of B2 language proficiency** in the language of the programme.

---

## How and When to Apply

Applications are submitted through the national **eVŠ portal**, and GEA's page says the application is then **printed, signed, and posted** with the supporting documents.

GEA publishes the call for enrolment **each year on 31 January** for the next academic year. For 2026, the published deadlines were:

- **EU citizens:** 18 February to 18 March, and 20 to 27 August
- **Non-EU citizens:** 18 February to 17 July, and 31 July to 4 September

These dates have passed, so check GEA's website for the 2027/2028 call.
