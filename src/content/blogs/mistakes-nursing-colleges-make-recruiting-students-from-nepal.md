# 7 Costly Mistakes Nursing Colleges Make When Recruiting Students From Nepal

Nepal can transform your admissions, or drain your budget. Most problems come from the same avoidable mistakes.

---

## 1. Starting Too Late
Families need time to decide and gather documents. **Start months before your intake.** See the [admission timeline](/blog/nepal-to-india-nursing-admission-timeline/).

## 2. Unclear Fee and Hostel Information
Hidden or vague costs make families hesitate or drop out later. Publish **total costs clearly.**

## 3. Working With Agents Who Charge Families
Agents who charge students create pressure, misleading promises, and complaints that land on your college. Choose a partner who **never charges families.**

## 4. Vague Commission Terms
Disputes over who earned what waste time and trust. Agree on the trigger, amount, timeline, and refund terms in writing. Read the [commission guide](/blog/flexible-commission-student-recruitment-nursing-colleges-india/).

## 5. Skipping Document Verification
Incomplete or inconsistent documents cause delays and rejections. Verify files before admission.

## 6. Using Many Agents for the Same Student
Competing agents create duplicate claims and unclear accountability. **Choose one accountable partner.**

## 7. No Arrival and Retention Plan
Students who arrive without orientation, hostel clarity, or a contact person are more likely to leave. Plan the first month carefully.

---

## The Simple Fix

Work with a partner who counsels honestly, verifies documents, agrees terms in writing, and stays reachable after arrival.
