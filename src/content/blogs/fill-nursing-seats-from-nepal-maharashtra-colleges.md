# How Maharashtra Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Maharashtra, home to Mumbai and Pune, is one of India's biggest education and healthcare hubs. It attracts students nationwide, so colleges here need a **clear, trustworthy pitch** to reach Nepali families.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Maharashtra

- **Major education hubs** in Pune, Mumbai, and Nagpur
- **Large hospital networks** for clinical exposure
- **Hindi widely understood**, with English common in institutions
- **A wide range of nursing programs** and institution types

## What Maharashtra Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **Honest living costs.** Big cities are expensive, so be transparent
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** with hostel, clinical, and travel costs
- ✓ **Hostel safety** and a named contact for Nepali families

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Higher living costs** in major cities
- **Strong competition** from many institutions
- **Long distance** from Nepal
