# Nepal to India Nursing Admission Timeline: Plan Your Intake

Colleges often ask, "When should we start recruiting from Nepal?" The answer: **earlier than you think.** Students and families need time to decide, gather documents, and arrange finances. Here is how the cycle works.

---

## The 7 Stages

### Stage 1: Program and Seat Planning
You confirm seats, eligibility, fees, hostel availability, and intake dates. **Clear information up front prevents confusion later.**

### Stage 2: Outreach and Counseling
Your partner reaches students and families, explains your program honestly, and filters for eligibility.

### Stage 3: Student Shortlisting
Candidates whose profile and budget match your program are shortlisted.

### Stage 4: Application and Document Collection
Applications and documents are completed and checked. See our [documents checklist](/blog/documents-checklist-admitting-nepali-students-nursing-india/).

### Stage 5: Admission Decision and Offer
Your admissions team reviews files and issues offers.

### Stage 6: Fee Payment and Confirmation
Seat confirmation with clear fee terms in writing.

### Stage 7: Travel, Arrival and Orientation
Travel planning, hostel check-in, and orientation, with a contact for families.

---

## What Slows Admissions Down

- **Incomplete or inconsistent documents**
- **Late fee clarity** that makes families hesitate
- **Eligibility confusion** between programs
- **Slow replies** from the college side
- **Starting recruitment too close to the deadline**

---

## How to Fill Seats Faster

- ✓ **Begin outreach several months before your intake**
- ✓ **Publish clear fee and hostel details**
- ✓ **Assign one admissions contact** for Nepal applications
- ✓ **Use one partner** who verifies files before submission
