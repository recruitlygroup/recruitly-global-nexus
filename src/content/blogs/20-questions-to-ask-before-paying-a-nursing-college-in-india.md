# 20 Questions to Ask Before Paying a Nursing College in India

> **Want us to ask these with you? WhatsApp: +977 974 3208282. Free for students.**

Copy these questions and send them to every college on your list. A good college will answer clearly and in writing.

---

## Recognition (1 to 5)

1. Is my exact program approved by the Indian Nursing Council for this year?
2. Is the college recognised by the state nursing council?
3. Which university or board is the college affiliated with?
4. Can I have copies of all approval letters?
5. Is there any pending case or notice about the college's approval?

## Registration and Career (6 to 8)

6. Can graduates register with a nursing council in India? Under what conditions?
7. Have Nepali graduates from your college registered in Nepal? Can I speak to them?
8. What do you advise for Nepal Nursing Council requirements?

## Fees (9 to 13)

9. What is the total cost for the full program, including hostel and food?
10. Can fees increase during the course?
11. What is the payment schedule?
12. What is the refund policy if I leave?
13. Are there any extra charges not listed?

## Training (14 to 16)

14. Which hospitals do students train in?
15. How many students are in each clinical group?
16. Is the internship included, and where is it done?

## Life and Support (17 to 20)

17. What are the hostel rules, security, and food arrangements?
18. Who is my contact person, and how can my family reach them?
19. What orientation is given to new Nepali students?
20. Can I visit the campus, or have a video tour and speak with current students?

---

## Tip

Keep every answer **in writing.** If a college avoids a question, treat that as an answer.
