## Questions to Ask About Fees

- ✓ Can fees **increase** during the course?
- ✓ What is the **payment schedule**?
- ✓ What is the **refund policy** if I leave?
- ✓ Are there **extra charges** not in the brochure?
- ✓ Is the fee the same for Nepali and Indian students?

> Fees vary widely between government and private colleges, between states, and between years. Always get the current fee sheet in writing.

---

## Hidden Cost Traps

- ❌ A low tuition fee with high hostel and "other" charges
- ❌ Pay-now discounts that push you to decide fast
- ❌ No written refund policy
- ❌ Fees quoted in cash with no receipt
