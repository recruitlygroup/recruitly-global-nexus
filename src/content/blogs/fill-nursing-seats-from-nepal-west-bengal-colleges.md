# How West Bengal Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

West Bengal borders eastern Nepal, and the Darjeeling and Siliguri region has a large Nepali-speaking community. For Nepali families, that adds **familiarity, accessibility, and trust** to a state already known for education and healthcare.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider West Bengal

- **Border region** with easy access from eastern Nepal
- **Nepali-speaking communities** in the north of the state
- **Kolkata as a major education and healthcare hub**
- **Siliguri as a regional gateway** for students from Nepal

## What West Bengal Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and clinical costs
- ✓ **Clinical training hospitals** and exposure
- ✓ **A named contact** and orientation for Nepali students

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Competition** from many Kolkata and north Bengal institutions
- **Big-city living costs** in Kolkata
