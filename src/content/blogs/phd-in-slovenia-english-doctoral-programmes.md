# PhD in Slovenia: English-Taught Doctoral Programmes Guide

Slovenia is a small country with a strong research profile, and it lists **more than 30 doctoral (third-cycle) programmes that can be studied in English**. This guide shows where you can study, which fields are available, what doctoral tuition typically costs, and how **Recruitly Group** supports your application.

> **Please verify:** The programme list used here comes from the official *Study in Slovenia* portal and was published for the **2024/2025 academic year**. The list changes yearly, and the availability of English instruction should be confirmed with each institution.

---

## Why Do a PhD in Slovenia?

- **Affordable tuition:** Study in Slovenia reports that doctoral tuition typically ranges from about **€2,000 to €6,000 per year**, depending on the programme
- **Wide choice:** More than 30 doctoral programmes in English, across about nine institutions
- **Research focus:** Public universities, graduate schools, and research-oriented institutions
- **Central European location:** Easy travel across Europe

Note that **doctoral students generally pay tuition**, even in cases where EU students are exempt at the Bachelor's or Master's level.

## Entry Requirements and Application Process

Requirements differ by programme, so read the call for enrolment of your chosen institution. In general:

- Applications for study in Slovenia are submitted online through the **eVŠ portal**
- You upload **scanned supporting documents**, and you must monitor the email you registered
- **EU citizens** may apply for places intended for Slovenian citizens, while **non-EU citizens** apply for places for foreigners
- **English proficiency** and any additional requirements (research proposal, supervisor contact, interview) are set by each programme
- A foreign degree may need **recognition**; the Study in Slovenia site has a dedicated page on this

## Funding and Scholarships

Most scholarship calls listed on the Study in Slovenia site target **master's studies for Western Balkan citizens** or Slovenians abroad. For a PhD, ask each institution about **research-project funding and fee reductions**.
