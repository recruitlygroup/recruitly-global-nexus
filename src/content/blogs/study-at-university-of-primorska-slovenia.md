# Study at the University of Primorska, Slovenia

The University of Primorska (UP) is a public university in Koper, Slovenia. In the 2026/27 academic year it offers **22 study programs fully in English** across Bachelor's, Master's and doctoral levels. This guide covers programs, application windows, costs and documents for international students.

> **Important:** every date and fee below comes from the university's **2026/27** call and price list. The 2027/28 call had not been published at the time of writing, so confirm dates before you plan.

---

## Why the University of Primorska stands out

**22 English-taught programs** is the headline. That is 10 at Bachelor's level, 6 at Master's level and 6 at PhD level. Students from outside the EU can study in English without learning Slovene first.

- **Tuition-free** for citizens of Slovenia, the EU, the EEA, and Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Kosovo.
- **Non-EU Bachelor's tuition** ranges from **€2,500 to €3,850 per year**, depending on the program.
- **Limited seats:** the 2026/27 call announced only **88 Bachelor's places for non-EU citizens**, of which 74 were full-time.

## Application windows (non-EU citizens, 2026/27 call)

| Level | Window 1 | Window 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's | 18 Feb – 18 Mar 2026 | 20 – 27 Jul 2026 |
| Master's | 8 Apr – 3 Jun 2026 | 20 – 27 Aug 2026 |

The second window opens only if places remain. Enrolment in Master's programs had to be completed by **30 September 2026**.

Applications are submitted online through Slovenia's **eVŠ portal**. Candidates can submit up to three applications for Master's programs.

---

## What it costs

### Tuition fees

- **Bachelor's:** €2,500 to €3,850 per academic year, depending on the program.
- **Master's in Management:** €4,300 per academic year.
- Other Master's fees are in the university price list for 2026/27. Check the exact figure for your program.

### Other charges

- **Interview fee:** €200, paid by card within five days of applying. It applies to citizens of countries whose nationals need a visa to cross the EU's external borders. It is **non-refundable**, whatever the outcome.
- **Enrolment fee:** charged each year. For the Faculty of Management it is €40.40 for 2026/27. Other faculties set their own.
- **Application fee:** the official call does not list one.

---

## The mandatory interview

Slovenian law requires an interview before an application from a visa-required country is processed further. The interview is held in **English on Zoom** with a university committee. Results arrive within three working days. Passing the interview does not guarantee admission: your application still goes through ranking.

---

## Documents and recognition

- Scanned documents are uploaded through the eVŠ portal.
- The exact list depends on the faculty and program and is stated in each faculty's call.
- If your diploma is from outside Slovenia, your application also counts as a request to **recognise your foreign education**.
- Foreign documents need an **apostille** if your country is a Hague Convention member, or full legalisation otherwise. A few countries need neither.

## FAQ

### Do I need Slovene to study at the University of Primorska?
Not for the 22 English-taught programs.

### Is there an application fee?
The official call lists none. Visa-required nationalities pay a €200 interview fee.

### Which programs have the lowest tuition?
Bachelor's tuition starts at €2,500 per year. See the university price list for your exact program.
