# Documents Checklist for Hiring Nepali Workers in Romania

Incomplete paperwork is the number one cause of hiring delays. Use this checklist to prepare early.

> **Important:** Requirements change and vary by role and sector. Always confirm the current list with the relevant Romanian authorities or your recruitment partner.

---

## Employer / Company Documents

- ✓ Company registration and proof of good standing
- ✓ Authorization of the person signing for the company
- ✓ Job offer or employment contract (role, salary, hours, duration)
- ✓ Proof of accommodation arrangements
- ✓ Proof of tax and social contribution compliance, where required
- ✓ Any sector approvals or licenses required for your industry

---

## Worker Documents

- ✓ Valid passport with sufficient validity
- ✓ Passport-size photos to specification
- ✓ Educational and skill certificates
- ✓ Experience letters or proof of past work
- ✓ Police clearance certificate
- ✓ Medical examination results and health insurance, as required
- ✓ Driving license or professional qualifications for relevant roles
- ✓ Signed employment contract

---

## Translation and Legalization

Foreign documents often need **official translation** and may need **legalization or apostille**. Plan time for this, because it is a common source of delay.

---

## Nepal-Side Processing

Foreign employment from Nepal involves its own approvals and procedures. A partner familiar with them prevents rejections.

---

## Tips to Avoid Delays

- **Check passport validity** before selection
- **Use consistent name spellings** across all documents
- **Keep scans clear and complete**
- **Submit everything together** rather than in pieces
- **Assign one person** to track the file
