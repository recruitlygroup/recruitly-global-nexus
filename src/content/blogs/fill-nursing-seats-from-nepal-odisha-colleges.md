# How Odisha Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Odisha is an eastern coastal state with a growing education sector, centered on Bhubaneswar. It is within reasonable reach of eastern Nepal by rail and offers colleges room to **compete on value and trust.**

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Odisha

- **Bhubaneswar as a growing education and healthcare hub**
- **Good rail links** with eastern India
- **Hindi and English** commonly understood in institutions
- **Potentially moderate living costs**

## What Odisha Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and clinical costs
- ✓ **Clinical training hospitals** and exposure
- ✓ **A named contact** and clear travel guidance

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Distance** compared with border states
- **Local language** in daily life outside campus
