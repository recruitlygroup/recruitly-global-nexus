# After Graduation in Georgia (2026/27): Post-Study Options, Work Permits, Returning Home & Next Steps

**TL;DR:** Completing your degree in Georgia is a major milestone, and your **Student Temporary Residence Permit** is tied to your active studies rather than serving as an automatic long-term post-study or work permit. Graduates generally choose among **returning home** (to enter the job market or complete home-country licensing/equivalency exams), **pursuing higher study** (such as a Master's or PhD), or **seeking lawful employment/residency** in Georgia—where securing a qualifying job offer and meeting Georgia's **Law on Labour Migration** right-to-work and residence permit rules is essential before starting paid work. Planning ahead before your final semester helps avoid status gaps, compliance mistakes, and rushed decisions.

## Key Post-Graduation Pathways for International Students

Once your final exams and graduation requirements are approaching, map out which route fits your degree, career goals, and legal eligibility:

* **Staying to Work (Subject to Job Offer & Permit Rules):** Remaining in Georgia for employment generally requires securing a qualifying job offer and completing the applicable right-to-work / work permit and residence permit steps rather than relying on an expiring student card.
* **Continuing to Higher Study:** Graduates who wish to advance academically can explore **Master's** or **PhD** programs (including research/doctoral degrees typically spanning three to five years), making sure admission and residence timelines align cleanly.
* **Returning Home or Moving Elsewhere:** Many graduates return to their home country—or apply for opportunities/further study elsewhere—to build their career or complete country-specific licensing, internship, or credential recognition requirements.

## Immigration & Work Compliance After Finishing Studies

Do not leave post-graduation status until the last week of your final semester:

* **Student Residence Permit Is Study-Specific:** A study residence permit exists for active study at an authorized institution—once your studies end or your status changes, you cannot simply treat a student permit as an open-ended work or stay pass.
* **Right-to-Work & Employer Filings:** Under Georgia's **Law on Labour Migration** (effective **1 March 2026**), paid work or self-employment generally requires proper right-to-work authorization in addition to eligible residency/visa status, with strict fines for non-compliance.
* **Avoid Overstaying:** Track your legal stay and permit validity dates carefully so you never risk overstay penalties or complications with future travel or applications.

## Special Considerations for Medical, Engineering, IT & Business Graduates

Your post-graduation checklist depends heavily on your field of study:

* **Medicine & Dentistry Graduates:** Clinical practice in most countries requires passing relevant licensing or screening examinations, completing required internships/clinical training, and meeting home-country medical council rules.
* **IT, Engineering & Business Graduates:** Prepare a strong portfolio, internships/projects, transcripts, and diploma documentation early so you are ready whether you pursue roles in Georgia, back home, or internationally.
* **Document Legalization & Apostille / Attestation:** Keep certified copies of your diploma, academic transcript/diploma supplement, and identity records ready in case an employer, licensing body, or university abroad requests verification or legalization.

## Smart Pre-Graduation Checklist (3–6 Months Before Finishing)

1. **Confirm Graduation & Transcript Timeline:** Verify when your degree certificate, transcript, and ECTS records will be issued by your university.
2. **Audit Your Immigration & Stay Dates:** Check when your current **Temporary Residence Permit** or legal stay expires and map out your next legal step in advance.
3. **Prepare Career or Licensing Applications:** Update your CV/portfolio, request reference letters from faculty/supervisors, and check licensing or postgraduate deadlines.
4. **Settle Housing, Bills & Banking:** Plan apartment notice periods, utility/internet closures, bank accounts, and shipping/luggage arrangements cleanly.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I stay in Georgia automatically after graduating on a student residence permit?
No—a student residence permit is tied to your studies, so transitioning to work, further study, or departure requires following the appropriate legal and immigration steps rather than relying on automatic extension.

### Do I need a job offer and work authorization to work in Georgia after graduating?
Yes—in most cases, working in Georgia requires securing a job offer and meeting Georgia's right-to-work / work permit and residence permit rules before starting paid work.

### What documents should I collect from my university before leaving Georgia?
Make sure you obtain your official degree/graduation confirmation, full academic transcripts, and any required verification/translation records before closing out university and housing affairs.

### Can I apply for a Master's or PhD after finishing a Bachelor's or MD in Georgia?
Yes—eligible graduates can apply for postgraduate or doctoral programs, provided they meet admission criteria and keep their immigration status aligned.
