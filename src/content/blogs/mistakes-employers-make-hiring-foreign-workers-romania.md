# 7 Costly Mistakes Romanian Employers Make When Hiring Foreign Workers

Overseas hiring can transform your business, or drain your budget. Most problems come from the same avoidable mistakes.

---

## 1. Starting Too Late
Waiting until you are short-staffed forces rushed decisions. **Start early** and build in time for work authorization, visas, and travel. See the [recruitment timeline](/blog/nepal-to-romania-recruitment-timeline/).

## 2. Writing Vague Job Descriptions
"Need 10 workers" is not a requirement. Specify duties, skills, experience, salary, and conditions. Work authorization applications also depend on clear job details.

## 3. Using Multiple Unconnected Agents
Several middlemen means unclear responsibility, duplicate fees, and inconsistent quality. **Choose one accountable partner.**

## 4. Skipping Skill Verification
A CV is not proof of skill. Use interviews and trade tests, especially for chefs, technicians, drivers, machine operators, and tradespeople.

## 5. Underestimating Compliance
Romania has clear rules on wages, working time, housing, and contracts. Treating them as an afterthought leads to rejected applications and penalties.

## 6. Ignoring the Full Cost
Focusing only on the agency fee hides permit, medical, travel, housing, and replacement costs. Read the [cost breakdown](/blog/cost-of-hiring-nepali-workers-in-romania/).

## 7. No Onboarding or Support Plan
Workers who arrive without orientation are more likely to leave. Plan arrival, housing, residence permit steps, language basics, and a point of contact.
