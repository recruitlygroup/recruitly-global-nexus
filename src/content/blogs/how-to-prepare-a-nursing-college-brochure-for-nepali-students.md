# How to Prepare a Nursing College Brochure for Nepali Students

A good brochure turns a hesitant family into an applicant. A weak one sends them to a competitor. Use this checklist before you send your materials to any recruitment partner, including us.

---

## Essential Information

- ✓ **College name, city, and state**, with a clear location map
- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details** (nursing council, university or board)
- ✓ **Programs offered** (BSc Nursing, GNM, Diploma in Nursing) and duration
- ✓ **Seats and intake dates** for the next cycle
- ✓ **Eligibility and admission requirements** for Nepali applicants
- ✓ **Application deadlines**

---

## Fees and Costs

- ✓ **Tuition per year**
- ✓ **Hostel and mess fees**
- ✓ **Uniform, books, and clinical charges**
- ✓ **Exam and registration fees**
- ✓ **Refundable deposits and refund policy**
- ✓ **Payment schedule**

> **Tip:** One clear "total cost over the full program" table builds more trust than any slogan.

---

## Student Life and Safety

- ✓ **Hostel photos and room details**
- ✓ **Security and warden information**
- ✓ **Food options**, including what suits Nepali students
- ✓ **Medical care and emergency support**
- ✓ **Contact person for families**

---

## Training and Outcomes

- ✓ **Teaching hospitals and clinical placements**
- ✓ **Labs and facilities**
- ✓ **Faculty overview**
- ✓ **Exam results and pass rates**, if you can share them truthfully
- ✓ **What graduates go on to do**

---

## Commission and Partnership Details (For Recruitment Partners)

- ✓ **Your commission structure**
- ✓ **When commission is earned** and the payment timeline
- ✓ **Drop-out and refund terms**
- ✓ **Documents you require** from each student
- ✓ **Admissions contact** and expected response time

---

## Common Brochure Mistakes

- ❌ Marketing language with no fee details
- ❌ Missing recognition proof
- ❌ Old photos or outdated fees
- ❌ No Nepal-specific information
- ❌ No contact person
