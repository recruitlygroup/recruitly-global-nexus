# Hiring Nepali Construction, Factory, Logistics and Skilled Trade Workers for Romania

Project deadlines and production targets do not wait for a labor shortage to resolve. Whether you run a building site, a factory floor, or a warehouse operation, a reliable skilled workforce keeps operations on schedule.

- **Plumbers and pipe fitters**
- **Welders and fabricators**
- **Painters and plasterers**
- **Tile and flooring installers**
- **HVAC technicians**
- **Heavy equipment operators**
- **General laborers and helpers**

## Factory and Production Roles

- **Machine and CNC operators**
- **Assemblers and line workers**
- **Packers and packaging staff**
- **Quality control checkers**
- **Warehouse and logistics helpers**

## Logistics and Transport

- **Truck and delivery drivers** (subject to license recognition and qualification rules)
- **Forklift operators**
- **Loaders and warehouse staff**

---

## Why Employers Hire From Nepal

- **Overseas work experience** among many candidates
- **Scalable numbers** for large projects and orders
- **Flexible skill levels** from helpers to supervisors
- **Willingness to work shifts** and structured schedules

---

## What to Specify in Your Request

- ✓ Role and skill level (helper, skilled, supervisor)
- ✓ Number of workers and start date
- ✓ Contract length and shift pattern
- ✓ Salary, overtime, and accommodation terms
- ✓ Safety training, licenses, and equipment requirements

---

## Quality Controls That Protect Your Output

- **Trade tests** to confirm real skills, not just certificates
- **Experience verification** with past employers where possible
- **License and qualification checks** for drivers and operators
- **Medical fitness checks** before deployment
- **Pre-departure briefing** on workplace rules and safety
