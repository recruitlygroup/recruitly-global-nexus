## Master Readiness Checklist Before You Apply & Fly

Check off each step before committing tuition or booking travel:

* [ ] **Verify Program & Career/Licensing Fit:** Confirm degree duration, language of instruction, university authorization, and any home-country licensing/equivalence requirements (especially for medicine/healthcare).
* [ ] **Prepare Clean PDFs & File Names:** Keep clear, legible PDFs organized with consistent naming (such as `LastName_FirstName_TypeOfDocument.pdf`) and accurate personal details across every document.
* [ ] **Complete Interview, EQE & Enrollment Steps:** Attend the admissions interview, complete translation/notarization and **EQE recognition**, and follow enrollment and fee payment instructions carefully.
* [ ] **Map Out Full Tuition, Living & Insurance Costs:** Budget for tuition, application/recognition/permit fees, health and accident insurance, and living expenses (monthly independent budget or **$9,000 USD/year** all-inclusive package).
* [ ] **Handle Home-Country & Immigration Steps On Time:** Complete home-country steps (such as NOC/bank remittance where applicable), secure your **Category D3 Study Visa** prior to travel (where required), and file for your **Temporary Residence Permit** at the **Public Service Hall** before your legal stay expires.
* [ ] **Stay Compliant During & After Study:** Maintain active enrollment, follow current work/residence rules, and plan post-graduation next steps 3–6 months ahead of finishing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the best way to start applying to study in Georgia for 2026/27?
Start by shortlisting an authorized program and university that matches your budget and goals, gather your academic and passport documents in clean PDF format, complete the application and interview, and follow the EQE recognition, fee/enrollment, and D3 visa/residence permit steps.

### How much total budget should I plan for Georgia?
Combine your annual program tuition (and any application, recognition, insurance, and permit fees) with your living costs—either budgeting roughly **$300 to $500 USD/month** independently (adjusting for city, housing, and winter heating) or opting for a **$9,000 USD/year** all-inclusive living package covering **3 meals a day, accommodation with cleaning twice a week, Wi-Fi, and utility bills**.
