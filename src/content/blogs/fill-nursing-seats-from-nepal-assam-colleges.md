# How Assam Nursing Colleges Can Fill Seats With Students From Nepal

Assam is the gateway to India's Northeast and has a long-established Nepali-speaking community. For Nepali families, that **cultural familiarity** can make Assam feel less foreign than many other states.

---

## Why Nepali Families May Consider Assam

- **Nepali-speaking communities** already living in the state
- **Guwahati as a regional education and healthcare hub**
- **Good rail and air connectivity** from eastern Nepal via nearby cities
- **A range of nursing program options**

## What Assam Colleges Should Highlight

- ✓ **Recognition and affiliation details**, easy to verify
- ✓ **All-in fee sheet** including hostel and clinical costs
- ✓ **Clinical training exposure** in Guwahati or partner hospitals
- ✓ **A named contact** for Nepali families and a welcoming orientation

> Rules, affiliations, and fees vary by college and change over time. Confirm current requirements with the relevant council and your affiliating university.

## Challenges to Plan For

- **Travel time** from western and central Nepal
- **Competition** from other eastern and northeastern states
