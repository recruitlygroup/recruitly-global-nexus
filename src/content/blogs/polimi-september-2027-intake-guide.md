# Politecnico di Milano September 2027 Intake: Programs, Deadlines & How to Apply

**Politecnico di Milano (PoliMi)** is Italy's largest technical university and one of the most competitive destinations for international students in **engineering, architecture and design**. If you want to start in **September 2027 (academic year 2027/28)**, the first application window is already open, and the **early bird deadline is 1 December 2026**.

This guide covers the English-taught programs, all key dates, entry requirements, and how to check your chances before you apply.

> **Quick answer:** For Master's applicants, the Engineering 1st call closes **1 December 2026** and the 2nd call runs **12 January to 25 February 2027**. Bachelor's test dates for 2027/28 have not been published yet.

---

## Why Study at Politecnico di Milano?

PoliMi's reputation is one of the main reasons students choose it.

- **QS World University Rankings 2027:** ranked **#87 worldwide**, up 11 places, and **#1 in Italy**.
- **Subject strength (QS by Subject 2026):** **#6 in Architecture & Built Environment**, **#7 in Art & Design**, and **#14 in both Civil & Structural Engineering and Mechanical, Aeronautical & Manufacturing Engineering**.
- **Scale and diversity:** about **49,000 students**, of whom roughly **9,900 are international**.
- **Heritage:** founded in **1863**, with campuses including Milano Leonardo, Milano Bovisa, Lecco, Cremona, Piacenza and Mantova.

A degree from PoliMi is recognised by employers across Europe and beyond, and Milan is Italy's business and design capital.

> **Note:** This list reflects the 2026/27 offer. Confirm the final 2027/28 list on the official PoliMi site, as new English programs can be approved or changed.

### How Bachelor's admission works
Admission is based on an **entrance test and ranking list**, not just grades.

- **Engineering:** the **TOL** (Test OnLine), PoliMi's own test. The minimum score to appear in the ranking is **30/100**. Accepted alternatives include the **SAT, TOLC-I or CEnT-S**, within set validity windows.
- **Design:** the **TOLD**. For English-taught Interaction Design, the English TOLD is required.
- **Architecture:** the **ARCHED** test.
- **English proof:** required for English-taught programs (for example an accepted IELTS or TOEFL score, an English-language school qualification, or citizenship of an English-speaking country).
- **Italian:** not an entry requirement for English-taught programs, but you will need to reach level B2 before graduating.

### Bachelor's dates: what we know
PoliMi has **not yet published the 2027/28 test calendars**, and says more information will follow from **January 2027**. For reference, the 2026/27 cycle ran like this:

| Item | 2026/27 cycle (use as a pattern only) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard TOL registration | 1 Feb to 10 Mar 2026, then 11 Mar to 31 May 2026 |
| Early TOL (penultimate-year students) | 11 Mar to 16 Jul 2026 |
| English TOLD for Design | 14 Mar to 14 May 2026 |
| Engineering enrolment phases | 4 May to 7 Sep 2026 |

**Plan to prepare between now and January 2027** so you are ready when the 2027/28 windows open.

## September 2027 Master's Deadlines

These are the official dates for applicants with a **foreign qualification** applying for the **September 2027 intake (1st semester)**.

| Study area | Call | Application period | Outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engineering | 1st call (early bird) | **1 Oct to 1 Dec 2026** | By end of May 2027 |
| Engineering | 2nd call | **12 Jan to 25 Feb 2027** | By end of May 2027 |
| Architecture & Design | General call | **1 Oct 2026 to 28 Jan 2027** (early bird: 1 Dec 2026) | By end of May 2027 |
| EEA students and non-EEA residents in Italy only | Additional call | 1 to 18 Mar 2027 | By end of May 2027 |

All deadlines close at **11:59 pm CET**.

### Key points
- **Apply by 1 December 2026** if you can. PoliMi encourages early bird applications to improve your chances and receive results sooner.
- You can apply **once per semester**, and up to a limited number of programs per call.
- Engineering also accepts students in the **second semester (February)**, but Architecture and Design accept only in the first.
- Avoid last-minute payments, since bank issues can block your submission.

## Master's Entry Requirements

- **A recognised bachelor's degree** in a relevant field. Some third-party guides say Indian applicants typically need a 4-year degree and around **70/100** to be competitive. Check the program page for your exact country and degree.
- **English proficiency at B2**, for example **IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 78, or PTE Academic 59**. A bachelor's degree taught in English can replace the certificate.
- **Documents:** transcripts, degree certificate or provisional certificate, CV, English proof, and any program-specific items such as a portfolio for design and architecture.
- **Application fee:** around **EUR 150** for the Engineering call (verify on the official page before paying).

> **Scholarships:** PoliMi offers merit-based scholarships to talented international candidates admitted to a Master of Science program, and Bachelor's scholarship opportunities exist for programs in Cremona and Piacenza.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the early bird deadline for PoliMi September 2027?
**1 December 2026** at 11:59 pm CET for the Engineering 1st call and the Architecture & Design early bird.

### Can I apply after 1 December 2026?
Yes. The Engineering 2nd call runs **12 January to 25 February 2027**, and the Architecture & Design general call closes **28 January 2027**.

### When will I get my result?
PoliMi lists outcomes for all September 2027 Master's calls **by the end of May 2027**.

### Does PoliMi publish an acceptance rate?
No official acceptance rate exists for the Laurea programs, and PoliMi does not publish cut-off scores. Treat figures you see online with caution.

### Do I need to speak Italian?
Not to enter English-taught programs, but you will need Italian level B2 before completing a Bachelor's degree.
