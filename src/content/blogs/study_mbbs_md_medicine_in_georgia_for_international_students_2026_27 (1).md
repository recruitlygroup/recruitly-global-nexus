# Study MBBS / MD Medicine in Georgia for International Students (2026/27): Tuition, WDOMS & WFME Recognition, Clinical Training & Licensing Rules

**TL;DR:** Medical education in Georgia is offered as a **6-year (360 ECTS) Doctor of Medicine (MD)** degree—globally recognized as equivalent to **MBBS**—taught **100% in English** with no external language test required at most universities. For the **2026/27 academic year**, annual MD tuition typically ranges from **\$5,000 to $8,000 USD** depending on the university, while monthly living expenses average **\$300 to $500 USD** (or an all-inclusive campus living package of **\$9,000 USD/year** covering meals, accommodation, cleaning, Wi-Fi, and utilities). Applicants complete an admissions interview and **National Center for Educational Quality Enhancement (EQE)** document recognition before obtaining a **Category D3 Study Visa** and filing for a **Temporary Residence Permit** in Georgia.

## Why International Students Choose Georgia for Medicine (MD / MBBS)

Georgia has become a prominent European-region destination for medical aspirants from **Nepal, India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Kenya, and the GCC** seeking transparent fees and an English-medium curriculum:

* **100% English-Medium Instruction:** Lectures, examinations, and clinical teaching in international MD tracks are conducted in English, removing language barriers during early academic study.

* **International Directory & Accreditation Alignment:** Reputable Georgian medical universities are listed in the **World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS)** and aligned with **World Federation for Medical Education (WFME)** and **Bologna / ECTS** standards, supporting eligibility for international licensing pathways such as the **USMLE** and **UKMLA/PLAB**.

* **No Capitation or Hidden Donation Fees:** Tuition is billed transparently per semester/year with no donation fees.

* **Two Main Intakes Per Year:** Most universities offer **Autumn/Fall (September/October)** and **Spring (February/March)** intakes.

## Program Structure: How the 6-Year MD Degree Works

In Georgia, the primary medical qualification is the **Doctor of Medicine (MD)**—equivalent to an **MBBS** degree:

* **Duration & Credits:** Typically **6 years (72 months / 360 ECTS)** combining foundational biomedical sciences, pre-clinical subjects, hospital clerkships, and clinical training.

* **Eligibility:** Completion of higher secondary / Grade 12 (10+2 or A-Level) with **Physics, Chemistry, and Biology** (commonly a minimum **50% aggregate** in PCB, plus any home-country entrance qualification required if you plan to practice back home, such as **NEET** for Indian students or **MEC** rules for Nepali students).

* **Admissions Flow:** Apply online → attend the university admissions interview → complete document translation, notarization, and **Ministry of Education / EQE recognition** → receive admission/enrollment documents and pay tuition → apply for the **D3 Study Visa** → travel to Georgia and apply for your **Temporary Residence Permit** within the required timeframe.

## Living Costs & Budget Planning for Medical Students

Alongside tuition, budget carefully for accommodation, food, utilities, insurance, and immigration filings:

* **Independent Monthly Living:** Typically **\$300 to $500 USD/month** in Tbilisi or Batumi for shared student housing, food/groceries, transport, and utilities (higher if renting a private apartment or running heavy winter heating).

* **All-Inclusive Living Package Option:** A **\$9,000 USD/year** all-inclusive package covers **3 meals per day, accommodation with twice-weekly cleaning, Wi-Fi, and utility bills**.

* **Visa & Residence Permit:** Apply for a **Category D3 Study Visa** via `geoconsul.gov.ge` / the relevant Georgian diplomatic mission or visa center, and file for your **Temporary Residence Permit** with Georgia's Public Service Development Agency / House of Justice after arrival.

## Global Licensing & Home-Country Rules After Graduation

Graduating with an MD degree from Georgia does **not** automatically grant a license to practice medicine everywhere—each country sets its own examination, internship, and regulatory rules:

* **United States (USMLE):** Requires WDOMS listing with WFME-aligned accreditation and ECFMG pathway compliance, followed by USMLE exams and residency matching.

* **United Kingdom & Europe:** Requires meeting the specific medical council's examination, language, and registration criteria (such as UKMLA/PLAB in the UK).

* **India (NMC) & Nepal (MEC):** Students who plan to practice back in India or Nepal must verify current **NMC (including NEET and FMGL/licensing rules)** or **Nepal Medical Education Commission (MEC)** regulations on course duration, clinical training, and home-country licensing eligibility before enrolling.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is an MD from Georgia equivalent to MBBS?

Yes. In Georgia, the primary medical degree is the **Doctor of Medicine (MD)**—typically a 6-year program—and is treated as equivalent to an **MBBS** degree.

### Do I need IELTS or TOEFL to study medicine in Georgia?

Most Georgian universities do not require IELTS or TOEFL for English-taught MD programs; instead, they assess English readiness through a **university admissions interview** and your academic records.

### Which visa do international medical students need for Georgia?

International students generally apply for a **Category D3 Study Visa** prior to travel and then apply for a **Temporary Residence Permit** in Georgia for their studies.

### Can I practice in Nepal or India after doing MD in Georgia?

That depends on meeting your home country's current medical council rules (such as qualifying **NEET** for Indian students or following **MEC** eligibility rules for Nepali students) and passing any required licensing or screening exams—always verify the latest council rules before applying.
