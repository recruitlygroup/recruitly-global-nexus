# Study Medicine in Italy from Nepal: The Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Searching for **MBBS in Italy from Nepal**? In Italy the degree is called **Medicine and Surgery (MD)**, a **6-year single-cycle programme** taught in English at many public universities. It is internationally recognised and, with the right preparation and scholarship support, can be far more affordable than private medical colleges at home or in other countries.

This guide covers the whole process, from your first decision to your first lecture, and shows how **Recruitly Group** supports you from day 1.

---

## Is "Fully Funded" Medicine in Italy Real?

**Short answer: scholarship-supported, not automatically free.**

- Public university tuition in Italy is **income-based**, so fees can be low for students who document their family income.
- Regional **DSU scholarships** can cover **tuition, accommodation and meals** for eligible students.
- Scholarships depend on **income, merit, region and available funds**. They are **not guaranteed**.

> **Tip:** Apply for the DSU scholarship in your first year and prepare income documents early. Deadlines are strict.

## Step 1: Check Your Eligibility

To apply for Medicine in English in Italy, Nepali students generally need:

- A **completed 12-year school education** (+2 or equivalent) with science subjects
- A **strong English level** (the IMAT and the course are in English)
- **Legalised and translated academic documents**, as required by each university and the Italian visa process
- A valid passport

Requirements can change each year, so always confirm with the official [Universitaly](https://www.universitaly.it) portal and your chosen university.

---

## Step 2: Understand the IMAT (International Medical Admissions Test)

The **IMAT** is the national entrance exam for English-taught Medicine and Surgery at Italian public universities.

### IMAT at a glance

- Held **once a year, in September**
- Multiple-choice test covering **logical reasoning and general knowledge, biology, chemistry, and physics and maths**
- Your score decides your **national ranking** and which university seat you can get
- Registration is separate from **Universitaly pre-enrolment**

### How early should you prepare?

Most successful candidates start **6 to 9 months before the exam**. Prepare with past papers, timed practice and a weekly study plan.

> **Note:** The recent "semestre filtro" reform applies to **Italian-language** medicine courses. English-taught programmes still use the IMAT. Always check the latest official notice (bando) for the current year.

---

## Step 3: Build Your University Shortlist

You can list several universities when you register, and **the order of your preferences matters** for your ranking. Consider:

1. Your realistic IMAT score range
2. City cost of living and DSU scholarship conditions in that region
3. Available seats for non-EU students
4. Teaching hospitals, clinical exposure and language support

---

## Step 4: Universitaly Pre-Enrolment and Visa

International students must complete **pre-enrolment on Universitaly**, which is required for the visa and university access process.

- Pre-enrolment is **not** the same as IMAT registration
- Validation by the university does **not** automatically guarantee a visa
- Submit documents **early** and keep digital and certified copies

---

## Step 5: Arrival, Housing and DSU Application

After your visa:

- Arrange **housing** (DSU accommodation if you qualify, or private rental)
- Complete **enrolment** at the university
- Apply for the **DSU scholarship** and regional services
- Get your **Italian tax code (codice fiscale)** and residence permit (permesso di soggiorno)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Nepali students study medicine in Italy in English?

Yes. Several Italian public universities offer English-taught Medicine and Surgery, admitted through the IMAT.

### Is MBBS the same as Medicine in Italy?

The Italian degree is a 6-year **MD (Medicine and Surgery)**. Students often search "MBBS in Italy", but the official name differs. Check recognition rules for the country where you plan to practise.

### How much does it cost?

Public tuition is income-based and varies by university. Living costs depend on the city. DSU scholarships may reduce costs significantly for eligible students.

### When should I start?

Begin **9 to 12 months** before the IMAT so you have time for preparation, documents and Universitaly deadlines.

### What if I miss the IMAT?

The IMAT is held once a year. Missing it usually means waiting for the next cycle, which is why early planning matters.
